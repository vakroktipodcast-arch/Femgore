# Continuity review: ch18

**Verdict:** REVISE. No ledger contradictions in dates, durations, counts or hand-offs. There is one internal logic gap (the bloody prints on a phone that never left its foil), one ambiguity that reads against Ch 10, and one flat assertion that needs framing.

## Findings

1. **Prints on the phone glass.** Quote: "there I was on the glass: a thumb". S1 puts the phone "in its foil sleeve in my jacket pocket" for the whole night, and she reaches for it only at 8:50 a.m. After 2:50 a.m. she wore fresh nitrile over the blood, and the foil would have to be opened for her to leave bloody prints on the glass. Nothing in Ch 17 or Ch 18 has her handling the phone between the heart (right hand bare) and the nitrile. Rule broken: beat ch18 New facts ("phone was in its foil sleeve in her jacket pocket during Ch 17"); internal consistency. Fix: add one clause in S1's car-park beat, before the gloves go on, where she thumbs the phone awake for the time (Ch 17 has her read the time from her watch, so use that). Alternatively, make the prints a lifted ghost on the foil, not on the glass.

2. **"my desk" and the fork.** Quote: "eaten sitting down at my desk with the fork". In S2 she is sitting at her home writing desk, so "my desk" reads as the home desk with the brass-locked drawer. Ch 10 (beats/ch10, New facts) puts the lamb lunch at her office desk, with a fork kept in the office desk drawer. Rule broken: ledger Objects (Food: Ch 10 "cold lamb and salsa verde at the desk") read with beats/ch10. Fix: "at my desk at work, with the fork I keep in that drawer".

3. **Nora stated as a signatory in plain narration.** Quote: "and she had been one of them". Also "signed what I signed" and "Let the other ten be believed. The dead one too." Ledger People (Nora): she never signed an NDA and is not one of the eleven, and "Iris's belief ... is a mistaken inference". The beat says the page doesn't correct it, but the narrator is retrospective ("this book") and states the claim as fact in her own voice. That puts a false fact on the page. Fix: frame it as her inference without correcting it, e.g. "I did the sum, and the sum had her as one of them", and "It was the only reading I could make." Keep "the dead one too" in the plan as her belief.

4. **Heating.** Quote: "because I hadn't touched the thermostat". Ledger Places: heating off from Ch 18 to Ch 27. The sentence leaves open whether it was on before this morning. A flat that was heated on 13 Oct would not be cold by 8:50 a.m. unless the heating is on a timer or already off. Fix: "the heating was off and I left it off", or "I did not turn it on", so the Ch 18 start is explicit.

5. **Hair, before the hand-off to Ch 19.** Quote: "My hair had come down against my face". Nothing earlier says it was up. Not a contradiction, just a new fact. It is harmless if Ch 17 or the kit's hair cover covers it. Optional: leave it.
