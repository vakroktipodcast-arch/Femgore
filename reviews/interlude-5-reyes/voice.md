**Verdict:** REVISE

**Findings:**

1. "She would spend the rest of her career remembering that face." This is a stock thriller flash-forward and could close any novel. It also explains the scene's weight instead of leaving it on the page. The Cam anchor ends inside the moment, on what his body did, not on what it would mean later (anchors/interlude-2-cam.md, final paragraph; 01 point 3, "never to explain"). Fix: cut the flash-forward. End on the pledge image, or on a plain physical line in the present, e.g. "She did not stop." On its own, with no "that night", the reader carries the rest.

2. "Twenty years of that." and later "twenty years had taught her which way to run". The phrase appears twice in five paragraphs and copies the Cam anchor's closing move ("twenty years of habit"). It reads as a template, not as Reyes. "Which way to run" and "what she was running to" are generic chase lines. Fix: keep one "twenty years" at most. Replace the other with a concrete procedural action, e.g. what her thumb does on the holster strap. Cut "she did not yet know what she was running to" entirely.

3. "Reyes had no words for it. She noticed that she had none." The first sentence fits any novel. The second borrows Iris's Chapter 25 tic ("I noticed that."), which breaks the interlude rule of no Iris-isms (02 point 4, the self-observing evaluative stance; brief: "plainer, cooler"). "Something at the back of her head had started writing it down anyway" closely echoes ch25's "Something in me started anyway". Fix: cut both "no words" sentences. Keep "writing it down" only if the author wants the deliberate echo of Iris. Otherwise make it Reyes's own habit, e.g. "She was already putting it in the notebook in her head."

4. Paragraphs 1–2 and "A staffer upstairs had told her counsel was downstairs". Three paragraphs of logistics (the question marks, the voicemails, where counsel is) come before anything is sensed. This is drift toward explanation. The Cam anchor opens on the room and the coffee and lets the backstory arrive in one line. Fix: cut paragraph 2 to one sentence ("Two of the names had question marks; counsel had the answers and had not returned her calls."). Fold the staffer line into the lift-doors paragraph as a clause.

5. "Dates against the eight before VALE, the Sunday ones and this morning's ticked." Compression has gone too far here and the sentence does not parse on first reading. Fix: "Dates against the first eight. The Sunday ones ticked, and this morning's."

6. "The flat shoes were why she could." This explains, and the shoes come from nowhere: nothing set them up earlier. Fix: plant the flat shoes in paragraph 3 next to the lanyard and the sandwich, then let the run go unexplained, or cut the line.

7. Sentence length is uniformly short to medium. The Cam anchor earns one long, accumulating sentence at its point of pressure (the cooling coffee, the fries, the carpet "of everybody"). Fix: let the corridor pass (paragraph 33) run as one longer sentence, with the hands taken in order, so the observation has duration before the short line "They were the only steady hands in the corridor."

**What works:**
- "The smile had not caught up with the news." and "She had it in writing three times." These are dry and cool, and the irony is exactly at interlude strength.
- "They were the only steady hands in the corridor." This carries the hands motif in layman's terms from outside Iris. Keep it exactly as it is.
- "Like a woman saying the pledge." This is the unit's best image: plain, specific to Election Eve, and it implicates without comment.
