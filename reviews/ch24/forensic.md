- **Verdict:** REVISE

- **Findings:**

1. **The drug acts locally, which IM drugs don't.** "from the shoulder outwards... His left arm went first, the one it was in." An intramuscular dose is absorbed into the blood and acts on the whole body. It does not spread down the limb it went into, and a pathologist with toxicology access would know that. Rule: `ledger/continuity.md`, Objects, agent syringe: "Effect: heavy, weak, slowed limbs and speech". That is a whole-body effect. Fix: cut "from the shoulder outwards" and "the one it was in". Let the weakness come on everywhere at once over a minute or so, with the tied hand simply the first place she sees it.

2. **The draft contradicts the ledger on his voice.** "leaves the voice where it is, which was the point of it" breaks the same ledger line, "slowed limbs and speech; consciousness and tears intact". Fix: "slows the voice and leaves it working". This also makes the later broken, halting speech sound like both grief and the drug, which helps the scene.

3. **Nobody comes to the door, and the clock doesn't allow this much time.** "Nobody opened the door. I looked at it once". The ledger has the slot ending at 40 s and Ch 23 already ending about 70 s ("thirty seconds" over). Holt reaches the green room at 8:52, and Iris must be out of the vendor gate shortly before 9:00, with Ch 25 and 26 still to happen. Ch 24 currently reads as several minutes of weeping, remembering and applause. Rule: `ledger/continuity.md`, Fixed numbers (Ch 22–23 count) and Timeline, Mon 2 Nov. A detail standing outside by instruction, at a slot already overrun, with a speech waiting, would knock. As written, the silence is luck. Fix, two parts. (a) Let the warm-up's "practise their applause" tell her that the stage is holding, so nobody is calling for him yet. That needs one clause, since the beat is already in the draft. (b) Shorten Scene 2's run of remembered facts (plane, sophomore, Wellesley, Sunday at four) into one breath of speech, so the chapter fits in about two minutes.

4. **The injection site is too low.** "a hand's width below the point of the shoulder". A pathologist would use two or three fingers below the acromion. A hand's width puts the needle near the axillary nerve and the lower edge of the deltoid. Fix: "three fingers below the point of the shoulder".

5. **She can't tie a wrist to an upholstered arm that fast.** "round his wrist and round the arm of the chair... drawn tight". Most upholstered armchair arms are boxed down to the seat, so there is no gap to pass a tie through. Going round a padded arm and a wrist also needs a long tie and two hands, and here it is done in under two seconds while her hand is on his pulse. Rule: `ledger/continuity.md`, Places, Alder Suite: "one upholstered chair". Fix: one clause that she feeds the tie under the open arm, where it meets the front leg, which she had checked while he sat. Also say the tie is a long one, and let her free her pulse hand to draw it tight.

6. **Wrong muscle for the grief brow.** "Corrugator supercilii drew the brows together and up". Corrugator pulls the brows down and together. The inner-brow lift that makes the "grief" shape comes from the medial frontalis. An anatomist narrator can't get this wrong. Fix: "Corrugator drew the brows together and the inner frontalis lifted them".

7. **You don't take a pulse on a corpse.** "taken the pulses of fourteen hundred people who couldn't refuse". The 1,400 are her autopsies, and nobody palpates a pulse at autopsy, so a professional would wince. Fix: "I have lifted the wrists of fourteen hundred people who couldn't refuse."

8. **Tears on the face aren't at 37 degrees.** "a line of warmth at thirty-seven degrees". The surface of the eye is about 34–35 °C, and tears cool further on the cheek. She is a measurer and would not round that up. Fix: "a degree or two under body heat", or simply "warmer than I was". Keep the second "thirty-seven" as figurative, or match it.

9. **She sets down an uncapped needle without comment.** "I set the syringe down... uncapped". Fifteen years of sharps habit would make her recap. The cap is also never placed anywhere after "took the cap off with my thumb". Rule: `ledger/continuity.md`, Objects, agent syringe: "Capped and taken". If uncapped is meant to show her habits failing, say so in half a line, as the pin passage already does ("It was not a decision"). Otherwise she should recap by reflex, and the cap should stay in the scene so Ch 25 can take it.
