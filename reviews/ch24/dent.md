- **Verdict:** REVISE (light). The structure is sound and all three Turns and the Snapper land. The remaining faults are length, a diluted run between the S3 Turn and the Snapper, and one convenient door.

## Findings

1. **Length.** Lint count 3,344 against ~3,150 is +194 (about 6%). Rule broken: style/10 point 1 ("Chapter 17 may run long; nothing else may") and strategy/03 (scenes ~1,000–1,100). By my rough scene count S1 is ~1,150 against a beat-sheet split of ~1,000, and S2 (which the beat sheet says should get the most room) is no larger than S1. Most of the overage sits in S1. Fix: cut ~200 words, mainly from S1. Take the aftershave/carpet/dog-breath run in "And there it was, coming up through everything else" down to one smell-first sentence, and trim the first paragraph's kitchen-and-ballroom simile stack. Keep the lid-on-a-pan line.

2. **Convenient door.** "the main door with the latch he had thrown himself" is the only thing keeping the detail out while Iris loses her count and the slot overruns. She did not lock it, and Holt's latch is a door that helps her. Rule broken: style/10 point 5 and strategy/03 hero rule ("no unlocked doors she didn't unlock"; the same spirit applies to a locked door that works for her). It is established in 22 (beats/ch22 line 124), not invented here, but 24 leans on it at its most exposed moment. Fix: make it her competence in one clause. For example, she had counted on his latch and had checked it with her eyes when she came round the table, or the silence at the door is noted as the thing she was relying on. Also state that the tie and his held-out wrist worked because 23 had made him trust her, not because he is simply obliging.

3. **Dilution between the S3 Turn and the Snapper.** "Somewhere there was another page, with my name typed on it" sits between the Turn and the hand. It re-states the fourth page already given earlier in S3 ("There was one other page I had written"). It also holds the only felt Reyes/paper pressure in S3, and it arrives as a trailing aside. Rule broken: style/10 point 7 (each scene ends on its Turn) and the pressure rule, point 4. Fix: delete the repeat and move the "that one would be read" pressure up into the paragraph where the first mention of the other page sits ("one other page I had written since the syllabus"). That keeps S3's pressure and lets the Turn run straight into his hand.

4. **S2 closes on a line after its Turn.** "You knew her," he said. The outline Turn ends on "grieving the same woman", and the draft adds a button that is clean but lets the scene end on his line, not her admission. Rule: style/10 point 3, the Turn lines being the minimum. The beat sheet allows it, so this is a low-priority note. Fix, only if cutting for length: drop the line, because S3's "I abandoned the protocol" already answers it. If kept, it must stay as the very last line and no more may be added.

5. **Pressure thin in S2 and S3.** Reyes is named once, in S1 ("E. Reyes"). S2 carries it only as an abstraction in "They would know from a list of eleven names". S3 carries it only in the aside from finding 3. The knowledge from 18 (Nora, the page at home) is present in all three scenes. Rule: style/10 point 4, the pressure felt in every scene. Fix: in S2, hang one concrete paper object on the list line, for example a federal file that opens on the eleven in order. In S3, the move in finding 3 suffices.

6. **First sentence is functional, not weighted.** "I took the second pair of gloves out of my blazer" starts the method but carries no stake, and the paragraph it opens runs ~190 words. Last sentence (the Snapper) is strong. Rule: style/10 point 8. Fix: either lead with the black pair going over the cold fingers ("My fingers were cold inside the first pair" is the better first line) or split the first paragraph after "waiting to be told what it was for", so the sentence-level weight falls where the beat sheet wants it.
