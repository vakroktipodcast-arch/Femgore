# ch24 — Dent structure review

- **Verdict:** REVISE. The structure and all three Turns are sound; the chapter is about 17% over length, and a few smaller points need tightening.

## Turn check

- **S1:** lands at "He had asked what I wanted. Not what I was going to do". It is an event (the question, her thumb stopping), and the outline line is verbatim.
- **S2:** lands at "I understood then that he would never be taught." It is preceded by the event "You knew her," and follows the tears. It is an admission, but see finding 3.
- **S3:** lands at "I put the page on the table, beside the Marine." The Snapper follows: "His hand on mine, warm, over the glove". It is the last paragraph, with no knock and no cliffhanger.
- **First sentence:** "I took the second pair of gloves out of my jacket". It is the method begun, as the beat sheet asks, but it is procedural rather than weighted.
- **Last sentence:** "The first time in this book I had been touched by someone who didn't know who I was, and it was him." It carries weight.
- **Scene 1 trouble:** Iris is in it from the first line.

## Findings

1. **Over length.** By my estimate the chapter runs about 3,600–3,700 words against a 3,150 target. S1 is about 1,150, S2 about 1,450 and S3 about 1,100. This breaks style/10 point 1 ("nothing else may" run long) and strategy/03 (scenes of about 1,000–1,100). The beat sheet gives S2 1,150 words. The excess in S2 is the long weeping anatomy paragraph ("Corrugator supercilii drew the brows together"), the applause paragraph ("practise their applause"), and the two-paragraph temperature passage ("a line of warmth at thirty-seven degrees"). Fix: cut S2 to about 1,200 by halving the weeping paragraph, merging the Wellesley and Sunday facts into the pieces paragraph, and trimming the temperature passage to three sentences. In S3, cut the 230-word Holt paragraph ("He was watching me through the wet") to about 120.

2. **S3 lacks the act pressure.** In S1 the pressure is felt through "E. Reyes" and the stock log, and in S2 through "a list of eleven names". In S3 Reyes and the paper are absent, and the beat sheet's "still no knock" motif was dropped. The only pressure left is the knowledge from 18, via the drawer page. This breaks style/10 point 4. Fix: add one line, in the register of "Nobody opened the door", noticing the door's silence once and the minute gone. Keep it at one beat so that ch25 can still own the knock.

3. **S2 Turn is told rather than staged.** "I understood then… The syllabus shattered from the inside" is realisation plus metaphor. Her crying is reported twice, in "Something was running down my face" and again in the Turn. Style/10 point 3 and strategy/03 want an event or admission. Fix: keep the outline sentences verbatim, but move "You knew her" to directly after "I was crying too" (or end on it). That way the last beat is Holt's act of misreading, and the reader sees the misreading rather than being told it.

4. **Appetite exit is missing.** The beat sheet requires S1's Turn to thin the taste ("the taste goes thin in her mouth"), so that she enters ch25 with nothing in the mouth. The draft has her thumb stop and nothing else, and S2 has no mouth beat. The first mention of the mouth afterwards is S3's "Nothing came into my mouth," which reads as a first touch rather than a loss. This breaks the act's "Appetite: starved" pressure in S1–S2. Fix: add one clause after "my thumb off it" that the water in her mouth went back to ordinary saliva, or similar.

5. **Stated duration after S1.** "heavier than it had a minute ago" is a stated duration. The beat sheet allows none beyond the S1 count, and ch25 needs the overrun kept ambiguous. Fix: "than it had been" or "than before".

6. **Convenient conditions, minor.** "the latch he had thrown himself" depends on ch23 having established that Holt locked the door. If it did not, then Iris is relying on a door she did not lock, which breaks style/10 point 5. Check ch23. If it is not established, change to "the door I had locked behind me". Holt letting her take his wrist ("he let me") is acceptable, since it is character and the chapter says so. Do not add more compliance.

7. **First sentence.** The opening sentence is not load-bearing beyond procedure. Fix, if wanted: lead with the snap, for example "The second pair of gloves was black, and I snapped it on over the pale pair, left and then right." This moves the cold-fingers lid image to a following sentence. It is optional and subordinate to the cuts in finding 1.

## What works

- The S1 smell sentence and "and I am reporting it" are the voice returning, then withdrawn in S2. Do not trim the sentence's length.
- "We had both done the reading," and the line "It had chosen which ones," surfaced as a finding rather than a thesis. Keep both as written.
- The S3 sequence of syringe set down, page opened along its creases, pin returned by her hand "not a decision", then "You know what it is before I do." The reader stays ahead of her, as the 23 Turn requires.
