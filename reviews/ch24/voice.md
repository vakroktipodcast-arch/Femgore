# ch24 — Voice

- **Verdict:** REVISE

- **Findings:**

1. "the machine I had spent two years writing pages about had never failed" (Scene 2, ¶ beginning "I looked for myself in it"). This states the book's thesis outright, which is drift toward explanation (02 voice register: she is reliable about everything except why; 01: never explain). Ch25's anchor never says what anything means. Fix: end the paragraph on "It had seen Diane down to the stammer." and cut "It had chosen which ones" along with the clause before it. The reader will do that sum.

2. "I am qualified to tell you what it was." / "qualified to sign for it" (the weeping paragraph). "Qualified" appears twice in one paragraph, and the close, "This was not. It was grief, and it was real", is a three-step verdict that could sit in any novel. Signing for it is also certifying, which is close to the evaluative voice that 02 says is gone in 22–25. Fix: cut "I am qualified to tell you what it was" and start the paragraph at "The face went in". End it on "I know what is performed and what is not. This was not." and drop "It was grief, and it was real, and I was the one person…"

3. "Whatever happened next was not curriculum." / "You know what it is before I do." (Scene 3). Two one-line suspense paragraphs back to back. "Whatever happened next" is stock cliffhanger phrasing (task brief: any-other-novel sentence). Fix: cut "Whatever happened next was not curriculum." and keep "You know what it is before I do." on its own. That line is the implicating address that 01 allows.

4. "I had not known there was anything left in me that warm" (tears paragraph). This repeats "It surprised me that I still ran that warm." "Anything left in me" also comes close to naming emptiness (11: show the absence through the body and never name it). Fix: cut the last sentence and end on "I had been cold since the bathroom floor."

5. "and that had always been part of the lesson: the eleven names" (¶ beginning "The hard case"). This is the third statement of the plan, after "Lesson Three, as written, was simple…" and before the restatement in "I abandoned the protocol". Ch25 restates it again, so ch24 is explaining ahead of the anchor (02, drift to explanation). Fix: end that paragraph at "Counting is what offices are for." That line is one of the few jokes left in the chapter, so keep it.

6. "the way" occurs about 18 times on 14 lines. The tic is denser than in either anchor and it flattens the similes (06, texture). Fix: cut at least five. Start with "the way he had done everything since the syringe", "the way I had watched him work a room on a feed", "the way faces go", and "the way a glove drags on dry skin", which lands one sentence after "the way skin keeps a line".

7. "The zip-tie came out of the lining of the blazer flat, a long one" (¶ beginning "I came round"). This is the chapter's one quick physical action, and it runs as a single 70-word sentence (02: short declaratives for action). Fix: break it at "under the arm of the chair" and at "and I let go of his wrist", so that "Tight." or "I drew it through its own head." stands alone before "before his face had caught up with his arm."

8. "the reflex going for the pen… and it found no hand to hold the pen with." The reflex reaches for a pen like a hand and then finds no hand, so the image contradicts itself (drift to purple). Fix: "Something in me began, out of fifteen years' habit, to write it up, and stopped at the first line."

9. "I understood then that he would never be taught." This stock lead-in is generic and pads the Turn. Fix: cut it and open the paragraph on "There was no page that reached him." The author's minimum, "The syllabus shattered from the inside", stays as written.

- **What works:**
  - The long fear sentence ("And there it was… and I am reporting it") is fully in the ch17 register: Latinate anatomy against an Anglo-Saxon mouth, and appetite reported without apology. Keep it whole.
  - Holt's em-dash break is the book's one permitted use (07) and it is executed correctly. Iris says nothing aloud in the whole chapter. No grading marks appear, which honours 02.
  - The jokes thin as the chapter goes on ("hold out a wrist for a watch", "Counting is what offices are for", "We had both done the reading") and Scene 3 has none. The cool fragments in the closing paragraph set up ch25's register. Don't add jokes back.
