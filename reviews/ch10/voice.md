# Voice review: Chapter 10

**Verdict:** REVISE

**Findings:**

1. **"I had never struck through a name before. The line was the crack."** This is the outline's Turn pasted in as narration. Iris names the theme for the reader, which is explanation, and she should be unreliable about *why* (02, final point; 01, first point: "never to explain"). Fix: cut "The line was the crack." End on the physical stroke, or on one exact finding about the line itself (the drag of the nib at the softened card, the ink sitting a fraction above the fibre). Let the reader name the crack.

2. **"The bait only works if nobody cuts the line."** The mechanism of the bait is explained twice: first in Scene 1 ("The item had two jobs... the looking was what he was counting"), then again here. The figures also collide: a line, a drowning swimmer, a worm, a ring on the water, and then "the rise to his fly". Worm and fly are two different kinds of fishing, and Iris is exact (02, first line: "exact"). The repetition drifts toward explanation (01). Fix: in Scene 1, keep "A shortened link is a door with somebody standing behind it, counting" and cut the next three sentences. In Scene 2, keep one fishing figure all the way through. Fly suits "I am not a trout", so swap the worm for the fly ("Nobody fishing watches the fly"). Cut "If a woman is drowning... the one he wants" down to a single clause.

3. **"That is not care... A pathologist cannot unsee a finding."** This is a run of self-diagnosis that tells the reader what to conclude. "Unsee" is internet idiom and the sentence could sit in any procedural. "Nobody ever asks me for anything, and I know everything anyway" tips into self-pity, a register the anchors never use. Breaks 02 (dry, exact) and the general instruction to flag sentences that could appear in any other novel. Fix: keep "I knew the dog's name. I hadn't asked for it." and cut the next four sentences. The list of findings already does the work, because "the way findings are" is in the paragraph's first line.

4. **"the heat had settled into the rooms like a guest who won't take the hint"** A stock simile that could appear in any other novel. Iris's comparisons come from the body, the kitchen or the morgue ("the way infection moves through a ward, along the hands"). Fix: use something from her own trade or table, e.g. heat held in the rooms "the way a body holds it in the core after the skin has gone cool", or simply cut the simile.

5. **"a breath taken before each sentence the way people breathe before they jump"** Generic thriller shorthand (see item 4). Fix: describe it as a finding, e.g. "a breath taken before each sentence, audible, the intercostals doing work the words didn't need."

6. **"Being hunted by somebody good was the most interesting thing that had happened..."** This is genre-thriller phrasing ("being hunted"). The paragraph has already shown the appetite through the body (heat in the face and forearms, the quickening under the sternum), so this line names what the anatomy just showed. Fix: cut it and let "He had read me, and now he had written back" carry the beat. That line is hers.

7. **Direct address is too dense and often explains** (01, first point: sparingly, and only to implicate). Scenes 2 and 3 contain "I'll give you the list as I'd give it in a report", "since I owe you it", "I can tell you what I knew", "I am going to be honest with you about that much" and "I am telling you that I am not". Only "Watch me not." and "since I owe you it" actually implicate the reader. Fix: cut "I'll give you the list as I'd give it in a report" (start at "Inside forty-eight hours:"), change "I can tell you what I knew" to "But I knew things", and cut "I am going to be honest with you about that much", which also reads as a soft apology next to the banned "I'm not proud of" register (11).

8. **"My hands were steadier than they had been all week."** Chapter 1 sets up the steady hands as a spent-later signal ("so you'll know what it means later if they do"). Saying in passing that they had been less steady all week spends that signal off-page and without a finding. This is voice drift away from the anchor's precision about her hands. Fix: "My hands were steady." Or, if they really have not been steady, show that earlier in the chapter as an observed finding.

9. **Scene 3 falls below the joke floor** (02, third point: one per page is a floor in Act II). From "By ten that night" to the end, roughly three pages, the only dry line is "history that isn't available". Thinning is reserved for Act III, and the reader should not feel the loss yet. Fix: give Scene 3 at least two of her reflexive jokes. One could go in the detachment paragraph (the history that "has never once tried to win me over" is close; sharpen it). The car-alarm paragraph is currently atmospheric filler that could appear in any novel, so it could become a joke or be cut.

**What works:**

- The Latinate/plain collision at full strength: "flexor pollicis longus holding the distal phalanx bent over nothing", "frontalis lifts the brows", and the cold-lamb paragraph's "salt and rot". Do not touch the lunch paragraph.
- The grading voice where she is in control: "I gave it an A. I don't give A's to fiction." "Correct. Full marks." Mara is not graded anywhere in the chapter, which is correct per 07.
- Jokes that are fully hers: "I am a woman who eats the crust first", "never held a scalpel or a spaniel", "the most accurate thing ever printed about me", "Watch me not." The clipped dialogue with Mara ("Not in the corridor.") follows 07.
