# Genre review — Chapter 10

- **Verdict:** REVISE (light). The chapter does what the genre needs. One choice spends an Act III device too early, and two small body details work against it.

## Findings

1. **Appetite absence borrowed from Act III (moderate).** "I had eaten nothing since lunch, and I wasn't hungry. I am reporting it." Also "I didn't eat them". This breaks `strategy/10` (Act II is appetite as identity, Act III is appetite starved) and `style/05` point 1 (the meal's *absence* belongs to 25/26). If she loses her appetite over Tessa in Chapter 10, the body is passing a moral judgement she refuses to make in words. It also weakens the effect of 25–27, where not being able to eat has to land as new. It contradicts line 103 as well ("full still from lunch"). Fix: she eats the heel and the salsa verde standing up while she thinks about the warehouse ("I eat standing up when I plan", line 19, is already set up). Leave the crack where the outline puts it: in the struck-through name and the memorised timestamp. If you want a trace, give it a different sensation: the bread tasted of nothing in particular, and she ate it anyway. Cut "I wasn't hungry. I am reporting it."

2. **Hands give away nerves the chapter hasn't earned (minor).** "My hands were steadier than they had been all week." This implies her hands were unsteady all week. That clashes with "It was steady" at the voicemail (line 73) and puts the crack into her body too soon (`style/05` point 6: hands are the watched instrument, and the steady needle comes in 12). Fix: "My hands were as steady as they are over a body." That keeps her competent and makes the line through the name feel colder.

3. **Morgue pleasure stays backdrop (minor).** "A subdural haematoma, a crescent of clot the colour of blackcurrant jam". The food image is right. But in Act II she has stopped pretending (`strategy/10`, `strategy/04`: "she loves this. The morgue"), and here the work gets no owned pleasure at all, while the War Room gets "I was enjoying it." Fix: add one short clause where she enjoys lifting the clot out whole, reported like a finding. Keep it to one sentence. The scene doesn't need more.

## What works

- Line 27: "I will tell you plainly what I felt. I was enjoying it." The pleasure is placed in the body (heat in the face and forearms, a quickening under the sternum) and reported like a finding, with no apology. This is the right Act II register for being hunted by Brandt, and it must stay.
- Deleting the voicemail and then eating the cold lamb (lines 73–75): "salt and rot", "my jaw ached at the hinge", eaten sitting down. This is the rhyme with the meal after a kill, and it passes The Test. The reader cannot look away and cannot dismiss her. Don't soften it or add any reflection.
- The humour does the work in a chapter with no violence: "never held a scalpel or a spaniel", "I am not a trout", "the most accurate thing ever printed about me", "Watch me not." Keep every one.
