# Dent review: Chapter 10

- **Verdict:** REVISE. The structure and all three Turns are sound, but the chapter runs well over its length target and the openings of S1 and S3 are slow.

## Findings

1. **Length overrun (S1 and S2 most).** My hand count is roughly 1,300 words for S1, 1,400 for S2 and 1,100 for S3, about 3,800 in all against ~3,150. It was not counted by script, so run `scripts/lint.py` to confirm. Rule: `bible/style/10-chapter-architecture-the-dent-checklist.md` point 1 ("Chapter 17 may run long; nothing else may") and `bible/strategy/03-*.md` ("roughly 1,000-1,100" per scene). Fix: cut S1 to about 1,050 by trimming the locker-room-to-evening run. Fold the ladder-case paragraph ("A subdural haematoma, a crescent of clot...") to two sentences. Cut "The misspellings were placed like seasoning" down to one clause. Trim the voicemail paragraph's paraphrase. In S2, shorten the livestream/spaniel catalogue and the braise paragraph by about a third. Keep the jokes.

2. **S1 opening is a smell inventory, not weight.** The first sentence, "The locker room at twenty to seven smelled of floor disinfectant...", is about 70 words of setting with no hook. The trouble does not arrive until "That Monday there was a new item" (roughly word 140, still inside page one, so the rule is met). Rule: checklist point 8 ("first and last sentences are load-bearing"). Fix: keep smell first but cut the sentence to about 25 words ("The locker room smelled of floor disinfectant and fresh-laundry steam, and I was half into my scrubs when I opened *The Cloakroom*."). That brings the item in sooner.

3. **S3 delays its action.** About 600 words of exposition come before the drawer opens: the machinery, the detachment doctrine, the tab, the inventory and the direct address. The scene's reversal is then a single stroke. Rule: `bible/strategy/03-*.md` ("Scene 3 reverses"). Fix: compress the detachment doctrine to three sentences and merge the tab with the inventory. Cut the explanatory line "That is not care. That is fifteen years of being trained...", because it half-apologises for the knowledge, which the beat sheet's banned list warns against. Get to the drawer by about word 400.

4. **S2 Turn is slightly pre-spent.** The press of 7 and the archive line already narrate the deletion. The paragraph's closing "I listened to it once. I deleted it. I ate lunch." then restates it before the food. It lands, but as a recap ahead of the taste. Rule: checklist point 3 and CLAUDE.md rule 4 (the Turn as an event, not a summary). Fix: move "I listened to it once. I deleted it." up to follow the 7 and the "I did not write the number down" beat, or cut the archive-folder generalisation ("Everybody in county service knows...") to one line. Then let "I ate lunch." open the final paragraph alone.

5. **Pressure and hero rule: no failure, one soft spot.** Brandt is felt in every scene: the sentence-craft recognition in S1, the feed and "Don't share it" in S2, and the dossier used as ruler and the quarter-inch in S3. Nothing is luck or rescue, and the cached copy, the reverse-image hit and the voicemail are not convenient doors. Weak spot: S2's second half (Mara, then voicemail) has no Brandt in it, since his last appearance is the Tuesday feed. Fix, one line only: after the voicemail, give a beat that ties back to the water, for example a glance at the handset's light as a ring on his water. Optional.

## Where each Turn lands

- **S1:** "The profile had a real face. It was a woman who looked like me..." Present, correct, last paragraph, an event.
- **S2:** "I listened to it once. I deleted it. I ate lunch." Present, with the taste paragraph carrying it. See finding 4.
- **S3:** "I drew his dossier to the edge of the page..." and "The line was the crack." Present. The scene's aftermath then runs into the Snapper.
- **Snapper:** "Wednesday 30 September. 11:38 a.m. Forty-seven seconds. I had deleted the message and kept the time." An admission, not a cliffhanger. The last sentence echoes the S2 Turn and carries weight.

## What works

- The Snapper's last clause ("kept the time") and its echo of S2's "I deleted it".
- The S2 voicemail and taste placement. The cold lamb eaten sitting down is the appetite shown with no commentary, and the heat is kept throughout.
- The S3 struck name as a physical act ruled against the dossier edge, with steady hands and the quarter-inch restored.
