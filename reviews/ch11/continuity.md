# Continuity review: Chapter 11 (The Counter-Surveillance)

- **Verdict:** REVISE. No ledger contradiction in dates, times, counts, codes or hand-offs. One clash with Ch 2 and a few small slips need one-clause fixes.

## Findings

1. **Diane's hand and "a copy in my drawer" (moderate).** Prose: "I had watched that hand for forty minutes across a desk three years ago, and I had a copy of it in my drawer."
   - Breaks `manuscript/ch02.md` (NDA scene): Iris sat and signed while Diane "watched my hand the whole time". Diane was the watcher, not the watched.
   - Also unledgered: the drawer holds Iris's own signed NDA (ledger, Places/Objects, drawer order) and a typed cover-email printout (Ch 3). No Diane signature is established there. Ch 5's "countersigned me into the cabinet" hints at one but is not ledgered.
   - Fix: "I had sat across a desk from that hand for forty minutes three years ago, and it had countersigned the agreement in my drawer." Add Diane's countersignature to the ledger (Objects, drawer). Or drop the drawer clause.

2. **"Skin to skin since the bun" (minor, literal error).** Prose: "the first thing I had touched skin to skin since the bun."
   - Wrong on the page. She handles the tablet bare-handed at 9:05 and lays out the kit before the gloves go on. She also drives and works all day.
   - The beat sheet's wording has the same flaw.
   - Fix: "the first thing I had held bare-handed since I pulled the gloves on", or "the first thing in an hour". The seed for the Ch 13 and Ch 17 bare hand survives.

3. **"Officer of record of four companies" (minor).** Prose: "Diane Pell was the officer of record of four companies."
   - The page shows her only on Fenwick Lane's annual report. Iris reads no officer for the other three.
   - The ledger and beat New facts say the register lists her on all four. Either Iris says she checked all four ("I checked the other three") or the line becomes "of the company at the top of the chain".
   - "The register" is used for both the city permit register and the corporate register. Name the second ("the state's register of companies") so "The register had not been told" lands.

4. **Hand-off with Ch 10 on the date of the paperwork (minor).** Prose: "After that it was paperwork... The city's register of permits named the occupier."
   - This reads as if she had the occupier's name before 1 Oct, between the 24th and the 30th.
   - `manuscript/ch10.md` (Wed 30 Sep night): "I knew where it stood and nothing about what was in it", and she does not mention a daytime watch or any permit work.
   - The ch10 beat sheet says Ch 10 gives "no facts" and Ch 11 S1 "finds it".
   - Fix: date the permit lookup. For example, "That morning, from the car, the city's register of permits named the occupier." Then Ch 10 stays true. The 24 Sep watch itself (ledger Timeline) is fine.

5. **Her phone and her day (minor).** Prose: "my own phone was at home in its foil sleeve" (7:40 a.m.), and in S2 "My phone was still at home in its sleeve."
   - "Still" means no pass through the flat between the unit, the ME office (about 10 to 5) and the unit again by 5:50.
   - She therefore works a full day phoneless. The ledger has her reading *The Cloakroom* every morning (People, Iris), and on 1 Oct nothing shows when.
   - Fix: one clause. Either she went home at lunch to re-sleeve it, or she carries no phone to work that day by choice, or "still" goes.
   - The ledger notes that the Ch 4 foil-sleeve habit is "during surveillance". A day at work is a new case.

6. **Food all day (minor, new fact).** Prose: "a woman who had eaten a pork bun at twenty to eight that morning and been working since."
   - It implies no lunch and no supper, about fifteen hours, from a woman whose meals the ledger tracks.
   - It does not contradict the ledger. It is not in the beat's New facts, so log it.
   - Ch 12's appetite beat reads fine against it.

7. **Foil and the office sensor (minor, internal).** The office's PIR is crossed under foil four times (about four minutes each). The lock-picking, the file grading and the vinyl cutting all happen inside the room with no mention of the foil.
   - If the PIR covers the room, say the blanket stays up ("tented over my shoulders"). If it covers only the doorway, say so.
   - The 41 minutes (9:40 to 10:21) can hold the crossings. Do not add time.
