- **Verdict:** REVISE. The narrator is right and the joke rate holds for Act II, but a handful of explanatory and stock-thriller sentences need fixing.

- **Findings:**

1. **(Moderate) Scene 3, para 2: "whose autopsy I had signed as reviewer, in which he kept whatever he kept."** The relative clause attaches to the wrong noun, so it reads as if he kept things in the autopsy. The whole paragraph ("He would sweep the place...", "He could not tell Holt without explaining...") also lays out the plan for the reader, which is the drift toward explanation (01 point 1; 02: exact, not expository). Fix: put the building back as the subject ("a building he kept whatever he kept in, owned three companies up by a woman eighteen days dead whose autopsy I had signed as reviewer"). Cut the sweep sentence. Keep "The three walls were still standing, and I was standing behind them."

2. **(Moderate) Scene 3, para 2: "It has no radio. It keeps everything that passes through it."** This is a spec sheet. It could sit in any tech thriller, and it is the only stretch of the chapter where Iris sounds like a manual and not like herself (02 voice register; brief's check for generic thriller prose). Fix: cut it down to one sentence that ends on her simile: "It transmits nothing; it sends a heartbeat out inside the building's own traffic, like a pulse taken through somebody else's wrist." Keep "read its manual the way I read a toxicology report".

3. **(Moderate) Scene 2, para 7: "Breaking and entering is mostly entering. The breaking is for amateurs."** This is a stock heist quip that any burglar narrator could say. "X is for amateurs" is also Brandt's line in Ch 17 ("Lying is for amateurs and candidates"). It should stay his, and here it takes the edge off his line (02 humour: jokes are about bodies, accurate; brief's any-novel test). Fix: keep the first sentence. Replace the second with a joke from her own trade, e.g. "Like a post-mortem, most of it is getting in neatly."

4. **(Minor) Scene 2, para 10: "A sensor like that does not see you. It sees change."** The generic "you" is being used to explain, not to implicate (01 point 1). Fix: give it to her as a finding: "A sensor like that does not see a body. It sees change: something warm crossing something cooler."

5. **(Minor) Scene 2, para 8: "The hall hit me in the face."** This is a generic action-thriller opener. It also skips her order of the senses, when the rest of the sentence is a good smell inventory. Fix: "Heat came out of the hall to meet me, the way it comes out of an oven you have opened to baste something." That brings in the plain food register against the Latinate (02 vocabulary collision).

6. **(Minor) Scene 1, para 5: "thought the body was the only one who had."** The punchline doesn't parse, so the joke after the meninges image fails. Fix: "the work of a man who had read the anatomy and assumed nobody else had."

7. **(Minor) Scene 1, para 2: "kept from spoiling the way you keep soup from boiling."** This mixes two images (spoiling and boiling over), and the long greedy sentence is spent on a building, not a body (02 rhythm). It leans purple. Fix: drop "from spoiling", so it reads "kept cool the way you cool soup, by blowing on it, for ever."

8. **(Minor) Scene 3, para 1: "I was a little proud of that, I admit."** "I admit" is a hedge. Iris reports her pleasures and doesn't apologise for them (11, the "I'm not proud of" item, and 02 point 5). Fix: cut "I admit."

- **What works:**
  - The meninges image turning into "She was the pia. She was the one that sticks." It joins the Latinate and the plot in one move. Keep it exactly as written.
  - Crossing the room under the foil "at the speed of the slowest thing I know, which is a body cooling." It's the voice at its best: anatomical, funny, and something only she would think. The plantar-flexion hold before it has the right long-sentence rhythm for the body.
  - The file-grading paragraph ("assessed as a fire risk. I had been assessed correctly."), followed by "I am telling you that I saw it... not going to think about it." Here the grading voice and the direct address are both doing their jobs: control, then implication.
