# Dent review: ch11, The Counter-Surveillance

- **Verdict:** REVISE (light). The structure is sound and all three Turns and the Snapper land. The problems are length, a buried Turn beat in Scene 3, and one self-contradiction.

**Turn map**
- Scene 1: lands at "Officer of record: Diane Pell." It is an event, followed by the laugh. PASS.
- Scene 2: lands at "Mine. A printed file, three years old..." PASS. It is a clean cut on the discovery.
- Scene 3: the proper Turn lands at "I didn't recognise her either." PASS. The first half of the outline Turn, taking the photo and leaving the rest, is weak (finding 2).
- Snapper: "In the bottom drawer of a dead woman's cabinet, under a paperclip that was holding nothing, the space where my face had been." It is an image, not a cliffhanger, and "empty" is not used of her.

**Findings**

1. **Length.** My estimate by paragraph is about 1,200 / 1,100 / 1,150, roughly 3,450 words against a ~3,150 target, about 9% over (not measured by tool; confirm with `scripts/lint.py` or `wc -w`). Rule: style/10 point 1, "nothing else may" run long. Fix: trim about 250 words. Candidates are the generator and service-card detail in Scene 1, the second sentence of "I had been doing to him what he did to me..." paragraph, and some of the Scene 3 grading list. Keep the jokes and the dossier items that carry the Turn.

2. **Scene 3, outline Turn part 1 is buried.** The line "I unclipped the photograph and slid it into a glassine envelope..." sits inside a logistics paragraph ending "Forty minutes and change." Rule: style/10 point 3, the Turn landed as an event, not summarised. Fix: give the act its own short paragraph, set apart from the exit procedure. Put the exit logistics after it, or cut them to a clause.

3. **Scene 3 opens by contradicting itself.** "I closed the folder without reading further" is followed by a full read and grading of the file (address, salary, movements, routing slip). Rule: strategy/03 and the beat sheet S3 beat 1, where restraint is the one beat of discipline in a chapter of indulgence. As written, the restraint is false and the "proud of the biscuit" joke loses its point. Fix: make the restraint specific and true, for example "I closed the folder before I reached the complaint." Or move the grading before the discipline beat. Keep the line that she turns the complaint summary over unread.

4. **"I didn't, for the rest of the night."** Rule: Dent Scene 3 reversal, and the beat sheet's "for the length of the chapter". Iris then spends the last page staring at the photograph, which reads as thinking about it. This blunts the Turn, because the reader should be the one looking for her. Fix: narrow it to "and I didn't, until I was in the car", or cut the claim and let the closing long look carry the refusal.

5. **Scene 3 has no live risk between the discipline beat and the photograph.** The re-entry under the foil is a clause, the tap fit is procedural, and the reasoning about the three walls is exposition. Brandt's pressure is felt (the walls, the redaction bar, the feed in the car), so the pressure rule is met. The scene's drive rests on the dossier grading alone. Fix, optional: put one beat of physical consequence into the re-crossing or the tap seating, for instance the amber light again or the foil crackle at the wrong moment. Her own competence must resolve it, and the beat should be short (it adds words, so net it against finding 1). If length is tight, leave this one.

6. **Scene 2, the unseen second sensor.** She maps every lens in the hall, then steps into the doorway before seeing the PIR above it. Her competence saves her afterward, so the hero rule holds, and I see no luck or rescue anywhere (the card, the midline and the wafer lock are all earned). But the error is unremarked. Fix, light: one clause of self-grading ("I had read every lens but the one that mattered") would keep the competence honest and fit the grade motif.

7. **Last line leaves the car.** The Turn ends in the car on "I didn't recognise her either." The Snapper then cuts to the drawer. That is acceptable and matches the outline and the sharpened option. Note for the reviser: keep it as its own paragraph so that the Turn gets its beat before the image.

**What works**

- The Scene 2 near-trip and the cooling-body recovery, solved entirely by her own knowledge, carry the declared appetite ("a liver in my hands").
- The Scene 3 close: the photograph held bare-handed, the hunger reported plainly, and "I didn't recognise her either" followed by the paperclip image.
