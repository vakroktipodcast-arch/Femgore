# Continuity review: ch07 (The Cleanup)

- **Verdict:** REVISE

## Findings

1. **The scene is declared clean, but Ch 4 and Ch 5 left Iris's fittings in it (line 11, line 15, line 19).** The draft says "Nothing else: no tie, no tape, no fibre of mine", then "Nothing stood up out of it but dust", then "Nobody had looked at that door in years." Ch 4 (adhesive foam weatherstrip round the frame, "a moving blanket hung on the inside of the door", the hasp side packed) and Ch 5 ("the sheeting, taped at its edges", the rubber wedge, the cord loop inside the door) put all of it in the room. Nothing in Ch 7 lifts, pockets or acknowledges any of it. This breaks ledger Places (pump station: "swept, sheeted and taped, weatherstrip, moving blanket on the door") and the Objects ruling that none of her kit is left at the scene. A fresh blanket and foam seal on a door "nobody had looked at in years" is also a trace. Fix: add one clause deciding it. Either the wedge, the cord loop and the sheeting go into the bag, or she names the blanket and weatherstrip as left and judges them readable as the station's own. Record whichever in the beat sheet's New facts. The author should rule on the blanket and weatherstrip, since the ledger is silent.

2. **Inventory gaps against Ch 5 and the ledger (line 7).** The planted prepaid was put "face-down in the grass beside the door" at 4:50 (Ch 5). Diane picks up a phone in the grass ("Hello?" to the dead line), so Ch 7 puts it "in after" the kit inside the station without saying how it got there. Iris's own prepaid and its earpiece, "one of a pair", are never mentioned. Also unaccounted: the filtered head-torch, the clear sleeve the Lesson One page travelled in (ledger Objects: Lesson One "travels to the reservoir in a clear sleeve"), the steel-pin box, and the remainder of the black vinyl roll and unused zip-ties. The last two must reach the back compartment (ledger Objects, drawer order; beats/ch09 carries "remainder of the black vinyl roll and the unused zip-ties"). Fix: one list sentence, e.g. "the second prepaid with its earpiece, the head-torch, the page's clear sleeve, the roll of vinyl with what was left on it". Say where the planted phone was picked up, or open with "from the grass".

3. **"Every scrap of that paper carried the number" (line 11).** The paper is cross-cut into "confetti the size of fingernail parings" (Ch 6, ledger Objects). Ch 8 says "On some of them, under a thin rule, the footer: LC-0311", and Ch 6 shows one fragment with only "LC-03". Fix: "Enough of that paper carried the number under its thin rule", or "Some of that paper".

4. **Reverse-order claim contradicts Ch 5 (line 13).** The draft lists "overshoes; outer gloves; hair cover; coverall" as "the reverse of the order I had put myself on". Ch 5 line 7 gives the donning order: coverall, hair cover, overshoes, two pairs of nitrile. The reverse starts with the outer gloves, then overshoes. Fix: swap the first two items (outer gloves before overshoes), or drop "in the reverse of the order". The ledger lists the same order, so it is not an authorial option.

5. **"Her own phone" is ambiguous (line 9).** The referent is Diane's phone, but the preceding paragraph is about Iris's prepaid. Iris's own phone is a separate object (foil sleeve, left at home on watch days, ledger Ch 4, Ch 15). Fix: "Diane's phone was still in its foil by the lantern."

6. **"I hadn't been asked" against Mara's second text (line 77).** Mara's text, quoted verbatim on line 71, says "You're senior reviewer." Ledger Ch 8: Iris is senior reviewer and signs under Mara. Iris is therefore asked, by office practice. The Turn only works if "asked" means asked to take the case. Fix: "Nobody had asked me to take it. I would be in the room anyway", or tie it explicitly to the reviewer's line.

7. **Trail "clear both ways, as it had been on every Sunday I watched it" (line 21).** The watches on the page end by about 8:40 a.m., and the kill ends at 10:47. Nothing she watched supports an empty trail at 11:25. It also sits oddly with the draft's "public footpath on a Sunday morning" (line 3). Fix: "as it had been at the hours I watched it", or cut the comparison.

8. **Egress duration (line 3).** "The long one took the best part of half an hour." Ledger Timeline gives the heels-sit to about 10:56 and exit at about 11:25, about 29 minutes. Ch 6 S3 (tape off, ties cut, pin, marker) falls inside that window. Line 7 shows "the bag was already half full", so the egress alone cannot be 25-plus minutes. Fix: "best part of twenty minutes", or state that the half hour starts when she sat back.

9. **Grades of herself versus the ledger.** The draft has "I graded it myself", "Presentation: distinction" (line 17), "Sear: distinction", and the lecture given "a pass". The ledger (People, Iris) says Ch 19's "competent" at the mirror is "her first grade of herself", and Ch 18 "explicitly does not grade herself". The beat sheet licenses grading her own egress, meal and lecture, so these are probably grades of work, not of self. The ledger does not say so. This is a ledger-scope note, not a prose fix. Add a line to People/Iris: Ch 7 grades her own work (egress presentation, sear, lecture); "first grade of herself" means her person, in Ch 19.
