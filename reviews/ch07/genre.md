- **Verdict:** REVISE (light: two line-level fixes; the chapter otherwise meets the genre contract).

- **Findings:**
  1. Line 59: "enjoyed the accuracy of it the way you enjoy a clock". Iris uses the word for the feeling. That breaks strategy/10, Act I ("enjoys ... and does not call it enjoyment"), and the outline's "Appetite: present and unnamed." Moving the pleasure onto "accuracy" helps, but the verb still names it four chapters too early, and it takes the edge off Ch 12, where she first describes the pleasure as such. Fix: file it under the grade instead. For example, "and I stood at the window and marked the accuracy of it, the way you check a clock that keeps good time and find it has," or end on a grade ("Timing: distinction."). Keep the clock.
  2. Lines 9, 37 and 63 together: "her daughter, asking her to call", "Somewhere a woman had sat beside a phone", "used her daughter's name". There are three family-pathos beats. The four o'clock one (l.37) is the only time Iris turns towards the victim's family on her own account, and "That is all I am going to say about it" tells the reader she has felt something she won't name. Together the three beats move her towards remorse. That breaks strategy/10 (cost is Ch 18's to deliver) and style/12 (it makes her easier to dismiss as a killer with a conscience underneath). Fix: keep l.9 and l.63, which are cold, procedural and grading. Recast l.37 as a flat schedule fact with no closing line, e.g. "I had slept through four o'clock. Diane called her mother every Sunday at four; she told a trade magazine so. That call was now somebody else's problem to explain." Or cut l.37 entirely.

- **What works:**
  1. The meal (ll.43–49): food described with the precision of tissue, the onglet/diaphragm turn ("I had watched one stop the morning before. I salted it."), and the lecture failing on its own terms ("silent on whether it wants bone marrow"). This is exactly where the reader stops believing her. Do not soften or shorten it.
  2. The scene-1 aftermath: smell first (urine, "named and stepped round"), the incisor print in the glove, "Presentation: distinction", and the forty seconds of lingering logged as a cost to the schedule. Appetite is present, noticed and filed, never named.
  3. The rigor paragraph, "set round the paper as hard as if she had decided to keep it", and the still left hand / steady thumb pair. Both put the body on the page with her response in the same line.
