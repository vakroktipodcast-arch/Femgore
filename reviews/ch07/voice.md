# Voice review: ch07

- **Verdict:** REVISE

## Findings

1. **Line 25. "in a flash of hi-vis and a slap of displaced air"** is the most generic sentence in the chapter. It is one long run-on for the only action beat in the scene. Breaks 02 (Rhythm: short declaratives for action) and the anchor test (could appear in any thriller). Fix: break it into short sentences, with the sensation first and the name after it, the way Iris always takes smell first and sight last. For example: "A bell. Two notes. Air on my cheek before I had turned my head. Hi-vis, a back, gone." Cut "flash of".

2. **Line 25. "and didn't care for the number."** She takes her pulse and keeps the figure back. She is "scrupulously reliable about everything else" (02, last point), and the anchors always give the count ("His was ninety-six. Mine was a hundred and ten."). Fix: state it, for example "A hundred and four. I didn't care for it."

3. **Line 25. "I had not been alone out there for as long as I had believed."** This repeats "a track I had believed I had to myself" two sentences earlier. Breaks 01 and 02: it explains instead of implicating. Fix: cut the earlier clause so the Turn line carries the beat alone. Alternatively, keep the earlier clause and end on the pulse.

4. **Line 45. "a city holding its breath to see whether you'll notice"** is a stock image that could sit in any novel, and it leans purple. Fix: replace it with something she can actually hear or name at three in the morning, such as the fridge compressor or a single car, in her exact register.

5. **Line 69. "I knew whose body it was. I had known before…"** This tells the reader what they have known since Chapter 6. It is generic suspense phrasing and breaks 02 (drift toward explanation). Fix: cut both sentences. The paragraph's real finding, "had not told her what had come in with it", is the stronger last line.

6. **Line 73. "So the Chief already knew it was political."** The next sentence already shows this, so this one just explains it. Fix: cut it and open the paragraph on "Nobody had opened her yet".

7. **Line 33. "I am reporting it, not making it mean anything."** Iris recites the house rule (11, the no-apology point) as a disclaimer. That is explanation, and it duplicates "I noted it as new data" and "I noted it as a fact" nearby. Fix: end the paragraph on "There were none." The bare finding implicates more.

8. **Line 61. "The grey came up slowly towards six."** Breaks 06 point 1: grey is never used unmodified. Fix: name the colour precisely, for example "slate", "the colour of dishwater", or "ash".

9. **Line 11. "as it turned out, to a list with me on it, and I should have asked."** The hindsight explains ahead, and "I should have asked" comes close to the apologetic register 11 warns against. Fix: keep the fact and cut the regret, for example "and that one ran to a list with me on it." Leave the reader to judge whether she should have asked.

10. **Line 63. "very good" appears twice in two sentences** ("it was very good" / "very good at their job"). This flattens the grading voice. Fix: change the second instance, for example "Somebody in that room knew exactly what they were doing."

## What works

- The Mara non-grade (line 73, "put no mark of my own beside them"). It is exactly the noticeable absence 07 asks for. Do not touch it.
- The register collision in the marrow and onglet passages ("medullary cavity of a femur… licking the spoon"; "the muscle that does the breathing… I salted it."). This is the voice at full strength, and the long sentence there is earned.
- The joke rate is at or above the Act I floor in every scene (for example "paperweight with ambitions", "taking it up with management", "the dead were busy"). Revisions must not thin it.
