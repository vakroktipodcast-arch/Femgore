# Continuity review: Chapter 16 (Continuity lens)

- **Verdict:** REVISE. One hard hand-off error (the vetiver "last met" line), one internal prop contradiction, and several small ledger slips. All are line-level fixes. Dates, times, counts, speech lines, the record sequence, the foil sleeves, the grading scope and the Ch 15 and Ch 17 hand-offs otherwise hold.

## Findings

1. **High. The "last met" smell contradicts Ch 13.** Locator: "the dry green note I had last met on the brass pull of my own drawer".
   - Rule broken: `manuscript/ch13.md` lines 5 and 53 (continuity ledger, Brandt, kitchen, Ch 13). On Sun 4 Oct Iris met the same note at Brandt's sink: "dry and green and rooty, a cellar somebody keeps well". She washed with the vetiver soap and says "My right hand would smell of him for hours."
   - Her last encounter with it is therefore his kitchen, not her flat in Ch 9. The "trespass with nobody attached" is also backwards, because in Ch 13 she was the trespasser.
   - The error comes from `beats/ch16.md` S1 item 2, which cites Ch 9 and is wrong on this point.
   - Fix: "the dry green note I had last met at his own sink, on my own right hand, when it was a house and not yet a man". Keep the note unnamed. The same fix applies to the S3 "the dry green note" line if it is reworded.

2. **Medium. The page is in two places at once.** Locators: "laid it on his knee, still folded"; "turned his glass a quarter-turn on the page"; "put his glass down on the page instead".
   - Rule broken: `beats/ch16.md` S1 item 5 and S2, and Ch 17 (the ringed page "on the table beside the photograph"). The page lives on the low table under his glass.
   - In the draft it goes onto his knee, and his glass then turns and settles "on the page" with no step putting the page back.
   - Fix: after "Read it a lot." and "I can see.", add that he laid the page back on the table and set the glass on it again. Alternatively, change both glass lines to "on his knee" and have him lay the page down before he rises. The Ch 17 ring needs glass on page on table.

3. **Low-medium. "Not cold; I want that on the record" pre-empts Ch 18.** Locator: "My face was hot. Not cold; I want that on the record."
   - Rule broken: `beats/ch16.md` S2 sensory lead ("Cold belongs to Ch 18. Don't preempt it."). Ledger, Ch 24–25: Iris is "cold since Ch 18".
   - The narrator announcing the absence of cold gives away the Ch 18 turn.
   - Fix: cut "Not cold; I want that on the record." and keep "The heat came up out of my collar and stayed."

4. **Low. Food inventory incomplete.** Locator: "I had eaten nothing since a cup of stock".
   - Rule broken: ledger, Ch 15 (stock and one heel of bread at about 7:30 p.m.) and `manuscript/ch15.md` line 67.
   - Fix: "since a cup of stock and a heel of bread at half past seven". Also check S3's "I hadn't eaten" against this.

5. **Low. Iris asserts "women" without a source.** Locator: "Eleven women had signed what I signed."
   - Brandt says only "names". The ledger gives no line telling Iris the signatories are all women. She can infer it from "the only one", but nothing on the page does.
   - Fix: "Eleven people had signed what I signed", or add a clause such as "and I knew without being told which kind of eleven".

6. **Low. Gloves location conflicts across beat sheets.**
   - `beats/ch16.md` puts the leather gloves in her jacket pocket on the second chair. This matches the prose ("put them in the pocket of my jacket").
   - `beats/ch17.md` (Body, S3) puts them "in her bag".
   - Ch 17 prose never mentions them, so no prose fix is needed. Correct the ch17 beat sheet, or record the jacket pocket in the ledger Objects.

7. **Low. Bound-in claim about the syllabus origin.** Locator: "until the first bus went past under the window, and wrote the rest".
   - It implies the whole syllabus was written in one night. The ledger says only "written and locked in the drawer" two years before, and Ch 1 says "at about this time of night" (the evening, about 9 p.m.).
   - Fix: "and kept writing", or leave as is and enter it as a fact. Do not state which pages were written that night.

8. **Low. Wording nit.** Locator: "He didn't say the name on it."
   - The Apex page carries a title, THE APEX, and no name appears in the ledger.
   - Fix: "the word at the head of it".
