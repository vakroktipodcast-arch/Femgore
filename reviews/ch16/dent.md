# Dent review: Chapter 16 (The Abattoir)

- **Verdict:** REVISE. The structure is sound and all three Turns and the Snapper land. The chapter is probably over length, and the named pressure drops out of Scene 3.

**Turn map**
- S1: lands at "He had set out two glasses." It is the last line and it is an event.
- S2: lands at "I had believed myself alone. I had built two years..." It is dramatised by the needle drop and "unbearable". See finding 3 for pre-statement.
- S3: lands at "It was going to be the best night of my life." Both halves are given. The Snapper follows on its own line: "The record ended. The arm lifted..."

**Word count.** I could not run `scripts/lint.py` (no shell access), so this is an estimate by paragraph. S1 is about 1,050 to 1,300, S2 about 1,200 to 1,350, S3 about 1,050 to 1,150. The total is about 3,300 to 3,700 against a target of ~3,150. Run lint to confirm.

**Findings**
1. **Over length (probable).** style/10 point 1: "Chapter 17 may run long; nothing else may." strategy/03 asks for scenes of about 1,000 to 1,100 words. S2 is the heaviest. S1 carries a long photograph paragraph ("I read it the way I read a body"), the "I have been read before" digression, and the Lantern lines ("Your reporter... Good phone voice. / Didn't fight it. The farm."). Fix: lint it, then trim S1 and S2 toward 1,000 to 1,100 each.
   - Cut the "Your reporter / The farm" exchange. It is not in the beat sheet, it spends Ch 13/14 material, and it delays the Turn.
   - Fold the "I have been read before... Including me." paragraph into one or two sentences.
   - Trim the pump-station phone aside.
2. **Named pressure is absent from S3.** style/10 point 4 and strategy/03 (her own knowledge begins here). The beat sheet's appetite-at-exit says "She folds it in, and the reader should see her do that." After S2, the eleven names never return in S3. Its pressure is only Brandt's compliance and her appetite. Fix: add one beat in the binding. For example, as she tapes a wrist she thinks of ten other signatures, and the thought goes into the work instead of stopping it. Use "folded" imagery. Keep it to two sentences and do not spend Ch 18's cold.
3. **S2 Turn is pre-stated before it lands.** style/10 point 3, landed as an event. "Since the night of the quarter-inch I had believed..." and "Eleven women had signed what I signed" already state the loss of "the only one". The Turn paragraph ("I had believed myself alone. I had built two years on being the only one, and every page in that drawer stood on it, and he had just told me the floor was a list") then restates it. Fix: cut either the "floor you lay" paragraph or the "floor was a list" clause. Let the Turn open on the needle and the turned back.
   - The "Eleven women" line also asserts gender. The beat sheet does not fix it and Ch 18 does the inferring. Make it "Eleven people", or cut it.
4. **S3 Turn is diluted by a stack before it.** After "I was going to take him at his word" (the admission, strong) come three paragraphs of inventory: gloves, nitrile, pulse, arousal and hunger. Then the Turn paragraph arrives. Fix: move the gloves and pulse/arousal material above "Take your time". Let "I was going to take him at his word" run straight into "It was going to be the best night of my life." The gloves paragraph can stay as the step between that and the Snapper.
5. **POV leak, small.** S1: "He had been sitting here since twenty past six with that sentence." Iris does not know the 6:20 arrival or the chosen sentence. Beats say this is Interlude 3 and "Iris doesn't know this." Fix: "He had been sitting here some hours," or cut the clause.
6. **Hero rule: mostly clean, but check.** She keys in the door herself and seats the seal by her own act, so there is no convenient door. Brandt hands over the phone, lies down unaided and tests the ties. That is consent, not luck or rescue. The only risk is that her competence faces no resistance in S3. The reader should feel the work as skill (S1 reading, S3 the binding), and it does. No fix is needed, but do not add helpful logistics.
7. **First and last sentences.** "I didn't knock." and "I rolled up my sleeves." both carry weight, and the Snapper is an image, not a cliffhanger. No fix.

**What works**
- The two-glasses Turn: the table has a prop, the glass is set square, and the line sits alone.
- The S2 pivot. The record runs out unnoticed, "the room had got bigger", Brandt's complete sentence, "I had not prepared a joke for being understood", and his turned back as the kindness. Do not touch any of it.
- The Snapper's single audible arm, set against the unheard run-out in S2, with nothing after "sleeves".
