- **Findings:**

1. **Scene 1, para 9: "I want to be plain about the arithmetic, because it was plain."** Breaks 01 (direct address is for implicating the reader, never for explaining) and 02 (the voice drifts toward explanation). The paragraph after it is a briefing on how regulators and abuse desks work. Fix: cut the opening sentence and "Regulators are slow… than it had to be." Keep "I needed him dark for forty-eight hours" and the Gatekeeper line, which carries the reasoning in her own voice.

2. **Scene 2, para 33: "Its lease, its alarm contract and its building cameras…"** This logistics block (vehicles, holding company, routing, facilities mail, floor plan) could appear in any tech-thriller. Of 06's tools it uses only colour-free nouns, it has no body, no joke, and no Latinate/plain collision (02). It is the longest stretch in the chapter without a laugh, which matters in Act II. Fix: cut it by half and keep one concrete image ("the way you'd write a shopping list" works). Put in one dry line about facilities staff, or about the specification's prose, which you can borrow from para 43.

3. **Scene 1, para 23: "My tap had gone dark with his line, because…"** Explanation drift (02). Three sentences of network topology before the line that matters. Fix: cut it down to "My tap went dark with his line. I would have to see him with my own eyes, like a person." and keep the touch to the seam.

4. **Scene 2, para 37: "I reasoned the car… a frozen shell does not pay for cars."** More reasoning shown on the page, and "That was a pressure I hadn't had to apply. So was the other one." is generic plotting language. Fix: compress the car logic to one clause. Keep "Holt was herding for me, for nothing. He has always been good with staff." It is the best line in the scene.

5. **Scene 2 Turn, para 53: "and I understood that this was his answer to the broken record."** This explains the Turn after para 49 has already landed it ("You bring a bottle to a dinner."). It breaks 02, Iris reports and the reader judges. Fix: end the scene on "He went willingly." plus at most "He had read the syllabus." Cut the "understood" clause.

6. **Scene 2, para 41: "That's twice, if you're counting, and I've stopped pretending you aren't."** This uses up ch17's anchor move early: "If you are keeping a list, and I think by now you are". Related tics are also bunched up here. "I am reporting that" (para 67) is lifted word for word from ch17. "to the flat, which had nobody else in it" (para 77) is lifted word for word from ch01. Fix: keep one callback at most, either the flat line as a deliberate echo or "reporting". Rephrase the "counting" line so it implicates without the ch17 list construction, e.g. "That's twice. You may as well start the column."

7. **Scene 1, paras 25 and 27: "And I distrusted it." / "and I didn't trust it"** The same beat appears twice, two paragraphs apart, which flattens the trust motif that scene 3 pays off ("I noticed that I trusted him"). Fix: cut "And I distrusted it." and start para 25 at "Nothing on the syllabus had ever gone faster…"

8. **Generic stakeout lines: "He didn't see me." (para 47), "the farm was dark" (para 21), "It was the only door that hadn't closed." (para 33)** Any thriller could contain these sentences (brief). Fix: cut "He didn't see me". The simile before it already does the work. Give "the only door" an Iris twist or cut it.

9. **Colour, 06 point 1: "pink" appears four times without a modifier** (paras 3, 17, 27, 63). Fix: name at least the seam's colour precisely in both places it appears, e.g. "the pink of boiled ham" or "the colour of a new scar on a white man's knee". Keep the blush, which is anatomically placed.

10. **Taste, 06 point 4: "I ate one heel of bread with it."** Everything she eats gets described, and the bread is the only food left bare. Fix: add a clause on its crust and its staleness.
