# Ch 15 — Forensic and procedural accuracy

- **Verdict:** REVISE

- **Findings:**

1. **"I couldn't see whether the freeze had reached the card"** (S2). Nothing in the chapter freezes anything. A regulator complaint freezes no accounts, and an abuse desk cuts a line, not a card. The car-service freeze is fixed in the ledger (Timeline, Mon 12 Oct: "car-service card frozen Tue"; Who knows what: Brandt reads it as hers), but the prose gives it no cause, so it reads as luck. "A pressure I hadn't had to apply" makes that worse. Breaks cast.md, Iris ("research, preparation and patience"). **Fix:** in S1, have her send the same death notice to the shell's bank, whose name is on the annual reports in the Lantern cabinet (ledger, Objects). Banks freeze an account as a matter of routine once the sole signatory is dead. Then S2's reasoning about the car is a consequence she set up and is only waiting to confirm.

2. **"Abuse desks are not, because abuse desks are run by people"** (S1). An upstream provider does not cut a business customer's line in nine hours on an anonymous interception allegation. It sends the customer a notice and waits. What it *will* do quickly is suspend an account whose only authorised contact is dead, pending re-verification. That also fits the ledger's "restored after its review" (Places, Lantern). **Fix:** one clause. The complaint points out that the account's officer of record is the dead woman and attaches the public obituary or news item. The cut then becomes a compliance hold rather than a fright. The "dead across the threshold" line already carries this idea, so make it the mechanism.

3. **"At 4:41 my phone buzzed against my hip"** (S1). If this is her own phone, it ties her personal device to the tap's relay endpoint. That is the first link Reyes would find. The ledger has the tap viewed only on clean devices (Objects, Lantern tap and Surveillance; Open, tablet ruled clean), and cast.md says she knows "what investigators look for". **Fix:** make it the prepaid ("the prepaid buzzed"), or the clean tablet in her work bag checked at the sinks. While you are there, let the house units dropping at the same minute be what tells her it is the line and not the tap. At present she asserts that the provider cut it before she has any evidence.

4. **"as far as the world was concerned, I was at home"** (S3). This is wrong, and an investigator would laugh. A phone in a Faraday sleeve doesn't stay "at home". It drops off the network, and a silence of several hours on the night of a homicide is a flag in itself. The ledger fixes the phone as sealed in foil and carried (People, Ch 15 and Ch 18), so keep the action and correct the claim. **Fix:** something like "so that the last place it had been was home, and after that nowhere. A gap is not an alibi; it is not a location either."

5. **"I left a message in my own voice"** (S2). This puts a recording of her voice on the victim's voicemail, two days before his death, from a fake reporter. Police will pull that recording. She has a public voice exemplar, since she is a pathologist who testifies. Ledger Ch 5's "does not disguise her voice" applies to Diane, face to face, and doesn't cover a recording left behind. **Fix:** have her price it in one clause, for example that the burner is dead by morning and she knows she has paid with a sample. Alternatively, change the register enough that it isn't her courtroom voice.

6. **"Hair washed and pinned up, because I would be working"** (S3). For Diane she wore a hair cover and a coverall (ledger, Ch 5). This kit has neither (ledger, Objects, Ch 15 kit). Her DNA is on her own office's elimination register (ledger, People, Ch 8/12). A professional will ask about it. **Fix:** don't add kit. Add one clause in which she knows the risk ("pinned tight; one hair of mine on his carpet comes home to my own register") and takes it, in keeping with the date-not-scene logic of the chapter.

7. **"seven pairs of dots either side of it"** (S3). Seven simple interrupted stitches leave seven puncture marks on each side, which is seven pairs *across* the line. As written it describes 28 marks. **Fix:** "seven dots either side of it" or "seven pairs of dots across it".

8. **"Then I filed my nails short and square over the sink"** (S3). The order is wrong. Nails come first and the scrub comes after. Anyone who has scrubbed in would notice. **Fix:** move the filing before the scrub.

9. **"they stayed there for fifty minutes, still"** (S2). Two sentences later, her fingertips are on the wound and "kept them there". **Fix:** "one hand on the wheel", or have the hands come off the wheel only at the wound.

10. **"his set began without him"** (S3, at 9:55 p.m.). The Tabard set runs from 9 to 11 (ledger, Brandt), so at that time it is an hour in. **Fix:** "his set was an hour in without him."

11. **"I attached the incorporation filing I had found in the cabinet"** (S1). Attaching an internal paper copy tells whoever reads the complaint that the complainant was inside Lantern. Incorporation filings are public. Also, a bus-station café is one of the most heavily filmed rooms in a city. **Fix:** she attaches the public state filing ("the same document I'd seen in his cabinet, from the state's own site"). Optionally, give her one clause about the café's dome camera or her back to it.

12. **"For the first time in my career I was trusting somebody else's paperwork"** (S2). A pathologist trusts tox, histology and investigators' reports every day. **Fix:** "For the first time on the syllabus".

13. **"it is almost always the thing that kills them"** (S1). A pathologist wouldn't say cardiomegaly is almost always the cause of death. **Fix:** "it is the commonest thing that kills them."

- **What works:**
  - The stitch removal: lifting the knot, cutting on the skin side, and drawing the stitch through its own track. It is correct technique and matches the ledger exactly (4-0 nylon, seven, tails long, day ten).
  - The chain of consequences in S1, where cutting Brandt's line also blinds her own tap and the house units at the same minute. This is honest and exactly right for the ledger's relay architecture. Keep it.
  - The kit layout follows the ledger's Ch 15 list item for item, with no page. The brine is cooled before the bird goes in.
