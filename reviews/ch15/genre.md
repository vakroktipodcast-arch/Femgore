- **Verdict:** REVISE (light). The appetite is present and at the right stage of the arc. Three local fixes, no structural change.

- **Findings:**

1. **Scene 2 goes dead in the logistics block (moderate).** The passage from "With the feeds gone I had to herd him blind" to "the only door that hadn't closed" (line 33), and "On Tuesday morning, before my shift, I reasoned the car" (line 37), are all procedure. There is no body in them, no relish and no joke. Act II needs "the planning is foreplay" (strategy/10, Act II), and the genre contract promises appetite and humour on every page (strategy/02). Right now the herding reads like a heist briefing. Fix: cut the paragraph at line 33 by about a third (the grommets and the contractor spec can stay, and the provenance of the card and mail can be compressed). Then give her one physical beat of pleasure in the herding, such as pulse, heat, or the wound under her fingers when she sees the keypad code in plain text. Also give her one line that names the pleasure of shutting doors on him, in the register of the "cheese course" line.

2. **Brandt's body is never the object of her appetite (moderate).** All the anticipation goes into clothes, lipstick, instruments and the chicken. When he appears ("He got out in charcoal") she reads his bottle and his stance but not his body. In Act II the appetite has to be "named, indulged" (outline/ch15, act pressure; strategy/10). The tasting-menu table invites one forward glance at the meal and doesn't get one, so the instrument list (line 57) reads as inventory. Fix: give her one sentence, at the table or as he crosses under the sodium light, in which she lets herself look at him the way she looked at the 460-gram heart: a weight, a temperature, one named structure she will reach. Then have her stop, without apologising. Keep it to one line so 17 is not pre-empted.

3. **The self-reporting tic is overused, and that pushes her toward case study (minor).** These appear in quick succession: "which you may write down" (l.17), "I've stopped pretending you aren't" (l.41), "I didn't take it back" (l.61), "I am reporting that" (l.67), "I'm telling you that I didn't" (l.71), "there was nobody to give it to" (l.73). At this density the reader starts diagnosing her, which is one of The Test's dismissal modes ("as a case study", style/12). It also dulls the two that matter. Fix: cut the tails at l.17 and l.41 so the Mara beats stand on the noticing alone. Keep l.67 ("I liked it. I am reporting that.") and the lipstick line, which carry the appetite.
