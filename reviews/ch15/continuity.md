## Findings

1. **Source of the incorporation filing contradicts Ch 11.** S1: "the incorporation filing I had found in the cabinet in Brandt's warehouse." In `manuscript/ch11.md` she finds Diane as officer of record on the public register (Fenwick Lane's annual report, "the register had not been told"). In the cabinet she opens the drawers, takes only the 6x4 photograph and "left the rest" (ledger, Places: Lantern cabinet; Objects: 6x4 photograph). She therefore has no copy of cabinet papers to attach. Attaching them would also hand the regulator proof of a break-in. Fix: make it the register document, e.g. "the annual report from the state register, Diane's signature at the foot." Keep "Officer of record: Diane Pell."

2. **Hands "still" and then on the wound.** S2: "my hands on the wheel at ten and two, and they stayed there for fifty minutes, still", then "I put my fingertips on it and kept them there." The ledger says her fingertips go to the wound when she thinks, so the touch is right and the absolute is wrong. Fix: "stayed there for fifty minutes, bar once", or move the touch before "ten and two".

3. **Chicken visible in a lidded tub.** S3: "the chicken on the bottom shelf, pale under its plate." S1 and ledger (Objects, Food, the Ch 17 chicken): "lidded tub", plate weighing the bird down. A lid hides the bird. Fix: "I lifted the lid a finger's width and there was the chicken, pale under its plate", or drop "pale".

4. **Closing bridge asserts unledgered geography and a mistimed set.** "somewhere across the river his set began without him." Ledger: the Tabard is "private", with no location. The set runs 9 to 11 p.m., and she parks at 9:55, so it began almost an hour earlier. Fix: "his set was an hour old and going on without him", and drop "across the river". The printworks is east of the river, so the phrase is also ambiguous.

5. **Front-door stair light versus the rear stairwell route.** S2: Brandt "let himself in with his own key" and the light shows "behind the frosted glass of the stairwell." Earlier in S2, and in the closing bridge, the route she uses is "a rear car park with a stairwell up to the suite corridor" and "the stairwell, by its code." The ledger (Places) fixes the rear car park and stairwell but says nothing of a street entrance. Fix: one clause saying the stair is one stair, visible from the street through the frosted front glass, with front and rear doors onto it. Otherwise Ch 17's reversed exit route has two stairs to choose between.

6. **Taxi never tied to the car-service inference.** The beat sheet and ledger make the 6:20 taxi the confirmation of the freeze Iris only inferred on Tuesday morning. The prose says only "Not his car." Fix: add a clause linking it, e.g. "Not his car. The shell had stopped paying for cars." Optional, but it closes the open inference.

7. **Mara's "twice" (note for the ledger, no prose change).** Ledger and beat sheet: Iris "says yes, twice". The prose gives two spoken "Yes" and narrates "as far as a needle would have lifted, and no further." That fits "side two never played" (ledger, Records). The beat sheet's "all of it, twice" would not have fitted. Record the prose version in the ledger. Mara humming four bars and "stopped in the middle of the fifth" is fine against "four bars".

8. **Iris's own voice on Brandt's voicemail (note).** S2: "I left a message in my own voice." Ch 5 (ledger) says she doesn't disguise her voice, so this is consistent. Brandt now holds a recording of it, and Ch 16–17 must not have him react as if he has never heard her. Ch 16 so far does not.
