# ch20 — Dent structure review

- **Verdict:** REVISE. The structure is sound: three scenes, all three Turns and the Snapper land. The problems are one convenient door, one muddled Turn sentence, missing pressure in S2 and S3, and length.

## Turn check

- **S1:** Lands. The label is found at "A white label ran across the gap" and recognised at "I knew it before I could read it." The outline's Turn then lands as the last two lines: "The green room was a converted meeting suite…" and "The seal was a sticker." The seal is delivered as an event and not summarised.
- **S2:** Lands, but muddied. See finding 3. The scene ends on "I signed it with the name Hannah used for me… had to put the pen down."
- **S3:** Lands at "This time I didn't look like someone going on a date." followed by "I preferred the date." The Snapper follows it: "Behind me, the letter squared to the edge of the desk." It is an image, not a cliffhanger.

## Findings (most serious first)

1. **Convenient door (hero rule).** "The closet was unlocked; it was set-up day." This is an unlocked door Iris did not unlock. It breaks `bible/strategy/03` (hero rule: "No luck, no rescue") and `bible/style/10` point 5 ("no unlocked doors she didn't unlock"). Ch26's escape depends on this closet, so the door has to be earned. **Fix:** make it her competence. Have her read the hotel plan or the par sheet and know that housekeeping closets are keyed alike or latched open on event days. Or have her try the handle on the way down, after reading the lock schedule in the annex, and record that she would have needed a different route if it had been shut. One clause is enough.

2. **Pressure drops out of S2 and S3.** The act pressure is Reyes closing on paper, and appetite starved. Reyes appears only in S1 ("Special Agent E. Reyes would have eleven names by the weekend"). S2 is set on Saturday night, the night the list is produced (`beats/ch20.md` S1 and Carries out), and the draft never says it has gone. S3 has no Reyes at all. This breaks `bible/style/10` point 4 (named pressure in every scene). **Fix:** add one clause in S2 saying the list went out that day, perhaps as a tap summary line she doesn't read. Add one in S3, for example the Monday phone switched off, with a reason that is about Reyes and not only Hannah.

3. **S2 Turn sentence is unclear.** "After the third letter my hand stopped on its own." "Third letter" collides with "third page" and "third voicemail". It reads as the third letter of the name, which is a strange count for a name Hannah uses. "It was probably somewhere in the voicemail; I wouldn't know" also undercuts the Turn. The beat says the Turn carries that Iris has stopped hearing the name, not that she has forgotten it. This breaks Dent point 3 (the Turn is an event or admission, not a fudge). **Fix:** cut "After the third letter" and the "probably somewhere in the voicemail" hedge. Keep the Turn as the closing event. For example: "I signed it with the name Hannah used for me. My hand stopped on its own, the nib still on the paper, and I had to put the pen down."

4. **Seeded element missing, so ch26 loses a set-up.** The outline editorial note says every element ch26 uses is seen here first, including the minute. The draft gives "about ten paces" from the stair door to the green-room door. It never gives the eight further paces to the seal or the twelve-second figure ("a figure she writes down and does not yet use", beat S1 item 6). Ch22 and ch23 carry that number. **Fix:** add one sentence in the S1 route paragraph, for example "Eight paces on, to the seal. Twelve seconds. I wrote that down and did not use it."

5. **Over length.** I estimate about 3,450–3,500 words against a ~3,150 target and a 1,000–1,100 per-scene guide. S1 is about 1,300 and S3 about 1,250. This breaks `bible/style/10` point 1 ("nothing else may" run long). **Fix:** run the lint count. Then trim S1's tap paragraph ("the farm had gone back to humming in its warehouse…"), which is a jargon-heavy re-explanation. The beat asked for one clause. Also trim the S3 stair and gate counts, and one of the two "decided to stay" echoes.

6. **S3 Snapper is pre-empted.** "My hand went to the pocket. The paper was there." comes a few lines before the Snapper ends on "where my hand kept finding it." The final image is already spent. **Fix:** cut "The paper was there." Keep "My hand went to the pocket" or drop it, so the Snapper does the work.

7. **S1 opening page.** Scene 1 gets Iris into the trouble in its first lines, which is correct. But the second and third paragraphs, with the warm plans and the tap, delay the actual task of the credentials by about 200 words. **Fix:** this is minor. Cutting the tap exposition (finding 5) also fixes it.

## What works

- The S1 Turn: the label is recognised and the click "gone", then the single-line restatement. Do not alter it.
- S2's engine is the list of omissions, and the line "I wasn't, and I wasn't going to start in ink" lands. It also keeps the beat's rule against apology.
- The ritual hollowed in S3, ending on "Now it was a rule. I didn't eat.", and the mirror line, which gives the second Turn without a dash cut-off.
