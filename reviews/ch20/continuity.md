## Findings (most serious first)

1. **"The only paper in the flat" is false in the same chapter.** S2 opens "The only paper in the flat was the stock I had printed the syllabus on." In S1 the floor plans "had come off the printer at the desk" and she writes the protocol in pencil on paper. Ch 19 has her mapping in pencil on squared paper. The ledger (Objects, fourth page) has the Ch 18 page written on "a blank printer sheet", and Places lists a printer with a tray at the desk. The letter on syllabus stock is fixed (ledger, Letter to Hannah), so keep the stock and fix the claim. Fix: "The only paper in the flat worth writing a letter on" or "the only paper left that wasn't printer stock". Alternatively, say she is out of printer paper after the plans and the badge.

2. **The Ruth Calder badge's printer and stock have no provenance, and the sequence is impossible as written.** S1: "The card printer had been secondhand and paid for in cash, and the stock in its hopper was the same white PVC, from the supplier whose name was on the campaign's own invoice." Two problems.
   - After "the campaign's own badge printer in the volunteer office" the phrase "the card printer" reads as the campaign's machine. Ch 21 and the ledger make it Iris's own. Fix: "my own card printer, secondhand and paid for in cash".
   - She could only learn the supplier from the Sat 24 Oct card. In Ch 19 (Sun 25 Oct) she has not opened the volunteer drive or the audit. So the PVC must have been bought between Sun 25 and Fri 30 Oct, and the prose should say so ("I had ordered a box of the same stock on Monday") or drop "from the supplier". Ledger Objects (Credentials, Election Eve) and ch21 both need the printer recorded. See New facts.

3. **Dock geometry is inverted for the Sunday entry.** S3: "A receiving supervisor sat in the glass booth… I walked the corridor in, past the service-lift lobby and on towards the stair door." Ledger, Places (dock-level layout, fixed): the booth and the Stair C fire door are at one end, about 40 m of corridor, and the bay, shutter and gate at the other. The lift lobby opens off the corridor short of the bay. Coming in from the bay she passes the lift lobby first and reaches the booth afterwards. The supervisor cannot look at her lanyard before she has walked the corridor. Fix: put the lift lobby before the booth ("I walked the corridor in, past the service-lift lobby, to the glass booth…") and then on to the stair door. This also keeps Ch 26's reverse route (stair door, booth, corridor, gate) consistent.

4. **"The order the names would be read in" gives Iris knowledge she lacks.** S1: "I didn't think about the order the names would be read in." Ledger, Who knows what: she knows from the tap that the list is produced Saturday, but not its order or her booking, and Ch 25's "Mine is near the end" is a guess. As written, the narrator implies a known reading order. Fix: "I didn't think about whose names they would be." Or cut to "I thought about Monday." Do not weaken the ledger's "never thinks of herself as tenth".

5. **"Nobody but Brandt had known it was there" is loose.** S1, on the farm. The Lantern day operator and the climate technician (Ch 11), the upstream abuse desk (Ch 15, which put the account on hold) and Diane as officer all know it exists. The ledger only fixes "Holt has never been told the farm exists." Fix: "nobody but Brandt had been told to care", or "nobody who was still alive and on the campaign".

6. **Low: "a week old now" for Hannah's voicemail.** S2, on Sat 31 Oct. The voicemail was left Sun 25 Oct at 4 p.m., so it is six days old. The 1:14 duration is right. Fix: "six days old", or leave it as loose speech and accept it.

7. **Low: repeated phrase.** "until it had decided to stay" is used for June's sticker (S2) and the gaffer strips (S3). If the echo is deliberate, fine. The ledger fixes only the sticker line (Ch 1), so check the repeat is intended.
