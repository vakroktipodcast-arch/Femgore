- **Verdict:** REVISE

- **Findings:**

1. **The elimination register is described backwards** (S1, l.17): "it can be written off as us… designed to make you disappear". An elimination profile exists so that a staff trace can be *identified*. A hit gets flagged and checked against who handled the case. It is not waved through. Any pathologist would know this, and the ledger depends on it: in Ch 12 her blood on the Lantern floor is dangerous *because* her DNA is on the register (ledger, People/Iris, Ch 12). As drafted, Ch 8 tells the reader the register protects her, and Ch 12's danger stops making sense. Fix: turn the thought around. A trace of hers would come back with her name on it. The only thing that would let it be read as contamination is that she is standing at this table today, gowned. Keep "only filing system… designed to" with its meaning reversed, e.g. "designed to find you".

2. **Ageing a bruise by colour** (S2, l.61): "I can age a bruise by its colour to within a day." This is the line a forensic reader would laugh at. Colour-ageing of bruises is notoriously unreliable, and pathologists say so in court. A pathologist with 1,400 cases boasting about it undercuts her. Fix: move the precision to histology, which keeps the joke's shape. Something like "Give me a section under the microscope and I can age a bruise to within a day or two. My own handwriting I could only get to within a year…" The punchline still works.

3. **Evidence bags labelled with surgical tape** (S1, l.19): "each label got its three strips of surgical tape". Three strips is Iris's habit for *specimen pots* (ledger, Ch 1). Evidence bags are closed with tamper-evident seals, initialled and dated across the seal, and Ch 22 already plants this ("The seal is the same make Iris uses on evidence bags at work", ledger Objects). Surgical tape on an evidence bag breaks chain of custody, and Iris then "approves" that chain. Fix: "each bag sealed and initialled across the seal". If the echo matters, let Iris notice Mara's three strips on the *specimen pots* instead.

4. **No investigator at a homicide autopsy** (S1, throughout): "I approved of the chain of custody." A detective from the investigating agency attends the post-mortem in a homicide and signs for the evidence. Here nobody is in the suite to receive the bags, so the chain she approves runs to no one. Fix: one clause, e.g. a sheriff's investigator by the door with a clipboard, signing for each bag as it leaves the tray. He can stay unnamed (the ledger allows unnamed minor figures and has a sheriff's investigator from Ch 9). This also gives "the people who read paper" a body in the room.

5. **External findings filed under "Inside"** (S1, l.21): "Inside, briefly. Petechiae in the conjunctivae under the lids". Conjunctival petechiae, facial congestion and the manubrial puncture all belong to the external examination. The internal begins at "Her Y was clean". Fix: move "Inside, briefly." to just before "Her Y was clean", or open the paragraph with "Then the rest of the outside."

6. **Gastric contents treated as a clock** (S1, l.23): "I had assumed breakfast at about seven. I had assumed right." Gastric emptying can't confirm a meal time. At most the contents fail to contradict it, and Iris would know that. Fix: "Nothing in it said otherwise." or "It did not argue with seven." That keeps the satisfaction without overclaiming.

7. **Minor rank term** (S1, l.11): "the way I would note it in a registrar". A US ME office has residents and fellows, not registrars. If house style deliberately keeps British idiom, ignore this. Otherwise use "a fellow".

8. **Optional: how Mara knows the ties came off after death** (S1, l.13): "The restraints were removed after death". The ledger fixes the finding, but the prose gives Mara no basis for it. Half a clause would earn it: the furrows are crisp, with no rebound and no recovery in the tissue.

- **What works:**
  - The layered neck dissection "in a bloodless field, as you must", with bruises at the thyroid border and an intact hyoid. It is correct, sequenced right and tied to the ledger's cause.
  - The pin puncture with "no vital reaction", the swab-by-swab handling of the lid residue, and the scale photographs of each furrow. These read as someone who has done the work.
  - S3's limits of the camera: she can see faces turn to the screen but never the screen itself, and can't read Brandt's phone. This keeps the surveillance inside what the planted units can actually do.
