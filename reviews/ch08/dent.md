(or keep only "I had no theory, and I didn't make one up.").

The extra clause adds a second beat. Fix: end on the file line, and let the withheld mark sit in the earlier "I was proud of her."
6. **Hero rule: no breach found.** Iris is not rescued. The elimination-profile aside is a system she did not rely on for escape, and she runs her own list. I found no luck and no convenient door.
