# Genre review: ch08

- **Verdict:** REVISE (light). The appetite is present, unnamed and in the right stage of the arc. Three small fixes will sharpen it.

## Findings

1. **The neck bruises get no response from her body** (line 25: "They were the print of two fingers of my left hand."). This is the most intimate trace of her on Diane, and the paragraph ends on a bare fact. Her response only arrives two paragraphs later, through Mara. This breaks style/04 point 7: every explicit line is also a line about what Iris feels as she looks. It also misses style/05 (hands). Fix: add one clause where the hands she is holding still behind her back remember the pressure. For example, the two fingertips of her left hand press together once, and she reports it as a finding. Do not name it as pleasure.

2. **Too many flags that she is not examining something** (line 45 "I am telling you that flatly…"; line 53 "That was all. I moved on."; line 55 "I did not ask myself why…"; line 65 "I'm telling you that and moving on."). Four of these in about twenty paragraphs turns "unnamed" into "pointedly named". The reader sees the method and stops being caught by it. This breaks strategy/10 (Act I: she does not call it enjoyment, and the reader should catch her out unprompted) and style/12 (it makes her easier to read as a case study). Fix: keep line 53, because the outline needs it there. Cut line 65's sentence completely, since the crackling simile already says it. Cut "I am telling you that flatly…" at line 45 and let "I know them by heart" stand on its own.

3. **In Scene 3 she says the pleasure outright and her body is missing** (line 75 "I was pleased, and I put that under professional observation"; line 79 "I was pleased about that as well."). "Pleased" twice within five paragraphs edges towards naming the appetite in Act I (strategy/10). Apart from the laptop and the thumb, the scene has no body: no cold, no hunger, no smell (style/05). Fix: keep line 75, because the filing joke is good. Replace "I was pleased about that as well" at line 79 with a physical tell in the Act I cold register. For example, she reaches for the tea on the floor, finds it cold, and drinks it anyway. Let the tell carry the pleasure.

## What works

- The stomach contents, from "My mouth filled" to "a fact about the hours" (line 23). Relish and disgust sit in the same breath, and she explains it away as physiology. This is the arc's Act I lie done exactly right. Do not touch it.
- The autopsy as a retrospective of her own work: the lashes in the tape, the paper "like bread left in the sink", the left tie one notch tighter. The gore is rendered, smell comes first, and every detail belongs to her.
- The meal at the start of Scene 2: eaten standing because she is planning, food described with tissue precision, "hungry again" put down to arithmetic, cold feet on the floorboards. This is the body register at the right point in the arc.
