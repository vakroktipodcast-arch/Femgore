# Voice review — Chapter 8

- **Verdict:** REVISE

**Findings:**

1. **Lines 71, 77, 83: narration copied word for word from the Cam interlude.** "Comms wanted a statement by six." / "Someone killed the screen." / "Somebody said *activist*." These are Cam's close-third sentences: plain, cool, deliberately free of Iris-isms. Put into Iris's first person, they flatten her into the interlude register, and the reader gets the interlude next as a repeat rather than as a second angle. Breaks 02 (voice register: exact, dry, hungry) and the interlude/Iris separation in 01. Fix: spoken lines can stay identical ("Phone. Not the wall." is speech and should match). Re-voice the narration in Iris's terms. For example, "Comms wanted a statement by six" becomes something she grades or times ("Comms asked for a statement by six, which is a deadline, not a position"). "Someone killed the screen" becomes what she sees from the bezel ("The wall went dark and took my lens's light with it").

2. **Line 31: "the way you'd say it looked like rain, to someone holding an umbrella".** This reworks anchor ch01's "the way you'd say you might take an umbrella" closely enough to read as a lift, not a callback. Breaks the anchors' standard: the same narrator, but not reusing her own phrases. Fix: write a new plain comparison, e.g. "I said it the way you agree with a stranger about the weather."

3. **Lines 45, 53, 65: direct address used to explain, three times.** "I am telling you that flatly because there is no other way to tell you." / "I will tell you this straight, because it is a fact" / "I'm telling you that and moving on." Breaks 01 (direct address sparingly, to implicate, never to explain). Three in one chapter turns a weapon into a habit. Fix: keep line 53. That is the unreliable "why" beat (02, last point), and its "That was all. I moved on." works because she protests too much. Cut the second sentence at line 45 ("I know them by heart." is enough) and cut "I'm telling you that and moving on." at line 65 (the crackling line ends the thought).

4. **Lines 19, 23: "grey" used without a modifier.** "gritted grey with dust", "grey and soft and swollen", "the grey of porridge". Breaks 06 (colour: never grey unmodified). Fix: name it exactly, e.g. "gritted the colour of cement dust", "pulp-coloured and soft and swollen", "the slate of cold porridge" (or "the colour of wallpaper paste").

5. **Line 85: "He went still."** This is a generic thriller beat; it could appear in any novel. Fix: Iris would chart it. "He stopped. No voluntary movement observed." That also calls back ch01's self-documentation without copying it.

6. **Line 75: "It leaked at about the speed of light."** A stock joke, not one of hers. Breaks 02 (her humour is about bodies, exact and accurate). Fix: give it an anatomical joke, e.g. "It leaked the way a nicked femoral leaks: everywhere at once, and nobody in the room trained to press on it."

7. **Line 27: "has an unusual level of anatomical knowledge."** This is the TV-procedural line word for word, and Mara is the most exact person Iris has. Generic dialogue. Fix: make Mara clinical and specific, and let the plot point land through precision, e.g. "Two fingers. Upper border of the thyroid cartilage, both sides, nothing wasted. They knew exactly where to put their hands." Do not have Iris grade the line out loud (07, Mara rule).

8. **Line 87: "as still as a record on a shelf".** The motif is forced in. Records on a shelf don't read as still or moving, so the comparison is decoration (leaning purple). Fix: cut the simile or replace it with a body image, e.g. "as still as a man waiting for a result."

**What works (do not break):**

- Line 33: "I had a mark ready, and I didn't give it." The Mara grading rule (07) handled exactly right, with "*Practised*" and the reviewer's line closing the Turn.
- The jokes meet the Act I floor and are hers: the retrospective (l.5), the chain-of-custody line (l.19), "Not wrong. Not even." (l.11), the kidneys (l.47), counsel billing (l.71), the butcher's calendar (l.73).
- Line 95: "my own thumb had stopped on the trackpad" mirrors Cam's thumb in the interlude without copying it. This is the right way to echo it. Keep it, and model fix 1 on it.
