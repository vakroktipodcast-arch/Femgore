## Findings

1. **S2 has no Reyes (the act's paper pressure) in it.** Reyes is felt in S1 ("an FBI liaison... a line on page four") and S3 ("a federal file, with my surname"), but S2 carries only the knowledge from 18 (the voicemail echo). Rule: strategy 03 point 3 and style 10 point 4 (named pressure in every scene). This is weak rather than broken, because the voicemail echo is itself a pressure from 18. Optional fix: one clause in S2 near "the ballroom, a muffled thudding" that puts the list back in the building, for example the name now travelling through the same walls. Do not add a number or "tenth".

2. **S3 explains what the reader should supply, which blunts the Turn's lead-in.** The line "Every sentence on this one was addressed to somebody. The somebody was not in the room." restates the preceding sentences. "I heard Diane in it" is followed by "I heard something else in it too. Two women had rung me once", and that draws the line the beat sheet (S3 point 4) says Iris must not draw. Rule: strategy 03 point 7 (admission, not summary) and beat S3 point 4. Fix: cut both restatements and end the paragraphs on "...in one courtesy" and "...somewhere to go." S3 then drops toward its target.

3. **Narrator tips the lying/forgetting question toward sincere, against the outline's "never permitted to know".** Examples are "he meant it", "He did mean it. I could hear that he meant it", "I believed him", and "as far as he was concerned the rest of the conversation was about me". The last also goes inside Holt's head. Rule: outline S2 and the beat sheet's voice rules ("If a line makes the reader sure, cut it"). The Turn is still intact, but the scene's ambiguity is the structure the S2 Turn depends on. Fix: cut "I believed him" and "as far as he was concerned...". Keep what she hears (tone, warmth) and drop her verdict. The S1 "he meant it" can go too, because S1 is the flat scene and should only report.

4. **Hero rule, minor.** Holt gives her the overrun ("letting his minute go for me a few seconds at a time, and he wanted nothing for it"), so the time she needs is a gift from him, not something she took. Nothing else is convenient: the door is sealed, the detail doesn't knock by standing instruction, and no one rescues her. Rule: strategy 03 hero rule. No change needed. If you want it tighter, let the narration say her stillness, and not his courtesy, is what holds the room.

5. **Possible continuity check, not a Dent issue.** "the latch he had thrown himself" in S2 should be checked against ch22 and the beat sheet's "door locked about 9". The beat sheet has the main door on its privacy latch, but who threw it is not stated there.
