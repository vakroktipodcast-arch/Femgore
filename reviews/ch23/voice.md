**Verdict:** REVISE

**Findings** (most serious first)

1. **Line 141: "for the first time in this book you were ahead of me".** This breaks 01 (direct address only to implicate). The point of the line is right: it makes the reader the one who already expects the killing. But "in this book" steps outside the frame. In the anchors Iris never names the book. She says "here", "on the record", "if you are keeping a list" (ch17). As written, the line reads as authorial winking, not as Iris. Fix: "and for the first time you were ahead of me, and I knew it." Or tie it to the ch17 list: "If you are still keeping that list, you were ahead of me now."

2. **Line 143: "There was the case. There was nothing else."** This breaks 02 point 5 (scrupulously reliable about everything except motive). Line 135 has just put the marker, pin and page in the same pocket, so the Snapper reads as a factual error, not a figure. On the same line, "checked for it the way you check a pocket" compares a thing to itself, and the chapter suddenly says "jacket" after a whole chapter of "blazer". Fix: "I put my hand inside the blazer and checked, the way you check for a ticket. The page. The pin. The case. Only one of them was any use." Keep the ending flat. Don't use ch25's "the way you check for your keys".

3. **Line 133: "I had planned for every response but the one he was best at."** This is a stock thriller line that could appear in any novel (brief: flag generic sentences). It also explains a beat the scene has already acted out. The list before it (denial, rage, bargaining, tears, the button) is good, and its dry nod to the grief stages is the one Iris-ism in the paragraph. Fix: end on "and for nobody to come." Or replace the last line with something concrete: "I had not prepared for manners."

4. **Line 119: "He had done a great many things. He remembered almost none..."** This breaks 02 point 1 (drift toward explanation). Holt's "Most of them were votes" lands, and then Iris explains it twice. Fix: cut from "He had done a great many things" to "as wrong". Keep "The ones he could name were on the record, under his own name, with the yeas and the nays." That is the finding. The rest is the narrator underlining it.

5. **Line 101: "He was worried about the time."** This explains (02 point 1). "Not for rescue. For his minute." has already said it. Cut the sentence. In the same vein, at line 113, "as far as he was concerned the rest of the conversation was about me" is a stock idiom. Fix: "and the rest of the conversation, for him, was about me."

6. **"He did not…" anaphora across the chapter.** Lines 13, 31, 39, 113, 119 and 129 repeat it ("He did not say so. He did not say anything… He did not ask… He did not look"). "He did not need to" appears twice (lines 31 and 129), and "hurry" appears four times (lines 15, 45). This breaks 06 and 02 point 5 (rhythm). Short declaratives are for action, and these paragraphs are observation. The ch25 anchor's short sentences each carry a new object (the cufflink, the shoe, the flag pin). Here they repeat a single verb, which pulls toward generic minimalist-thriller staccato. Fix: keep the run at line 13 (it shows what he doesn't see), cut "He did not need to" at line 31, and at line 129 cut "He did not press. He did not need to." Cut "He was in no hurry" at line 45.

7. **Line 131: "I could have recited it in my sleep."** This is a cliché, and the rest of the paragraph explains how Lesson Three is built (02 point 1, and 08, the third page is never reproduced; describing its logic clause by clause comes close). Fix: cut the cliché and cut "It went from there to his exposure… on the one before it." Keep "There was no clause in it for a man who would not stand where the page put him" and "The somebody was not in the room."

8. **Lines 83 and 93: the face examination has no Latinate term.** This breaks 02 point 2 (the Latinate/plain collision is the voice). The numb pole can be plain, but this is the one moment in the chapter where Iris reads a body "for the finding", and it should sound like her doing it. Fix: put one anatomical term into the frown, e.g. "two short lines drawn in at the glabella", or name corrugator. Keep "The pupils did nothing" plain.

9. **Smell is missing.** "Smell first. It always is, with me" (ch01, ch17). Ch25 keeps the habit even when numb ("carpet cleaner… and under that, of him"). In ch23 she checks for taste (line 89, good) but never smells him, even across a small table. Per 11's last point, show the absence through the habit. Fix: add one line near line 21 or 93 where she smells for him and gets only the damp carpet, or nothing she could name.

10. **Line 135: "I could see my hands. I could not see what they were going to do."** This is generic, and it takes ch25's hands beat ("I watched them… as if they belonged to somebody on my table") two chapters early. Fix: cut the second sentence, or end the paragraph on "I knew what was in every pocket I had on."
