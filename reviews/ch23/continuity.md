## Findings (most serious first)

1. **The "four sentences" are not four.** Quoted: "A hotel room, after an evening function. Not this hotel." As punctuated, the spoken text is five sentence-units: "It was June, three years ago." / "A hotel room, after an evening function." / "Not this hotel." / "You closed the door." / the unheard fourth. Rules broken: ledger Timeline (June, three years before: "first three sentences... 'You closed the door'... then 'the fourth'"); beats/ch23 New facts (date, place, "You closed the door", act); Ch 24 recalls "through the four sentences", and the ledger says her out-loud sentences are "four sentences and 'No.'". Fix: merge the place into one sentence, e.g. "A hotel room after an evening function, not this hotel." That gives exactly three heard plus the fourth.

2. **Holt's interior is asserted, and the ambiguity is closed.** Quoted: "He remembered almost none of them as wrong." Related lines: "the list he could name had nothing on it but votes"; "there was nobody in the room for it to be about"; "as far as he was concerned the rest of the conversation was about me". Also the S2 passage "A man who meant to dispute it would have asked. A man who was trying to find it would have asked." That passage rules out denial and searching and leaves only indifference. Rules broken: ledger Who knows what ("Holt never knows. Whether he remembers is never confirmed"); bible/cast ("The reader is never inside his head"); beats/ch23 voice rules ("If a line makes the reader sure, cut it"). Fix: keep what Iris can observe (he asked nothing, his voice was steady, he was at ease) and cut or hedge the claims about what he remembers or what the list contains. For example: "He asked nothing. I did not know what that meant, and I was not given a way to find out." Delete "He remembered almost none of them as wrong." Reword the "nobody in the room for it to be about" and "as far as he was concerned" lines as her impression ("it seemed to me").

3. **Internal contradiction at the fourth sentence.** Quoted: "He did not look away while I said it." The next paragraph says his eyes "went down to it, and stayed down for two seconds". Rule broken: ledger People, Holt, Ch 23 (cuff quarter-turn during the fourth sentence, eyes down) against the prose's own "did not look away". Fix: "He did not look at the door, or at the photograph, or at my hands. The only place his eyes left me was his cuff." Alternatively move the cufflink sentence ahead of "did not look away" and soften the latter to "he never looked away from my face except once".

4. **"Two years of drafts" and "Every draft of it had begun there".** Ledger: the syllabus was written and locked in the drawer two years before Ch 1. Ch 24 (and the Ch 16 ledger note) say Lesson Three was "written two years ago at my own desk". Drafts across the two years do not clash with that, but "had written it out at length, in the drafts, over two years" implies ongoing revision of the lesson, which Ch 24 does not. Fix: "two years of rereading and cutting", or keep the line and leave it; low priority.

5. **Hand-off to Ch 24 (note only, no change to ch23).** Ch 23 correctly keeps her right hand "beside the roll of tape" and never closing on it, as the ledger and Ch 24 ("I had not touched it all night") require. beats/ch24 "Carries in" still says her right hand is "on the vinyl roll".
