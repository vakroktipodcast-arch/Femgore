# Continuity review — ch02 (The Breach)

**Verdict:** REVISE (one real hand-off gap and four small clarifications; no contradiction of the ledger's fixed facts)

## Findings

1. **Cold does not carry from Ch 1 through the lift to Ch 3.** The draft gives only the ballroom's chosen cold ("cold air on my forearms", "the morgue's cold, the sort you choose") and, in the lift, no cold at all. `manuscript/ch03.md` opens its S1 with "the cold I had brought home from the lift", and `beats/ch03.md` (Appetite at exit) and the ledger (Ch 5 "cold has left her forearms for the first time") need the Ch 1 unchosen cold to run unbroken. Fix: one clause in the lift paragraph, e.g. the cold from Tuesday still in the forearms under the hand she is holding. Keep the ballroom AC as the chosen kind; the lift gets the other kind.

2. **Connecting-door bolt is ambiguous against "leaves the door as she found it".** "I turned mine and tried the handle" reads as locking her own bolt. `beats/ch02.md` S3 beat 4 says "deadbolt untouched", and the ledger (Places, Calloway) has deadbolts both sides with the War Room side unbolted. Fix: say she drew her own bolt before trying the handle. The closing "turned my own bolt home" then restores it, and the ledger holds.

3. **"a bin of paper cups" does not match the bin in Interlude 2.** `anchors/interlude-2-cam.md` has Brandt drop his cup "in the bin by the urn", a waste bin. Fix: "beside it a waste bin and a stack of paper cups". Related and internal: "coffee stewed on its element since lunch" sits against "the element ticking as it cooled". Pick one (urn switched off at close, with the stewed smell lingering).

4. **"A small tablet, for pairing" may read as the Ch 1 tablet.** Ch 1 ends with the debate tablet left on, propped against the fruit bowl at the flat. Nothing says she retrieved it, and Ch 3 treats it as a fixture of the kitchen. Fix: "a second, smaller tablet" or "a cheap tablet", so Ch 3's "propped against the fruit bowl (as the tablet was in Ch 1)" is not undercut.

5. **Own phone carried into the building.** "my phone held face-up in front of me" is Iris's own phone (no other is mentioned). The ledger's foil-sleeve and phone-at-home practice starts at Ch 4 and has no earlier rule, so this does not contradict it. Fix: none required. Optionally make it a prepaid, so the Ch 3 and Ch 4 phone habit does not look like a lapse here.
