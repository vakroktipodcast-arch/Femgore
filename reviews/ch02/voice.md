# Voice review: ch02

- **Verdict:** REVISE (light; line-level only, the voice is right)

## Findings

1. **"The recon had become an opportunity."** (l.31): stock thriller language, and it could sit in any novel. It breaks 02 (exact, dry, alive) and 12 (the test). The line before it, "She was here tonight, unscheduled, and so was I.", already lands the Turn. Fix: cut the line. If a closing beat is wanted, give one in her register, e.g. a finding: "Recon, revised."

2. **"which was, I had begun to notice, the voice I use for everything I mean"** (l.49): she is explaining the device. 08 wants the reader to *recognise* the syllabus voice as hers by Chapter 2, and this clause recognises it for them. That breaks 01 (address and narration never explain) and 08. Fix: cut the clause. End the sentence at "in the voice I use for the dictaphone." The graded block that follows makes the point without help.

3. **"I was invisible to these people. It was my whole advantage"** (l.55): the narrator states the book's theme ("the original injury, the same fact facing two ways") outright. That is drift toward explanation (02; 01, never to explain). The body already carries it: cold air on the forearms, then the coin at the back of the tongue (06, taste of the original injury). Fix: cut "I was invisible… I felt both." Go straight from "She registered nothing." to "The first…", rewritten as "It felt like cold air on my forearms. Then it came up at the back of my tongue like a coin held there too long, and I didn't swallow it."

4. **"It is the door everyone walks through, because everyone already has a lanyard."** (l.9): this restates the two sentences before it, the logic goes in a circle, and it uses up "the door everyone walks through", which l.65 uses again for the real payoff. It breaks 02 (exact). Fix: cut this sentence and keep the phrase only at l.65, where the cardboard in the strike earns it.

5. **"He was the only person in that building who checked."** (l.87): together with "the way nobody had all night" in the line before and "He looked at me as though I was there." (l.93), that is three statements of one point. The third copy explains, and the subtext turns into text (02). Fix: keep "the way nobody had all night" and the l.93 grade. Cut l.87's second sentence.

6. **"It was the only A I gave all night."** (l.93): she gave Diane three distinctions at l.49, so the reader hits a scoring contradiction in a voice whose whole claim is exactness (02: scrupulously reliable about everything but motive). Fix: "It was the only pass I gave all night." (Diane failed.) Alternatively, make the scale explicit.

7. **"until the thing that had got me off the kitchen stool"** (l.5): this is vague where she is exact by rule. Ch01 named the thing as a finding (the cold in the forearms, the jaw). "Walked… walked down" also repeats. Fix: name it clinically, e.g. "until the cold that had started in my forearms had been walked down into something I could use."
