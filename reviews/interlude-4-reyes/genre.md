This passes the genre lens. It has no violence, so the forensic gore is right-sized: Brandt is "opened... and closed again, roughly", which the reader of Ch 17 knows was tenderness. Appetite is noticed by proxy ("might have called what she felt pleasure"), dry humour ("promptly", "described them for her", the gifted hour) carries the page, and Iris, seen as "someone who had once been graded and had kept the paper", is harder to dismiss (bible/strategy/02; bible/style/12).
