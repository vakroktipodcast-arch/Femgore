This breaks style/10 point 8 (first sentence load-bearing). Fix: open on the object that carries the dread, for example the two photocopies under the lamp, with the Halloween or empty-floor note folded in second.

2. **The mid-piece reveal competes with the Turn.** "The tenth line read *VALE, I.*" stands alone as its own paragraph, and then "Vale was tenth." restates it two paragraphs later. The Turn is the booking, not the discovery (outline Turn; strategy/03 "carry their own single Turn"). Fix: keep the isolated line, but cut the opening of the last paragraph to the booking ("Reyes booked Vale for Wednesday at ten...") so the name is not announced twice.

3. **The Snapper image is summarised.** In "She filled the squares in her own hand... eight of them, the ninth noted and passed over", the beat sheet says "the image goes in the penultimate lines". Here it is a tally, not something seen or heard. Fix: give the pen a sound and a single concrete picture of block capitals filling the squares. The beat's sensory lead wants "the scratch of pen in a paper calendar's square". Do not invent surnames, because they are not in the ledger.

4. **Check before sign-off.** The draft says "the hotel" for the Election Eve detail, while the beat sheet and interlude-5 say "venue". Confirm against the ledger. This is a continuity point, not a Dent one.
