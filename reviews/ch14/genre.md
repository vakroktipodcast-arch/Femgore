- **Verdict:** REVISE (light). The appetite and body carry a chapter with no violence, and the arc stage is right. Three small fixes would make the denial cost her something.

- **Findings:**

1. **Mara's sandwich does not tempt, so refusing it costs nothing.** Location: "an egg-and-cress sandwich she'd had in the fridge since eight". This chapter's appetite is appetite *denied*. Denial only reads as hunger if the food is on the page with the same precision as tissue (style/05, Eating; strategy/10, Act II). Here the sandwich is only listed: it has no smell or texture. Fix: give it one sensory clause before "No, thank you, Mara." Sulphur of the egg, the cress, the bread sweating in clingfilm across the basin. The reader should smell what she turns down.

2. **The egg is reported but never tasted.** Location: "one egg, boiled, eaten out of the cup with a teaspoon". The style rule is "named, cooked, tasted" (style/05). The tap water gets a taste ("flat and mineral"). The egg, which is the one thing she ate, does not. Fix: add one taste or texture note (yolk set or soft, the salt or no salt) so the "column" of food is exact and the rest of it is visibly empty.

3. **Diane's death is recalled as logistics, with no trace of the appetite.** Location: "packed them into Diane's mouth so that the footers would be there". This is Act II, appetite as identity. A recalled kill should carry one physical flicker of the pleasure, in the same line as the fact (style/04, points 3 and 13; strategy/10, "she stops pretending"). As written it reads like filing. That makes her easier to file as a strategist (style/12). Fix: add one sensory half-clause about the act, such as the paper going soft against the tongue or the jaw's give, said as a finding. Keep it to a single beat. The paragraph's job is the dread of being followed, and that should stay.

- **What works:**
  - The Mingus, the humming and the reduced appetite as displaced hunger: "Appetite reduced, onset Thursday... Patient reports her mood as good." This is the body knowing before the plan does, and she reports it without apology or moralising. Keep it.
  - Brandt's three password attempts read through his hands ("the best thing about him"), and the refused-sympathy "warm displacement" with her hand going to the wound. This is the Act II flirtation, kept on the page as appetite noticed rather than appetite named.
  - The morgue case follows the bible's sensory order: smell first (bag, radio dust, biscuit tin), then sound (fans), then weight, cold and texture (the 480 g heart). The jokes are about bodies: the rejected kidney, stalling as "the one bodily function a law firm never loses". Don't trim these for pace.
