# Dent review: Chapter 14

- **Verdict:** REVISE. The structure and all three Turns are sound, but the chapter runs about a third over length and Scene 3 is mostly summary.

**Turn check**
- Scene 1: lands at "His hands stopped on the trackpad…", followed by the refused sympathy and the hand on the nylon. It is an event, not a summary.
- Scene 2: lands at "I went back to the letter and read it a second time…", then "There was no page for Reyes." The Ch 12 distinction ("a thing I did… a thing I was in") is made in the text.
- Scene 3: lands at "What is that?" / "Mingus," I said. It is an event and an admission, with no regret narrated.
- Snapper: "At twenty to ten that night my phone lit up…", ending on "I reached out and stopped it there myself." It is an image, not a cliffhanger.

**Findings**

1. **Length (major).** By my paragraph-by-paragraph estimate, the chapter is about 4,100–4,300 words. The target is about 3,150 and the scenes should each be about 1,050; I estimate about 1,400–1,450 each. This breaks style/10 point 1 ("nothing else may [run long]") and strategy/03 ("roughly 1,000–1,100"). Confirm the count with `scripts/lint.py`. Fix: cut about 1,000 words. The places to cut:
   - S1: merge the "room had noticed" and "Diane would have managed it" paragraphs, and trim the "I have read the file" paragraph. Keep the hands paragraph and the Turn.
   - S2: cut the car-smell paragraph to two sentences, and drop either the "plumbing" joke or the "through the drains" joke, since they say the same thing. Shorten the LC-0311 paragraph by about half.
   - S3: see finding 2.

2. **Scene 3 is summary, not scene (major).** Roughly the first 700 words ("I did arithmetic" through "Noted. Unexplained. Not discarded.") are narrated exposition, and the Turn arrives only at the end. It breaks style/10 point 3, because the Turn is not earned dramatically. Fix: compress the arithmetic and the leave booking to about 250 words, and fold the eating ledger into about 100. The ledger has two further problems. "Thursday… one egg" is then repeated by "On Thursday I ate an egg. I'm telling you about the egg…" (cut one of the two), and the clinical-note paragraph partly names the state, which is close to the "don't name it" line in the beat sheet. Give the freed words to Mara at the sink and the humming.

3. **Scene 3 Turn is under-weighted (moderate).** The "slip as danger" has no beat. The sentence "The fans had been keeping it for me" is good, but nothing in the narration shows Iris's blind spot about handing the title to the woman who wrote "practised". Beat 10 asks the reader to feel it while Iris doesn't. Fix: keep Mara's one-line question and Iris's answer, and add a single physical detail, such as Mara's pen stopping or Iris's hands still on the scale. Add no commentary.

4. **Scene 1 opening is history before action (minor).** The first page is 7:20 a.m. backstory (Monday's accusation, the ward simile, Diane, the 9:14 exit), and the 7:42 huddle only arrives later. Iris is in the trouble (watching Brandt not sit), so the rule is met, but the Scene 1 trouble is told rather than seen. It breaks style/10 point 2 only weakly. Fix: when trimming per finding 1, move the 7:42 huddle up so the live action starts within about 250 words.

5. **Snapper over-explains (minor).** "It would have gone straight on into the other one" explains the image after "A phone has no sides". The beat sheet says "Do not explain it." Fix: cut that sentence, so the chapter ends "A phone has no sides. I reached out and stopped it there myself."

6. **Pressure and competence check (no issue).** Brandt and the wall-off are the pressure in S1, Reyes in S2, and Reyes and Brandt in S3 (the moved timeline, via the "letter reaching him before I did" line), with appetite in all three. No luck or rescue appears. The plumbing that still delivers counsel's mail is her own Ch 11 tap, so it is earned. The IT man's door is Brandt's, not hers.

**What works**
- The S1 Turn: the three-keystroke password and "To hear it not answer", then the refused sympathy and the hand going to the nylon.
- The S2 close: the reread in reverse order, "no page for Reyes", and the half-sentence distinction from Ch 12.
- The Snapper: the unreturned link, the stiff half-toast, and the phone with no sides. Only the explaining line should go.
