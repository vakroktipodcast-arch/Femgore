## Findings

1. **S1, 7:20 a.m. opening, "I was watching Cam Brandt not sit down."** The prose puts Brandt in the room at 7:20. It then has the huddle start "without him" at 7:42 and has him arrive outside the glass and be refused at 7:51. Holt takes his seat at 7:42 and Brandt logs in standing. Rules broken: ledger Timeline, Thu 8 Oct (huddle 7:42, card fails 7:51), and the beat sheet's order of events. Fix: make the 7:20 line habitual or anticipatory. For example, "I was waiting to watch Cam Brandt not sit down", or "I was watching the room for Cam Brandt."

2. **S1 Turn, "performed by the man who wrote it".** The sentence is ambiguous, and both readings cause trouble. If "the man" is Holt, it contradicts Ch 9 and the ledger, where the Holt dossier is Iris's own compilation. If it means Brandt, it carries knowledge only Interlude 3 gives ("Cam had written that exit for other men"). Fix: name Holt as the performer. For example, "a procedure I had only ever read in a dossier, performed now by the man it described, by the book, without his ever being seen to hold the book."

3. **S2, "I took the tablet out of my bag and opened the tap's summary".** The beat sheet puts the tap digest on her phone. Ledger Objects says the tablet was ruled clean (author, 14 Nov): "used only for watching (the debate, the feeds)" and no accounts. Ch 15 has her phone buzz at 4:41 for the heartbeat alert. Reading a mail digest and searching "E. Reyes" on the tablet strains that ruling and splits the tap's device between Ch 14 and Ch 15. Fix: either use the phone here, as the beat does, or add a half-clause saying the tablet carries only the relay and the feeds. Then log the tap digest on the tablet as a pending fact. (The later "Out of habit I opened the War Room" suits the tablet.)

4. **S3, "I promised to tell you everything I put in my mouth".** Ch 1 establishes only "I tell you the facts about lunch". No promise is on the page. Fix: "because I tell you the facts about lunch, and nothing is also a menu."

5. **S3, "Friday so far: water".** The paragraph follows the 9:30 booking, so the vantage is mid-morning Friday. The chapter then runs on to Friday lunch (the sandwich refused), supper and the toast, with no further entry in the "column" she has just promised. Fix: either keep "so far" and let the Snapper stand as the Friday evening entry, or add one dry line at the Snapper. The stiff toast already does some of this work. Optional.

6. **S1, "In Brandt's chair" and "His chair had somebody in it".** The beat sheet allows one literal chair ("A literal chair is allowed once"), to keep clear of the Ch 17 anchor's furniture image ("Your furniture gets moved"). The prose uses it twice, a few lines apart. Fix: keep the 7:42 line and change the 7:51 one to "Somebody was sitting at his place, so he logged in standing."
