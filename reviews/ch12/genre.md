# ch12 — Genre contract

- **Verdict:** REVISE. Line-level only. The appetite, the register and the body are all right for this chapter; three small fixes to the kill and the meal.

**Findings**

1. "the spray came up warm ... under the jets the jugular's liver-coloured welling" comes before "Only then did I look." This breaks style/04 point 1 (sight last; she looks after she knows). The jets and the liver colour are things she sees, but the next paragraph claims she hasn't looked yet. Fix: make the jets felt, not seen, e.g. "in pulses against my face, one to each beat of his heart". Move "liver-coloured welling" into the "Only then did I look" paragraph so the looking pays off.

2. The neck cut has no smell. This breaks style/04 point 1 (smell first; she is a pathologist). The scene opener "nothing for a while that I had time to smell" covers the scuffle well, but the kill is the slow moment where she does have time, and the order starts with texture. Fix: put one smell clause before "Under the fans": opened blood, iron in the air before it reaches her mouth, cut through his menthol and the cologne from the bay. Then the taste of it in her mouth lands as a second sense, not the first.

3. "Haemorrhage wants iron and protein replaced, and I replaced them." This breaks strategy/10 (Act II: she stops pretending; the physiology alibi belongs to Act I). Read straight, the line re-files the hunger as medicine just after she has said "I liked it". Fix: keep the line but let her hear it as an alibi, e.g. "...and I replaced them, which is what I would have said in August." Or cut it. "I had known since the floor" already does the honest work.

**What works**

- The layer-by-layer stab ending in "*I'll live,* ... and I was delighted." Rendered, anatomical, pleasure in the same line. This is the chapter's register exactly.
- "I liked it. That is the whole finding." / "wanted to keep looking." / "for no reason except that I could." She enjoys it and never apologises. This is what makes the reader unable to look away.
- Scene 3's body work. The suturing sentence with pain and pleasure in one breath, steady hands, the fingertips going to the dressing unbidden, the stitches felt during sex. Keep the length and the run-on of the suture passage.
