**Findings**

1. **Length.** By my hand count (no wc tool was available), S1 is about 1,250 words, S2 about 1,280 and S3 about 1,400. That makes about 3,900 against a target of ~3,150. This breaks style/10 point 1 ("nothing else may" run long except Ch 17) and strategy/03 ("roughly 1,000–1,100" per scene). Fix: confirm the count with `scripts/lint.py`, then trim about 700 words. Candidates:
   - S1: the "stood up" paragraph ("I had thought he would come himself…") and the second paragraph's hall description.
   - S3: the saline and brine aside, the kit inventory, and the long Mara paragraph. Keep the stitching sentence whole.
   - S2: the paragraph that opens "Smell first."

2. **S2 Turn contradicted by a motive.** "He was the man who had said my name." The Turn, per the outline, is "the first thing I did for no reason but that I could." Giving her a reason (he used her name) lets the reader read the kill as retaliation, and the book's central admission goes soft. Strategy/03 and the outline Turn both need the kill to be reasonless. Fix: delete that sentence. If the name must be present, have her note afterward that it was not the reason.

3. **S2 Turn is telegraphed and restated.**
   - "It was the last cut I planned that night" tells the reader the kill is coming and that it is unplanned.
   - "He was gone in under a minute" is immediately followed by "He was dead on the warehouse floor," so the Turn's first sentence repeats the paragraph above it.
   - Fix: cut "the last cut I planned that night" from the wrist paragraph. Open the Turn paragraph on "There was no page for him," or merge it into the preceding paragraph, so the fans-didn't-notice beat is the last event.

4. **S3 Turn is pre-empted.** "I woke rested. I am reporting it." The second half of the Turn ("I slept better than I had in years") is already delivered two lines earlier, so the sink paragraph only repeats it. Beat 9 says to report it as a finding, but beat 10 puts the pairing at the Turn. Fix: at the waking, give only the hours and "deep as a well." Hold "rested" and "better than in years" for the sink, where it lands against the failed grade.

5. **Brandt is thin in S3.** One paragraph ("I gave the job to the Architect") carries the act's pressure, and after that the scene is domestic. The strangers, the sex and the sleep are appetite, not Brandt. The beat sheet wanted the stranger's soft fingertips to carry it, and that does not come through. Dent checklist point 4 asks for the pressure in every scene. Fix: one clause tying the toothbrush ("because it had me on it") to Brandt's reach, or tie the hand-check explicitly to being hunted by a man whose hands were not soft. Do not over-explain.

6. **Luck and convenience, minor.** The hero rule holds in the fight. The "pulled thrust" is asserted as "I had known he would", and the brief was read off the kit, so it is earned. Two spots lean on assumption:
   - "Nobody was coming" is asserted, not shown. Give one clause of evidence, such as the van, the empty bay or the dinner.
   - "He would clean it better than I could" depends entirely on Brandt behaving as she predicts. This is acceptable because she names it as a bet, but do not let any later chapter treat it as fact.

7. **Last-sentence and first-sentence weight.**
   - The last sentence ("I threw it away and went to work") carries weight. Keep it.
   - The first sentence stacks three "and" clauses and ends on the clock time, a "little after ten on a Friday", which is low-stakes. The task-in is correct, but the sentence's weight is in its middle. Fix: end it on the card or the twelve metres, and move "a Friday" into the next sentence, where it pays off.
