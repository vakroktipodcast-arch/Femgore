# ch12 — Dent structure review

- **Verdict:** REVISE. The structure is sound: three scenes, all three Turns land, and the Snapper is clean. But the chapter is about 10% over length (3,468 words against ~3,150) and Scene 3 carries most of the excess.

## Turn check

- **S1:** lands at "And then he said my name. Not Dr. Vale…". It is an event, and the scene ends on "…across the city from his dinner."
- **S2:** lands at "There was no page for him. I had not planned him." It is an admission, preceded by "wanted to keep looking". The scene ends on "…for no reason except that I could."
- **S3:** lands at "I had a mouthful of foam when I reached for his grade… there was nothing there." The beat "I had slept better than I had in years" closes that paragraph. The Snapper follows in its own paragraph. Both the grade failing and the sleep are on the page, not summarised.

## Findings

1. **Length.** The chapter is 3,468 words, about 318 over target. `bible/style/10-chapter-architecture-the-dent-checklist.md` point 1 says "nothing else may" run long except Ch 17. By my estimate the split is S1 about 900, S2 about 1,200 and S3 about 1,400 against ~1,050 each. The beats allow S2 ~1,150 "at S3's expense", but S3 was not shortened. Fix: cut about 300 from S3 and about 50 from S2. In S3, trim the saline and chicken joke, the one-sentence suture catalogue ("the forceps' teeth biting the skin edge, which is my skin edge and has opinions…"), the cleanup reasoning paragraph ("My blood was on his floor…") and the tartare ingredient list. Keep the needle, Mara's two lines and the stranger's fingertips. In S2, cut one of the two post-fight sensory sentences in the neck-stroke paragraph.

2. **Brandt is thin in S2, and the kill is not tied to his brief.** The beat sheet's line "He is the man who said her name" was dropped from the draft. Brandt reaches S2 only through "somebody had told him not to kill me" and "the brief gone out of him". `bible/strategy/03-*.md` ("Menace in every quarter") and `bible/style/10-*.md` point 4 ask for the pressure to be felt. The beat's own idea of the brief as "the rule she breaks, on his floor" is not made explicit. Fix: add one clause at "Then I opened the side of his neck" so the kill is aimed at the man who spoke her name. For example, "He was the one who had said my name" or "Brandt had said don't kill me; I was not going to be told."

3. **Luck risk on the wound.** "I guessed which layer it had stopped in. At home I found I was right" reads as a guess that happened to pay off. That is the nearest thing in the chapter to luck, against the hero rule in `bible/strategy/03-*.md` ("No luck, no rescue") and `bible/style/10-*.md` point 5. Fix: let her read the layer from the felt stop ("the bite of transversus, the blade stopped dead against it") and say she was sure. Alternatively, add a clause tying it to the pulled thrust: "he was holding back; the depth was his choice, and I read it." Keep "I knew he would" in the preceding line, since that is the competence.

4. **The S3 cleanup rests on a bet about Brandt's behaviour.** She leaves a living witness tied up, her blood on the floor and the body in place, on the reasoning "He would clean it better than I could." The reasoning is on the page and is hers, so it is not luck. But it is the longest paragraph in the exit, and it is where the S3 cuts in finding 1 should come from. Fix: compress to two sentences. Keep "a man who could not let anything have happened on it" and "I gave the job to the Architect", because that is the pressure and the competence in one line.

5. **First sentence is functional more than weighted.** "It was a little after ten, the side door to the loading bay was twelve metres ahead of me…" puts her in the task inside the first page, which passes. But it is a three-clause inventory that ends on the card, and the voice and appetite arrive only in sentence two. This is minor. Fix, if the reviser wants more load: lead with the Friday-and-fork joke, and move the metres and the card into sentence two. The last sentence ("I threw it away and went to work.") is load-bearing and should not change.

## What works

- The S1 Turn, kept as a bare event: "And then he said my name."
- The S3 sequence of Turn then Snapper: the grade fails, the sleep admission follows, then the needle "parallel to the tap" and the toothbrush. It closes on the morning, not on a cliffhanger.
- The S2 beat "I held it for one breath." followed by the stroke. It makes the kill a choice, so the Turn is earned, not accidental.
