# Continuity review: ch21 (The Infiltration)

- **Verdict:** REVISE. Small, local fixes only. The clock, the kit, the hand-off to ch22 and the Interlude 4 firewall hold. One hard contradiction with ch20 and one ledger/beat conflict need fixing.

## Findings (most serious first)

1. **Phone location contradicts ch20.** S1: "My own phone was at home on the counter, switched off." Ch20's last scene has her write at the desk with the phone face-down at her elbow, then "switched the phone off and left it face-down where it was", at the writing desk. The ledger (People, Iris) only says it stays at the flat, switched off. Fix: "on the desk, face-down and switched off", or just "at home, switched off". The counter is not established and is the wrong room.

2. **Pale nitrile carried through screening conflicts with the ledger and ch20.** S1: "Over the left outer pocket, where the pale nitrile was folded small". Ledger, Objects, Election Eve staging: "nothing of the kit is on her at screening", and "the closet yields … pale and black nitrile". Ch20 S3: "nothing would pass the magnetometer but me, a badge, a bag, and a page". The ch20 lay-out list is lanyard, bag, run-of-show, with no gloves. The ch21 beat sheet logs the pocketed pair as a New fact, but the ledger was never amended. The prose is internally consistent: out of the pocket in the closet, back in, then on again in the passage. Two options. (a) Ask the author to ratify it and add it to the ledger (Objects, Election Eve gloves), with a ch20 clause such as "gloves in the pocket, the kind half the building wore". (b) Keep the pale pair in the closet kit. That breaks the ledger's own sequence, where gloves go on before the box is set down, so (a) is the better fix. Do not leave it as an unrecorded fact.

3. **Ch21 cites a ch20 act that is not on the ch20 page.** S3: "I had written that figure down on Friday night and not used it". The twelve seconds and the "eight more paces" (stair door to sticker) are not in manuscript/ch20.md. It gives only "about ten paces from the green-room door" and writes down the sixty-second minute. The beat sheet for ch20 says she writes the twelve seconds down, but the draft dropped it. Fix: add "and the passage behind it eight paces more, twelve seconds door to sticker, which I wrote down" to the ch20 S1 floor-plan paragraph, or soften ch21 to "I had worked that figure out on Friday night".

4. **Dock layout conflicts with the ledger.** S1: "a line of staff and vendors that ran from the dock office along the wall under the shutter". Ledger, Places (dock-level layout, fixed): the booth and the Stair C fire door are at one end, about 40 m of corridor lie between, and the bay, shutter and gate are at the other end. The booth cannot see the shutter or gate. A line running from the booth to under the shutter is 40 m long and joins the two ends. Fix: put the line "along the wall under the shutter" and leave the dock office out, or run it "from the bay doors along the corridor wall". Note also that ch20's Sunday entry has Iris pass the booth first and then the corridor, the reverse of this layout. That is ch20's problem, but fix it at the same time if either chapter is touched.

5. **"Warmest room … for three weeks" is a flat claim that her own days contradict.** S2: "The closet was the warmest room I had been in for three weeks". She works daily in the ME office and walked the heated Calloway on Sunday. Only the flat has been unheated since Wed 14 Oct (19 days). It also sits against ch20's "only warm thing in the flat" printer line. Fix: "the warmest room I had stood in all day", or "warmer than my own flat had been for three weeks".
