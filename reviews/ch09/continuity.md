# Continuity review: ch09

**Verdict:** REVISE

## Findings

1. **Drawer layers skipped (S1 and S3).** S1 lists the drawer top to bottom: dossiers, photographs, signed agreement, then the sleeve. It then says "The sleeve lies under all three, so a man who wants the pages has to lift the dossiers as a block". S3 does the same thing: Iris lifts only the dossiers, slides the sleeve out, and lowers the dossiers "onto it". This breaks ledger Objects, "Drawer order, top to bottom (Ch 1)", where the photographs and the NDA sit between the dossiers and the sleeve. The beat sheet and ledger line 109 both repeat the shortcut, but the prose is where it shows. Fix: make the block everything above the sleeve ("the dossiers, the photographs and my agreement, lifted together"). Do the same in S3. Keep the quarter-inch on the top folder.

2. **"put the file back first" contradicts "on top" (S1).** The line is "It had been on top since Monday night… put the file back first because it was the one I was working." If his file went back first, anything laid over it would bury it. Ch 8 says Diane's dossier was lifted off the stack and set aside before his was opened, so Diane's may be the one that went back on top. That is an ambiguity the rest of S1 depends on, because "a man… reads his own first" needs his file on top. Fix: "put the file back last, on top, because it was the one I was working." Ch 8's "lives on top" wording is not to be touched.

3. **Fear stated, then denied (S1 against S3).** S1 says the fridge check "told me… exactly what I was afraid of". S3 says "I noticed I was not afraid." The ledger has no rule on this, but the unit contradicts itself, and the S3 discovery is its Turn-adjacent beat. Fix: in S1 change it to "exactly what I should have been afraid of", or to "what a body like mine expects". Then S3's absence reads as a finding and not a reversal.

4. **Supper timeline stretches (S2).** She is home at about 6:30 and cooks first. She is still eating ("I kept eating") when Holt enters at 10:30, and the last eggs are "cool and firm in the pan" near midnight. Ledger Timeline: home about 6:30, Holt about 10:30, phone scroll about 11:50. Nothing in the ledger says when she cooked. Fix: one clause that she plates in stages, or that the scrub-back and the cooking ran later than the bag's arrival. For example, "I ate in instalments, the pan on the back ring."

5. **Pencil note recased (S1).** The note appears as "*The only one who'll understand it.*" with a capital T. Ch 8 and the ledger give it in lower case ("*the only one who'll understand it.*", ledger People, Brandt, Dossier). Fix: lower-case T.
