# Voice review: ch09

- **Verdict:** REVISE

## Findings

1. **Line 57: "We were the same kind of alone."** This line could be in any thriller. It turns her relationship to Brandt into a tagline, and it explains a link the scene has already shown through the three phone calls. Breaks 02 (exact, dry) and the brief's test of whether a sentence could appear in another novel. Fix: cut it. End the paragraph on "with me." If a closing beat is needed, make it a finding or a grade, e.g. "Two subjects, one cohort."

2. **Line 17: "Pathologists know that the cleanest way into a body is through its fridge."** The whole paragraph drifts into explanation. She interprets her own instinct ("my body had known it before I did… exactly what I was afraid of") where the anchors would only report it. The aphorism means nothing anatomically. "I said I would tell you which was the tell" points back to a promise she never made, and it contradicts line 13 ("Here is the tell."), which made the dossier the tell. Naming fear here also takes the force out of line 75 ("I noticed I was not afraid"). Breaks 01 (direct address never to explain) and 02 (reliable about everything except why). Fix: cut lines 17's first four sentences. Change line 13 to "Here is the evidence." Leave the reader to put the fridge together with the threat, keeping at most "The fridge had told me he had not come to kill me. Not today."

3. **Line 71: "is hunting the student."** This spoken line is genre melodrama. "Hunting" is thriller wording, not syllabus wording, and the narration says she is using the syllabus voice. Breaks 02 (syllabus voice is formal and evaluative) and 07 (spoken Iris is clipped, not dramatic). Fix: put it in grading terms, e.g. "The Architect," I said… "has sat the paper early."

4. **Line 3: "like a cellar somebody keeps well"**. This uses up the anchor image early. Ch17 introduces vetiver as "smells of a good cellar" and treats it as a discovery. If it appears here first, the set piece will read as an echo. Fix: keep "dry, green, rooty, expensive" and give it a different comparison here (a florist's back room, a new cricket bat), or leave it unnamed. Ch17 should get the cellar first.

5. **Lines 35–37: "decide three times not to do something" / "Three phone calls a man could have made"**. The same beat is stated twice in a row. Fix: cut "Three phone calls a man could have made, and" so line 37 opens "I watched him not make each of them, which is…"

6. **Line 39: "To give them a name you would have to tell them…"** In the first call, the free-indirect "you" turns into a plot briefing (shredded paper, three days, seven weeks out). The second and third calls are each about three lines. This one is twice as long, and its "you" slides toward addressing the reader in order to explain. Breaks 01 (direct address never to explain). Fix: cut it down to the length of the other two calls, e.g. "You can't call them. A name means the paper, and the campaign never made the paper. Correct."

7. **Line 73: "and reading the marking scheme. Most students wait for the exam."** The joke is set up twice before the punchline, and "marking scheme" appears twice in three sentences. Fix: cut "and reading the marking scheme" from the first sentence and cut "Most students wait for the exam." entirely. Let "broken into the staff room" carry the setup.

8. **Line 31: "until it was the colour of putty"**. "The colour of putty" already appears in both ch01 (the liver) and ch17 (Brandt's lips). A third use makes the anchor phrase a tic. Breaks 06 (colour named precisely, with variety). Fix: use a fresh colour for anchovy butter, such as "the colour of a healing bruise" or "pale salmon gone to clay".

9. **Line 77: "I am reporting that finding. I am not going to name it."** This copies the formula in ch17 ("I am reporting that. I am not going to explain it."), which is placed at that chapter's peak. Spending it in ch09 weakens it there. Fix: end on the spleen finding and drop the closing disclaimer, or swap in a plain note such as "Interest, in the place where fear should have been. Noted."

## What works
- The grading of Brandt's three non-calls ("Correct." / "Correct again." / "Full marks.") is the syllabus voice used in control, as 02 requires. Keep the structure.
- "a press release with candles", "I own a fruit bowl", "red on paper nobody was wearing". The jokes come at the right rate and in the right register. Don't trim them.
- The motif callbacks: cold "on the volar surface" as on the night of the debate, the "long wooden sigh" of the drawer, "I put it down to the tea". They keep the narrator continuous with ch01 and ch17.
