- **Verdict:** REVISE

- **Findings:**

1. "I enjoyed it. I want to put that plainly." (Scene 2, para beginning "I enjoyed it"). Breaks `strategy/10`, Act I: "She enjoys … and does not call it enjoyment". This is the Act II "stops pretending" move, made a chapter early. It also weakens Ch 12, where the pleasure is first "described as such". The outline only asks her to *enjoy* being understood, not to announce it. Fix: cut both sentences. Open the paragraph on "I had been read correctly, from across a city…". Let a clinical report do the work, for example one physiological line filed as a grade: "Pulse up. Full marks to both of us."

2. Too many disclaimers about her own pleasure. "a fact about supper", "I want you to know that was a choice", "that is all I am going to say", "I want to put that plainly" and "I am not going to name it" add up to five in one chapter. They break `strategy/10` (Act I appetite is "present and unnamed") and `style/12`. Each one tells the reader how to read her, which makes her easier to file away as a case study. Fix: keep "I am not going to name it" (spleen) and "I want you to know that was a choice" (Holt, cold). Cut the tail after "I was very happy" and the tail of the second-plate paragraph (see 3).

3. "It was not a meal after anything. Nobody had died today." Breaks `style/09` (the meal after belongs to 7, 12, 17, 27) and `style/04` ("narrates it as physiology, and the reader stops believing her"). Here she names the motif and argues against it, so the reader is told rather than left to doubt her. Fix: cut those two sentences and "and that is all I am going to say". End the paragraph on "It was more than I had meant to eat." The second plate of cold eggs, eaten while watching Brandt, already makes the point.

4. "the cleanest way into a body is through its fridge". The food-as-vulnerability tell is the chapter's best appetite idea, but this line hides it. The reader cannot easily see that she checked for poison. It then clashes with "I noticed I was not afraid", because the fear was never shown on the page (`style/05`: the body as her continuous report). Fix: add a plain clause, e.g. "he had not touched anything I was going to put in my mouth". Report the fear as something her body did and she found out afterwards ("my body had been afraid before I had"), so the later spleen passage reads as a change in the finding, not a contradiction.

- **What works:**
  - The entry is smell first and anatomical ("like a joint going back into its socket"; the cellar smell "at the height of a standing man's chest"). It follows the `style/04` sensory order with no violence in the chapter. Keep it.
  - The humour carries this chapter's page: "I checked the butter… I know where the jewellery is", the vigil as "a press release with candles", the safe-deposit box against the fruit bowl, "a cheat and a scholar".
  - The appetite is correctly unnamed and only noticed: the spleen passage "packed in tight and dense"; "something warm… I put it down to the tea" (first warmth against the Act I cold); red ink and "paper nobody was wearing". Do not make any of these more explicit.
