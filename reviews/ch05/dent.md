## Findings

1. **S1 is slow to its grab.** Roughly 900 words of smell, dark adaptation, inventory and route-grading pass before the ring. The Turn is strong, but strategy/03 says "Scene 1 grabs". The paragraph beginning "I had her numbers" and the heron paragraph could each lose a sentence. Suggested cuts: "I would enter it in the logbook later. I keep the logbook honestly." and one of the three smell sentences in paragraph 1. Optional.
2. **Snapper phrase "her daughter's last words" is narratorial foreknowledge** (style/10 pt 8, "last sentence load-bearing"). It is allowed for retrospective first person, and the beat sheet flags the author's fallback ("her daughter sealed inside it"). Author's call; it is not a structural fault.
3. **S2 pressure is the lightest of the three.** The campaign's certainty appears only as the notification count. Optional fix: one clause in the notification paragraph saying what the staff messages assume, for example that she is reachable and on schedule. Do not add more than that, or the Olivia line loses the top spot.
