# ch05 — Forensic and procedural accuracy

- **Verdict:** REVISE

- **Findings:**

1. **The planted phone is lost.** Line 35, "had the door open before the tone died". Diane has the prepaid in her hand when Iris takes her, and nothing says where it goes after that. If she drops it on the spur, it lies in the grass outside a shut door for three hours. Ledger (Objects: "Planted prepaid: battery out, taken"; Ch 7 egress bag) needs it inside the station. As written, the chapter gets there by luck. Fix: one clause in S2 para 2 or 3. Either it comes over the threshold in her fist and Iris prises it out, or Iris's left hand takes it as she clamps the mouth.

2. **The drag doesn't add up.** Line 35–37, "My right hand went over her mouth from behind" / "Backwards over the threshold". One gloved hand over the mouth cannot haul a 70 kg woman (ledger, Diane) backwards from a kneel. The left hand is never used; it only carries the tape. Diane's hands are free until she is face-down, and a panicking adult claws at the hand on her mouth and grabs the door frame. Cast.md says Iris is "not a trained fighter", so the reader needs to see how the leverage works. Fix: add a clause putting her left forearm across Diane's collarbones or under the armpit, tape still on the back of the glove. Give Diane's hands one beat (nails on the nitrile at the wrist, or a grab at the frame that misses) so the speed reads as earned.

3. **Powering off.** Line 51, "I held the button down… It asked me nothing." No current handset powers off from one held button without a slider or menu. On one platform, holding the button calls the voice assistant. A forced restart turns the phone back on. Anyone who has seized a phone will notice. Fix: keep the Turn but make the procedure real. She slides the bar or taps the menu with a gloved knuckle (thin nitrile works on glass), and the text stays visible under the dimmed overlay until the screen goes dark. "The words and the light went out together" can stand.

4. **An abrasion through plastic sheeting.** Line 37, "her left cheekbone met the concrete through the sheeting". Smooth polythene over concrete gives a contusion, not a 2 cm abrasion. The ledger and Ch 8 need an abrasion. Fix: put the grit under the sheeting, or a taped seam or ridge where the cheek lands. Ch 8 already finds pump-station dust on the vinyl, so the grit is in the scene.

5. **"Never once varied" goes against the ledger.** Line 17, "In a hundred and forty-one Sundays she had never once varied it". Ledger (Fixed numbers, Trailmark) has late starts that are "short-cut Sundays" and four slower Sundays. A planner who read 141 tracks would know about both, and the trap depends on Diane passing the post. Fix: "never once left it", or a clause saying that every short cut still passed the post.

6. **The knee isn't on her when the phone buzzes.** Line 39–43, "Then up, and the cord, and the door drawn shut" and then "I felt it through her, in my knee". Iris has stood up to shut the door, and no line puts her back on Diane. Fix: half a sentence that returns her (e.g. "back down, a knee on her") before the buzz.

7. **She reaches across the safe distance.** Line 57–59, "out of reach of a kick" and then "I peeled the tape from her mouth". She sits 1.5 m away and then removes the tape, which means coming back inside the range she has just set. Fix: peel and bag the tape before she sits on the plinth, or say she gets up to do it.

8. **Pathologists don't weigh bladders.** Line 11, "I have weighed the bladders of enough of the dead". Bladders aren't weighed, and the dead in general can't show her cold diuresis. A full bladder is a recognised finding in hypothermia deaths. Fix: "I have drained the bladders of enough people who died of cold". The physiology that follows is correct and should stay.

9. **Cold hands, fast fine work.** Line 9, "settled into the backs of my hands inside both layers of glove". After three hours at 11 °C, dexterity drops, yet the zip-ties and tape still go on in under four seconds. As written, that is luck. Fix: one line in S1 where she keeps her fingers working (flexions, hands under the coverall at the armpits) from eight o'clock. That is how a planner would handle it.

10. **Minor: light bouncing off the page.** Line 55, "my face stayed black". A head-torch on a white page lights the reader's chin and face from below. The real reason Diane sees only a hood is that she came in from daylight a minute earlier and her eyes haven't adjusted. Fix: say so in a clause ("her eyes still full of the morning"), or have the page held low.

- **What works:**
  - The earpiece latency: "Olivia?" is heard once through the air and a fraction later through the earpiece. It is accurate and it is the chapter's best effect.
  - The cold-diuresis reasoning behind the fast, the nose left clear ("I needed her breathing"), and the capture tape folded sticky-to-sticky and bagged all read as someone who has done the job.
  - The timings (8:27 is six minutes inside the window, bench to post in thirteen minutes, ring to capture to the text at about 8:30) match the ledger. Keep them.
