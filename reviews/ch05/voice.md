**Verdict:** REVISE

**Findings:**

1. **"I am going to tell you what it felt like, because nobody else"** (scene 2, para 7). This is nearly word for word the anchor's ch17 line "I would like to report my own findings now, since nobody else was going to". Using it here, in Chapter 5, spends the set piece's move twelve chapters early. The same goes for **"I don't explain that either"** (final scene), which echoes ch17's "I am reporting that. I am not going to explain it." Rule: 01 point 1 says direct address is there to implicate. A borrowed formula reads as pastiche of the anchor, not as the same narrator. Fix: open the paragraph on the finding itself ("It felt good."), with no preamble. Cut "I don't explain that either" and end on "My mouth was no longer dry."

2. **"She had heard the page once, and she had recited it"** (after "there is a process"). This explains the joke. The reader already holds "confused process for protection" from Ch1. Rule: 01 point 1 and 02 point 1 (exact, not explaining). Fix: keep "She led with process." and cut the sentence that follows.

3. **"It was not a negotiation, and it had never been one"** (para after "I want the grade"). This restates the previous paragraph's "Wrong currency" and "She was sure there was a price", so the scene's turn gets explained twice. Rule: 02 point 1 (drift toward explanation). Fix: cut the sentence. "There was nothing she had that I would take" already carries it.

4. **"Somewhere downtown the campaign was asleep, and certain of everything."** (scene 1, heron paragraph). This is a stock thriller cutaway line that could sit in any novel, and Iris has no sensory access to it. Fix: cut it, or tie it to something she can see or hold, such as the sticker or the app.

5. **"and my pulse was in my teeth"** (Snapper). "Pulse in my teeth" is a genre stock image. It also repeats "I could hear my own pulse in that room" from two paragraphs up, so the closing beat is weakened. Rule: 02 point 2 (plain words for appetite, not cliché). The anchors end on a concrete object or a flat fact. Fix: end on the foil brick and the daughter's message, or give the body finding in her register, for example "Pulse, at a guess, a hundred and ten" (but don't take ch17's figure).

6. **Final scene from "I graded the negotiation" to the end.** No joke lands in roughly the last 450 words, and the Latinate/plain collision also drops out there. Rule: 02 point 3 (at least one joke a page in Act I) and 02 point 2. Fix: give one dry anatomical aside to the waiting beat. "The cold had begun to come back into my hands" is the obvious place.

7. **"the smell of a puddle remembered"** and **"waited for a woman to walk into my morning"** (opening paragraph). Both lean purple, and the rest of the smell paragraph is exact and good. Rule: 02 point 1 ("not cold — exact"). Fix: cut "the smell of a puddle remembered". Make the last clause plainer and more clinical, for example "and I waited for her."

8. **"The room went quiet for it."** (end of the long reading paragraph). The room was already established as deaf, so this is generic filler after a strong long sentence. Fix: cut it. End on "It was the voice the page was written in."

**What works:**

- The Latinate/plain jokes in the action. "Popliteal fossa, where the leg has no opinion", "a courtesy to the forensic examiner and none at all to the zygoma" and the rug joke are exactly the anchors' register. Don't touch them.
- The grading voice appears only where she is in control ("Distinction.", "Pass.", "A strong opening. Poorly sourced. Wrong currency.") and is pointedly withheld at "I didn't grade the daughter."
- The appetite is reported through the body and never named: the dry mouth, the oxtail, "My mouth was no longer dry." The ch1 callbacks also work, namely "outside before inside" in "the last time I saw her from the outside" and the door that closes without being slammed.
