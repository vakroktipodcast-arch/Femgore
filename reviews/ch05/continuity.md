# Continuity review: ch05 (Continuity lens)

**Verdict:** REVISE

## Findings

1. **Wrong place for the plaster.** "Since the plaster in the hotel corridor, I check." Ch 2 (manuscript/ch02.md, lines 60-99) puts Desmond's plaster in the overflow suite, a bedroom used as a cupboard, across the corner of a desk, not in a corridor. Ledger, Iris, Ch 2: the tremor is "taking Desmond's plaster". Fix: "Since Desmond's plaster, I check." or "Since the plaster in the supply suite, I check."

2. **"Never once varied" contradicts the Trailmark facts.** "In a hundred and forty-one Sundays she had never once varied it." The ledger (Fixed numbers, Trailmark) says the late starts are "short-cut Sundays or walks begun from the far side of the car park". The Ch 3 prose (line 39) says so too. The ledger (People, Diane) also gives four slower Sundays. Fix: narrow it to what is true and what Iris needs: "never once left the loop", or "never once gone up the spur". The spur is the point, and it is what the later "which she had never once looked up" rests on.

3. **Ankle ties don't add up with Ch 7 and Ch 8.** "Her ankles, a tie and then a band of industrial tape." That reads as one tie round both ankles, so three ties in all (two wrists, one ankle). Ch 7 line 7 bags "the four zip-ties, cut at the bracket and at the ankles". Ch 8 finds furrows at "both ankles" and residue "at each ankle". The ledger (Objects, Diane's restraint) has "zip-ties at wrists and ankles". Also, only two ties are laid out on the sheeting in S1, so the ankle ties have no source. Fix: "Her ankles, a tie on each, then a band of industrial tape round both." Mention in S1 that the ankle ties are in the row or the bag if you want the source shown.

4. **Planted prepaid is never recovered on the page.** Diane picks it up, hears the dead line and is taken; Ch 5 never says where it ends up. Ch 7 line 7 has it in the bag with the battery out ("the prepaid that had rung for her"). It is not a contradiction, but the hand-off is open: it was either dropped on the spur or carried through the door. Fix: one clause in S2 paragraph 2 or 3, for example the phone falling in the grass and being scooped into the bag before the door was drawn. Alternatively, put the retrieval in Ch 7 and leave Ch 5 alone, but then Ch 7 should say where it lay.

5. **Door mechanics don't fit together.** Ch 4 (line 79; Places, pump station) hangs a moving blanket on the inside of the door. S1 watches "through the gap" and S2 later seats "the moving blanket". As written, the blanket would cover the gap Iris watches through, and the rubber wedge is never removed before the door is drawn shut. Fix: in S1, one clause saying the blanket is tied back or hooked aside, and in S2 a clause on the wedge kicked out and the blanket let fall. Ledger, Objects: wedge, cord loop, door drawn shut by the loop.

6. **Sheeting versus concrete.** "met the concrete through the sheeting". Ledger, Places: the floor is swept, sheeted and taped. Ch 6, Ch 8 and the ledger (kneeling on the concrete, "met the concrete") all say concrete. "Through the sheeting" is self-contradictory, and an abrasion from plastic sheeting is the wrong source. Fix: cut "through the sheeting" (and drop "the sheeting underfoot" in S3), or say the sheeting lies only along the wall. The Snapper's "on the concrete" and the S3 "concrete between us" then stand.
