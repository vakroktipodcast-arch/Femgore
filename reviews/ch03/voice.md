# Voice review: ch03

**Verdict:** REVISE

**Findings:**

1. **Direct address that explains (line 3).** "which tells you most of what you need to know about the evening". This breaks 01, point 1: "you" is only for implicating, never for explaining. It is also a stock narrator line. Fix: cut the clause. The unlocked drawer before the shoes already does the work.

2. **She says she won't explain the plan, then explains it (lines 61 and 67).** "The plan, which I'm not going to explain." is followed by a full walkthrough, and then line 67 ("Closed is not the same as kept closed…") explains it a second time. This breaks 01, point 1 and drifts into explanation (02, point 1). Fix: drop "which I'm not going to explain". Cut line 67 down to its body fact, e.g. "A body without air does what it does in a fixed order, and the first thing on the list is that it stops arguing." Let Attempt three carry the rest.

3. **Generic thriller phrasing that could appear in any other novel (lines 45 and 67).** "the kind of building nobody looks at twice", "take the fight out" and "I know to the second where further begins" all fail the any-other-novel test. Fix: replace the building cliché with one exact detail she would actually see (roof material, a padlock, what the sumac has done to the door). Replace "take the fight out" with the physiology: what the intercostals and the hands do, in order. Cut the closing boast; the stopwatch numbers already show it.

4. **The same deduction stated twice (lines 23 to 25).** Line 23 ends on "never alone… other people's diaries". Then "There was a gap… In a hundred and sixty-eight hours, Diane had no unsecured hour. Nobody lives like that. Not even Diane." says the same thing again, and "Nobody lives like that" is a stock line. This is explanation drift (02, point 1). Fix: cut everything before "The hour existed, and I didn't have it."

5. **Morals spelled out for the reader (lines 33 and 41).** "The most dangerous document anywhere on that campaign was my own agreement…" and "The campaign that paid for her driver… could have found that bench by typing her name." Both tell the reader the irony after the scene has already shown it. This is explanation drift. Fix: cut both. If one stays, keep the second and make it a grade ("Campaign security: fail.") so it is in her syllabus voice (02, point 4).

6. **Absence of feeling named outright (line 49).** "I looked, honestly, for whatever a person is supposed to feel… and couldn't find it." This breaks the spirit of 11, final point: show absence through the body, never name it. It also uses up the numb pole of ch25 in Act I. The margin pairing on line 51 already does this better. Fix: cut the sentence and keep the pulse count, the cayenne and the margin note.

7. **Too many phrases borrowed from the anchors (lines 57, 59, 39, 17).** "until it had properly decided to stay", "propped against the fruit bowl", "read them the way I read a body, from the outside in" and "I'll tell you the facts about dinner" are all lifted from ch01. One deliberate callback is voice. Four reads as pastiche of the anchor. Fix: keep the facts-about-dinner callback, which earns its place. Rephrase the other three in fresh terms of her own.

**What works:**

- The kidney paragraph (line 9): "public-lavatory note if I'm honest, and I am", "unipyramidal" and "the body's compliance department". This is the Latinate/plain collision at full strength. Don't trim it.
- The rug rehearsal's jokes and grading ("if a rug has knees, and by then it did", "C for dignity and A for commitment", "losing a wrestling match to soft furnishings"), and the clipped "Phone side. Hand. Weight. Tab. Mouth." Short sentences for action, with the syllabus voice present because she is in control.
- The margin pairing on line 51: "*Affect: nothing identifiable.*" set directly above "*Lamb's kidneys ×6, devilled.*" The reader gets the unreliability without being told.
