# Dent review — ch03, The Re-engineering

- **Verdict:** REVISE. The structure is sound and all three Turns land as events. The chapter runs roughly a third over length, and Scene 1 carries most of the excess.

## Turn check

- **Scene 1:** lands at "There was a gap in the dossier, and when I saw it…", closing on the red margin question *Where does she go to not be reached?* Landed as an event.
- **Scene 2:** lands at "In the margin of the dossier I wrote: *Affect: nothing identifiable.*", followed by the meal line in the same hand. Landed as an event.
- **Scene 3:** lands at "On the video, at half speed, it was three point eight seconds. Under four." and runs through "breathing hard, and I was happy." Landed as an event. The Snapper (capped marker, rug squared) follows with no scene break and is an image, not a cliffhanger.

## Findings

1. **Over length.** By my count (estimated, no word-count tool run) the chapter is about 4,100–4,300 words against the 3,150 target. Scene 1 is about 1,600 against ~1,050, Scene 2 about 1,150, and Scene 3 about 1,400. This breaks `bible/style/10-chapter-architecture-the-dent-checklist.md` point 1 ("nothing else may" run long) and `bible/strategy/03-*.md` point 1 (scenes of ~1,000–1,100). Fix, in order of saving:
   - Scene 1: compress the kidney anatomy paragraph ("Raw kidney smells of what it does for a living…") to about half. Keep the compliance-department joke and the unipyramidal line. Cut the butcher exchange to one sentence or drop it.
   - Scene 1: trim the opening paragraph's master-copy digression ("like a student's notes in a textbook he has started to argue with").
   - Scene 3: cut one of the three rehearsal-setup paragraphs ("I stood it up against the counter edge…" and "The plan, which I'm not going to explain") down to a few lines. Shorten the retired-couple aside.
   - Scene 1 should land near 1,100 and the chapter under about 3,500.

2. **Scene 1 gets to its Turn slowly.** The task is in the first paragraph (the hour, the drawer), which satisfies checklist point 2. But the dossier work that leads to the Turn does not begin until "The grid was a week…", about 500 words in, after the hotel strike and the kidney prep. This breaks `bible/strategy/03-*.md` point 6 ("Scene 1 grabs"). Fix: interleave. Put the first grid fact (the driver at ten to seven) before the kidneys, so the hunt and the cooking run together as the outline's "cooks while she thinks" asks.

3. **Act pressure is thin in Scene 1.** The pressure is the campaign's certainty that nothing is coming (`beats/ch03.md` act-pressure line; `bible/style/10-*.md` point 4). Scene 1 shows a heavily guarded woman. It never shows that nobody is guarding the guard, or that the campaign is complacent. "Which is an alarm system on a lead" is the nearest it gets. Scenes 2 (the terms-and-conditions joke) and 3 (the radio caller) carry it clearly. Fix: add one sentence in the sum paragraph ("The campaign had made her the one person on it who was never alone") that turns on the campaign's belief. For example, nobody had written the threat column of the grid in two years because nobody believed in it.

4. **A convenient door in Scene 2.** "It will have been the one slip of her career, and it came to me." The stray CC reads as luck handed to Iris. This breaks the hero rule in `bible/strategy/03-*.md` point 6 and `bible/style/10-*.md` point 5 ("no unlocked doors she didn't unlock"). The public Trailmark profile is Diane's error and is fine. The CC is the only thing that makes the lookup possible. Fix: show Iris choosing it. She noticed the CC three years ago, judged it a lever, and clipped it deliberately ("I clip the thing I don't yet need"). That makes the find her competence, not an accident.

5. **First sentence is procedural.** "I had the marker uncapped before I sat down…" sets smell and instrument but carries little weight on its own. The real hook, "unlocked the drawer before I took my shoes off, which tells you most of what you need to know about the evening", is buried mid-paragraph. This is a weak fit with `bible/style/10-*.md` point 8 (first sentence load-bearing). Fix: lead with the drawer line, or open on "I wanted an hour." Fold the marker smell in second. The last sentence ("as if nothing had been asked of it") is strong and should stay unchanged.

6. **Minor: the Scene 2 Turn is slightly over-explained.** "I didn't ask myself why the second line belonged on that page. Neither of them got a comment." says twice what the outline needs once. Fix: keep one of the two sentences. Cutting the second ("Neither of them got a comment") lets the meal line sit last, as the Turn.

## What works

- The Scene 2 Turn: the two red margin lines in the same hand, with the meal filed beside the affect.
- The Scene 3 findings paragraph ("Respiratory rate up… Forearms still cold… a personal best"), a straight report that misfiles the pleasure. It keeps the cold in the forearms as the brief requires.
- The Snapper: the capped marker pointing a finger's width from the X, and the rug squared to the boards. It closes on an image with no admission and no cliffhanger.
