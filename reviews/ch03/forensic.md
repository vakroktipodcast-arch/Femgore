1. **Smothering physiology is wrong, and the certainty sounds like luck.** Line 67: "the first thing on the list is that it stops arguing" / "I know to the second where further begins". A conscious adult whose nose and mouth are covered fights *harder* first: air hunger, panic, a surge of strength. Resistance only fades close to loss of consciousness, and when that comes varies a great deal with breath-holding, fitness and adrenaline. That variation is exactly why smothering kills people by accident. A pathologist with 1,400 cases would not say "to the second", and if the capture works on Sunday it will read as luck. This breaks the reviewer brief (no luck; FBI/ME credibility) and `bible/cast.md` (her violence is "research, preparation and patience").
