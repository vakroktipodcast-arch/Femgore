## Findings

1. **T-shirt versus sleeve.** "in the gap between glove and sleeve" breaks `ledger/continuity.md` People, Iris, Ch 3 ("rehearses in a T-shirt and socks"; the prose says the same at "A woman in a T-shirt and socks"). A T-shirt has no sleeve at the wrist, so the abrasion cannot sit there. The 2 cm inside-right-wrist abrasion is ledger-fixed (Fixed numbers, Rug rehearsal). The beat sheet repeats the slip. Fix: "in the gap where the glove cuff rode up" (or "between the glove cuff and the bare skin"). Keep the 2 cm, the inside right wrist and "weeping a little".

2. **"On Sunday" reads as the coming Sunday.** "On Sunday the airway would be shut just long enough" conflicts with the timeline (`Sun 30 Aug` and `Sun 6 Sep` are watches; the kill is Sun 13 Sep). Ch 4 S1 has her only watching on 30 Aug. In the same scene she says "on the day" ("the same roll I would take with me on the day"), so the vocabulary exists. Fix: "On the day the airway would be shut…".

3. **Grid note "beside Tuesday" and the struck "Thursday".** The first-ring answer was seen at the Thursday fundraiser (Ch 2), yet "I wrote it beside Tuesday". Earlier she "ruled one line through Thursday" for a one-off event on a standing week grid, so Thursday is both struck and the place the observation would belong. Rule: ledger Objects, Pell dossier (Ch 3), which fixes the notes but not their placement. Fix: "beside the weekday block" for the phone note, and "ruled one line through the hotel row" (or "through Thursday's entry") for the strike.

4. **Drawer never relocked.** She unlocks the drawer in the first paragraph and puts the zip bag in its back compartment at the end, but never locks it. The ledger treats the drawer as brass-locked, and Ch 18 makes the unlocked state a marked event ("stays unlocked until dusk"). The signed NDA, the three dossiers and the photographs are in it. Fix: one clause in the closing tidy, e.g. "and turned the key". This also fits the "squared" closing.

5. **Roof colour, Ch 3 against Ch 4.** Ch 3: "a square of slate-coloured roof: an old pump station, brick". Ch 4 line 13: "a pump station whose brick roof I could just see". The ledger fixes only "single-storey brick". Fix in Ch 3: "a square of dark roof" (leave the colour to Ch 4). If the reviser prefers to keep slate, Ch 4 needs the matching change. Flag it for that unit's reviewer either way.

6. **Self-grading versus the Ch 19 "first grade of herself".** "I would grade the picture C for dignity and A for commitment" and the "Fail." for attempt one grade Iris and her performance. The ledger says Ch 19 S3's "competent" at the mirror is "her first grade of herself" (People, Iris, grades line; Ch 18 line repeats it). The beat sheet explicitly asks for "how she looks doing it". The prose is fine as written. The ledger needs a scope note (for example, "first grade of her own person; grades of her work, her lies, her performances and 'the picture' do not spend it") or the later unit will clash. Do not change the prose; the author should approve the ledger line.
