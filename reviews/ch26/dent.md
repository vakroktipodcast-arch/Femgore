# Dent review: ch26 (The Escape)

- **Verdict:** REVISE. Structure, Turns and Snapper are sound. The chapter runs well over length, Scene 2's Turn is pre-empted, and Reyes drops out of Scene 3.

## Turn map

- **Scene 1:** lands at "I was on the wrong side of it by eleven seconds. I kept walking." It is an event: the radio, the maglock, the count. Present and correct.
- **Scene 2:** lands at "She didn't stop." and closes on "I knew exactly who she was. I am telling you now…: Special Agent E. Reyes." Present, but see finding 2.
- **Scene 3:** lands at "I was not hungry. I noted that I would once have called that a finding." It is an admission, set up by the hand-on-the-page event at the crossing. Present and correct.
- **Snapper:** "Behind me, an hour back, the vendor gate had closed on its timer, unhurried. I hadn't counted it." It is an admission, not a cliffhanger.

## Findings

1. **Length overrun.** The target is ~3,150 words (style/10 point 1, "nothing else may" run long). By my paragraph count, with no wc tool available and so approximate, the draft runs about 3,700 to 3,900 words. Scene 1 is ~1,450 to 1,500 against a ~1,100 suggestion. Scene 2 is ~1,200 against ~900. Scene 3 is ~1,200. Check with `scripts/lint.py`. Fix: cut Scene 1 by about 350 words and Scene 2 by about 250.
   - Scene 1 cuts: the stair-air and lighting paragraph ("At each landing an emergency light in a wire cage"), the closet inventory ("The round ivory cloths were where I had left them"), and the tote packing list, which duplicates the beat sheet's own New facts.
   - Scene 2 cuts: the mop bucket and sign, and one of the two Reyes-body paragraphs.
   - The long Reyes-hands sentence stays.

2. **Scene 2's Turn is spoiled before it lands.** "I had her surname and her face. I wasn't sure that made us even." comes about 10 lines before "Special Agent E. Reyes". It breaks outline Scene 2, whose Turn is "I tell you her name before her interlude does", and Dent point 3 on Turns, because the reader already has the surname. The arithmetic paragraph ("one federal person in this building…") also gives away the identity. Fix: in the face/file paragraph, cut "I had her surname and her face" and keep only the asymmetry, "Tonight she had my face, and nothing to attach it to." Let the name arrive first in the closing paragraph. Optionally trim the arithmetic paragraph to the absences (no earpiece, no pin) and drop "federal person… distribution list".

3. **The named pressure is thin or absent in Scene 3** (style/10 point 4; strategy/03 menace-in-every-quarter). Reyes does not appear in Scene 3 at all after the corridor. The beat sheet planned her as "a face Iris can't stop seeing". The only pressure there is the single "locked drawer with my own page in it" line, and sirens heard "going the other way". Reyes as paper or as a body is not felt on the walk. Fix: add one or two lines mid-walk, for example Iris catching herself composing Reyes's face against a shop window, or counting what Reyes now has. Do not add a grade. The pressure of Reyes and the knowledge from 18 is also near-absent in Scene 1 beyond the borrowed-name badge. One clause in the stair or booth passage would do it, for example the radio voice that says his name. Appetite is only first felt in Scene 2.

4. **The Scene 1 Turn line is ambiguous, and the geometry behind it is soft.** "I was on the wrong side of it by eleven seconds." The maglock locked behind her, so she is on the free side, and the line can read as if she were trapped. Separately, the booth is "a few steps from the fire door" yet eleven seconds have elapsed since the latch when she is "level with his glass". Fix: keep the outline line verbatim, but give one beat before the radio that explains the margin. Either she slowed to confirm the man's back was turned, or the booth is further off. State plainly that the eleven seconds was the thin margin she earned by pacing the route.

5. **Hero rule: low-grade convenience, not a breach.** Several things go her way and are not all explicitly earned on the page: the security man's back turned, the servers who "neither side looked", the lockdown stopping at stairs and lifts, and Reyes not stopping. Most are set up (Ch20 maps the gate and the 19-second timer, and "That is what the wordmark is for" covers the jacket). The booth man's back is the weakest. "I had checked that on the plan" covers sight lines but not his posture. Fix: have Iris note why he doesn't turn (the radio is his focus, or she gives him a reason not to) or let her read it as a risk she took. Keep "Fire code requires a building to let you out." as the explanation for the gate not locking.

6. **Two codas stack at the end.** "The light changed. I left the hand where it was and crossed." then the Turn, then the Snapper. The shape is as designed, but "I noted that I would once have called that a finding" and "I hadn't counted it" are both admissions in the last three lines. Keep both; just make sure the crossing line and the Turn are not padded further in revision.

## What works

- First sentence: it puts Iris in the trouble immediately (door shut, torn sticker, third knock), with no gap from Ch25.
- The right-hand silence and its payoff at the crossing, with the Ch2 echo made explicit. The Turn is an event (she looks down), not a summary.
- The last line, "I hadn't counted it": it closes the clock motif by admission and not by cliffhanger.
