**Verdict:** REVISE (light; a handful of sentences, no structural change)

**Findings:**

1. "I didn't make the joke that would have made it easier... there is always one available." Breaks 02-voice-register, Humour point: in Act III the jokes thin and "the reader should feel the loss before they can name it." This sentence names it for them. It is the chapter's one explicit statement of the novel's joke strategy. Fix: cut from "and there was one available" to the end of the sentence. Keep "I didn't make the joke that would have made it easier," or cut the whole sentence and let "I didn't ask about her weekend" carry it.

2. "The machine was intact. Only the man was gone." Drifts toward explanation (agent brief; 01 point 1, never to explain). It is a thesis line that could close any political thriller. The sentence before it ("I read his name on the captions twice to keep it, and I haven't kept it") already does the work through her body and memory. Fix: cut both sentences.

3. "and I had just eaten for the first time in a week." Explains what the scene has just shown, and it repeats "after a week of tea and water" from four lines earlier (02 point 5: she reports, the reader judges; agent brief: no drift toward explanation). It also flattens the run-up to the Snapper. Fix: end the sentence at "Thursday was in three days." If the contrast has to stay, keep it only in the stomach sentence.

4. "My mouth was dry. I swallowed." This is a stock thriller tic that could sit in any novel. Elsewhere the chapter does dry mouth in her register ("My tongue was dry against my teeth"; "Nothing happened in my mouth"). Fix: cut both sentences, or make it anatomical and exact in her way, e.g. something about the tongue against the palate, as at line 125.

5. "I don't remember deciding to." / "hadn't yet decided to take it off" / "I hadn't decided to do that either." That is three non-decisions in the first half of the chapter. The ch25 anchor uses the device twice, sparingly, at the moments that count, and here it becomes a tic. Fix: keep the third (the roasting, where it pays off against the eating) and cut the other two. For the coat, use "with my coat on, because the heating was off" or simply "with my coat on".

6. "along with the fact that it was only work." This comes close to naming the absence of appetite, which 11 (empty/hollow/numb point) says to show through the body and never state. Fix: end at "I would like that on the record." The flat dictaphone line before it already shows the absence.

**What works:**

- The eating paragraph (line 137). It is one greedy sentence with Latinate and plain words colliding ("the parotids going like taps", "the only honest thing about a chicken"), the 02 rhythm at full strength, and the right inverse of the ch17 chicken. Do not break it up.
- The Hannah call as graded technique ("I want you to see the work"; "even bites, even tension"). Direct address there implicates the reader as it should, and "It was the only thing I said all evening that wasn't technique" lands.
- The hand over the bin echoing the ch25 hand over the chest ("It didn't shake, either"), and the Snapper's steady hands. Leave both exactly as they are.
