# Voice review: ch27

- **Verdict:** REVISE

- **Findings:**

1. "I can tell you why the story could be that simple." This paragraph uses "you" to explain the lone-extremist cover story to the reader. It does not implicate them. Breaks 01 point 1 (direct address "always to implicate... Never to explain") and 02 (drift toward explanation). Fix: cut the opening two sentences. Keep the facts ("There had been no page on him... It had one.") so the reader works out the logic for themselves. If address stays, make the reader complicit, for example: "You would have filed it there too."

2. "The machine was intact. Only the man was gone." Then "and nobody home." This states the book's argument outright, and "nobody home" is a stock idiom that could sit in any novel. Breaks 02 (drift toward explanation) and the "any other novel" test. Fix: cut both sentences and the "I see that on the table" line. End the paragraph on "I haven't kept it." If a table image is wanted, use one specific anatomical observation, without the gloss.

3. "Mara's had been near it at every one of them." "Them" has no antecedent ("politics" is singular, and no killings are named nearby). The joke gets lost in the grammar. Breaks 02 (exact). Fix: name the antecedent, for example: "Mara's had been six inches from the last two."

4. "I knew what was in it. I knew what my hands had done." The second sentence is generic thriller prose ("any other novel"). Fix: cut it, or make it exact and anatomical, for example a cut or a gram weight from ch17 that the report would show.

5. "I could feel the look land, between the shoulder blades" is the genre's stock "felt eyes on her back", and the draught simile is generic too. Breaks 06 (exact sensation) and the "any other novel" test. Fix: give it a source Iris would notice, for example the change in the squeak of the clogs stopping, or Mara's reflection in the steel of the table.

6. "That was how I found out: from Mara, at the sinks, who had been asked..." This sentence repeats what the scene has just shown and blunts the Turn. Breaks 02 (explanation). Fix: end the scene on "rescheduled for Thursday."

7. "She was quiet for one beat too long." This is a stock phrase. Iris measures. Fix: count it the way she counts, for example "She was quiet for two of the dishwasher's turns," or time it against the tap.

8. "the skin broke under my thumb like the top of a crème brûlée" is a stock food-writing simile inside the best long sentence in the chapter. Breaks 06 (taste/texture named precisely) and the "any other novel" test. Fix: use an image from her own register, for example the way a scalp reflects, or wet parchment, or keep "gone the colour of a violin" as the model.

9. "I looked at them for a long time." The Snapper ends on a generic closing line. "After a long time" also appears earlier in the same scene. Breaks 02 (exact; "scrupulously reliable about everything else") and the "any other novel" test. Fix: end on "and they were steady." Or give a duration she would log, for example "until the clock on the cooker had gone round to ten."

10. "I had disposed of the kit from three lessons without once looking back." The bin image has already made the point, so this line explains it, and "without once looking back" is a stock phrase. Breaks 02 (explanation). Fix: cut this sentence and the next, and go straight from "It didn't shake, either..." to the hinge creaking.

- **What works:**
1. The chicken sentence ("the parotids going like taps", "the only honest thing about a chicken", the joint giving "when you know where") matches the ch17 anchor's Latinate/plain collision and greedy length. Keep its length and its callbacks.
2. The Hannah call: "I want you to see the work" is direct address used correctly (it makes the reader her student). The suture simile ("even bites, even tension") and the "Soon" / "She had heard it." Turn are right too. Leave the clipped dialogue alone.
3. The jokes are thinned for Act III but still there: "no colder than my flat", "Nobody has ever complained about the wording", "there is always one available." Do not add more and do not cut these.
