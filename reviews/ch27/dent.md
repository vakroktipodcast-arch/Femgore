# ch27 — Dent structure review

- **Verdict:** REVISE (small, targeted fixes; the structure is sound and no scene needs redrafting).

**Count and shape.** Three scenes. The lint count of 3,246 is about 3% over the ~3,150 target, which is inside the "~" in style 10 point 1. By eye the scenes run about 1,150, 1,000 and 1,000, close to the beat sheet's split.

**Where each Turn lands**
- Scene 1: "And Dr. Vale. Postponed by Election Eve." This is the chapter's last line of the scene. It is landed as reported speech and an event, with no reaction from Iris. Present, but weakened (finding 1).
- Scene 2: "I began to say Sunday." The gap, then "Soon," the two seconds of quiet, and "She had heard it." It lands as an event and an admission. Present and strong.
- Scene 3: "The drawer didn't lock. I didn't lock it." This is its own paragraph and then the Snapper. Present, slightly pre-empted (finding 2).

**Other checks.** The Snapper is "My hands were on the table either side of the plate", ending on "I looked at them for a long time." It is an image, with no cliffhanger and no instruction to the reader. Reyes and the act's pressure are felt in all three scenes: the rota call in S1, "somebody else's diary" in S2, and the unlocked drawer in S3. There is no luck or rescue. She turns her own key, and Mara's report comes through the rota, not a coincidence. The last sentence carries weight.

- **Findings:**

1. S1 Turn is half-spent before it arrives. Mara says "Your shift pattern for Thursday. I said days." and "you were meant to see her last Wednesday." That delivers both Thursday and the fact that Iris is on Reyes's list about ten lines before "Dr. Vale... Rescheduled for Thursday." The reader and Iris have already inferred the news, so the final line confirms it instead of landing it. This breaks style 10 point 3 (the scene must end on its Turn as an event) and strategy 03 point 1 (a Turn is information that changes the situation). Fix: have Reyes ask only for "Your shift pattern. I said days." and cut "for Thursday". Keep "meant to see her last Wednesday" only if "Before." stays as the sting. Then "Thursday" is heard for the first time in the last line.

2. S3: the Turn is pre-empted by the key beat. "I didn't turn it." appears at the drawer, about 350 words before "I didn't lock it." The beat sheet says "No comment yet; the Turn line comes after the meal." The reader has the admission before the meal, so the Turn line repeats it instead of delivering it. Fix: end that beat on "The key stayed in the lock." This still shows the key and meets ledger ruling 3, so it cannot read as a drawer with no lock. The later "I didn't lock it" is then the first time Iris owns the choice.

3. S1 slackens between the case and the Turn. Five consecutive pluperfect summary paragraphs ("The result had come in...", "The Chief had done the Senator...", "Also on Friday, a statement had gone out...") carry about 300 words of reported fact. Here the "scene 1 grabs" rule (strategy 03) and the pressure rule (style 10 point 4) both slip. Reyes is absent from about 600 to 900 words, and Mara reappears only after the Chief and statement paragraphs. Fix: compress the result, Chief and statement paragraphs to roughly 120 words. Alternatively move the Chief paragraph, which has the best line ("My hands had written most of it."), to sit directly before the Mara-at-the-sinks beat so it points toward Reyes.

4. S1 opening, minor. The first sentence ("The leaf mould came in on his trouser cuffs.") is concrete but carries nothing of the aftermath. The first trouble, the captions at "acted alone", lands about 330 words in, at the edge of "inside its first page" (style 10 point 2). The paragraph also spends "I noticed none of it" on the opening. Fix: either cut the second half of paragraph 1 so the captions land by about word 250, or keep the opening and open paragraph 2 with the week-after fact ("a week after") already pressing, for example moving the scar-touch beat earlier.

5. Word count, minor. 3,246 is over target; style 10 point 1 says nothing else may run long. Findings 1 to 3 would recover roughly 150 to 200 words, so no separate cut is needed.

- **What works:**
  - The S2 Turn: the unfilled "Sunday", "Soon", the one extra second of quiet and "She had heard it." It is an event inside the call and is not explained.
  - The S3 structure: refusal, the bin, the drawer, then the single long sentence of eating, and the Snapper on the cooling fat and the hands. It does not tell the reader what Thursday holds.
  - Mara's report in S1 as the way the chapter pays off the paper-closing pressure through the office's own rota, with no coincidence.
