## Findings

1. **S1 Turn is summarised, then explained.** "She dried her hands... and told me the rest" followed by "She had said there were only two left. She had interviewed nine..." puts the chapter's information Turn in indirect speech. The last sentence, "That was how I found out: from Mara, at the sinks", restates the outline's wording as narration. This breaks style 10 point 3 and strategy 03 ("information that changes the situation") by relaying the Turn instead of staging it. Fix: give the Turn to Mara as one flat line of dialogue in the report voice she was taught, for example "Nine of eleven done. Two left. A woman who's left the country, and you." Cut "That was how I found out..." and end on Mara's paper towel or on the line itself. Beat 10 also has Iris not asking what else Mara was told. Add that as a single action, because it is the only competence Iris shows in the scene.

2. **S1 Reyes pressure arrives late (style 10 point 2; strategy 03 pressure).** About 700 words pass before Reyes is named. The middle (TV, result, replacement, Holt's autopsy, the three women) is five paragraphs of status report with no pressure on Iris in the room. The first page is a heart and a thrombus. Mara's stopped stride and the "acted alone" scar touch do carry trouble, but they sit after about 350 words. Fix: trim the thrombus detail by roughly a third and bring Mara's stopping short and the TV up to the second paragraph. Trim "Chief did the Senator himself" to one line.

3. **S2 last line glosses its own event.** "She had heard it." follows "quiet for one beat too long" and "Okay... Love you." Those already carry the Turn. The beat sheet says to end on Hannah having heard without stating what she thinks. Fix: end on "Love you." and let Iris's gap and swallow carry the realisation, or keep a version that is Iris's own admission rather than a report of Hannah. For example, "I put the phone down on the desk beside the letter" with no commentary.

4. **S2 does not feel Reyes (style 10 point 4).** Nothing in the scene touches Thursday except the unspoken gap at "Sunday". It works only if the reader still holds S1's news. The beat sheet wants Reyes to be the Thursday Iris cannot say. Fix: add about 40 words, which also closes the word gap. One clause as she says "Soon", such as the date having a name now, would do it without naming Reyes.

5. **Escape is credited to the system, not to Iris (strategy 03 hero rule).** "It had liked a lone extremist. It had one." and "a system that is handed a body... will file it" make Holt's clean closure look like the machine's convenience. Fix: add one clause making it hers, for example that she had not pinned it and the filing followed from that. This is also the reason Iris has the "Full marks" authority later.

6. **Closing image over-announces (style 10 point 8).** "the window over the sink was black... and nothing in it" names a reflection by denying it. The reader supplies 27. Fix: cut "and nothing in it", or cut the window sentence. End the meal on the stomach finding.

## What works

- The Snapper is the fat setting on her fingers and "I looked at them for a long time." No instruction to the reader and no Thursday plan. Keep it exactly.
- The S3 sequence of dry mouth, the pedal bin and the unopened hand, the key left in the lock, then the single long greedy sentence. The appetite return is correctly timed and has the only joke in S3 (the oysters).
- The first and last sentences are sound. "The leaf mould came in on his trouser cuffs" sets up "the only thing in the room that had been anywhere". In S2, the technique description and "It was the only thing I said all evening that wasn't technique" are strong and need no change.
