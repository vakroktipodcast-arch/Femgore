- **Verdict:** PASS

Ch27 passes the genre lens. The appetite is starved and on the body (the skinned-over coffee, the belt loose on the trussing-needle hole, "Nothing happened in my mouth"). Then it returns in a meal described as precisely as tissue, with relish and disgust in the same lines ("roof of a blister", "tendons in it like wet string") and "because I sit after" tying the meal to a kill. She never apologises and never moralises, and the chapter ends on steady hands without saying what they will do. That leaves her harder to look away from, not easier to dismiss.
