Breaks `ledger/continuity.md`, People > Iris: Ch 19 S3's "competent" at the mirror is "her first grade of herself" (stated twice, Ch 18 and Ch 19 lines). Leave "whether he meant me, or a cabinet he had never once pictured with anything inside it."

4. **"her phone stayed inside her jacket the whole hour"** (S1). Iris's watch runs from the logged 7:52 heron to the 8:33 post, about forty minutes. The chapter itself says "forty minutes" in the opening paragraph. Fix: "the whole forty minutes" (or "the whole time I had her").

5. **"in the cold of the footwell"** (Snapper paragraph). The ledger turns the weather cold only on 13–14 Oct, and the beat sheet forbids calling 12 Sep cold weather. The fat sets because the oxtail came out of the fridge, not because of the car. Fix: "the fat had set to a pale lid, as it does from the fridge, and the gelatin underneath had firmed."
