- **Verdict:** REVISE

- **Findings:**

1. **The dark adaptation needs some light.** "the room comes back to you not in colour but in no colour" (S3). Rods adapt to dim light. They do not make an image out of none. The seam has just been sealed with weatherstrip, packing and a blanket, and nothing else lets light in, so the room should stay black however long she waits. A pathologist narrating rhodopsin would know this. Breaks: the outline Turn (ch04, S3: "until my eyes adjust") and the ledger's twenty minutes of dark adaptation (Fixed numbers). Smallest fix: one clause that gives a faint source facing away from the trail, such as an airbrick high in the back gable or a hairline of dusk under the roof slates. Because clap three passed with that gap open, the source also makes the soundproofing claim stronger.

2. **The app can't record where she looked.** "In a hundred and forty-one Sundays she had never looked up the spur." A GPS track records position, not gaze. Iris says she separates what she observed from what she supplied ("so you'll know the difference"), so this overclaim undercuts her own method. Breaks: ledger Fixed numbers, Trailmark (141 walks, positional data only). Fix: "In a hundred and forty-one Sundays her track had never once turned up the spur." Related: "her phone stayed inside her jacket the whole hour". Iris watched from the bench onwards, not the whole hour. Change to "the whole time I had her".

3. **The 6 September close pass can't be done as described.** "walk round below the dam the other way and take the stretch from the bench to the marker post at her pace … twenty past to thirty-three past". She watches Diane sit at 8:09 from the west shore, 180 m across the water. She would then have to walk round the reservoir and reach the bench by 8:20, which is close to impossible. Walking the stretch in those same minutes also puts her a few metres behind the target, in plain view, a week before the kill. A surveillance-trained reader will stop here. Breaks: ledger Fixed numbers (bench 8:05–8:20, bench to spur post about 13 min) and the logic of the cover. Fix: once Diane has gone, she walks the stretch at Diane's measured pace, timed against 8:20–8:33 on her watch. Alternatively she meets Diane head-on, coming counter-clockwise from the dam. Either needs one clause.

4. **An "identical" padlock fails the first time an authority key is tried.** "hung an identical one of my own in its place. Nobody had noticed. Nobody checks." The replacement can look the same but cannot be keyed alike, so any inspector finds out at once. As written, the plan works only because nobody happens to come, which is luck. Breaks: the agent brief (nothing should read as luck) and the beat sheet (ch04 S3.2: "Nobody checks" is meant to be the county's pressure, so it needs evidence behind it). Fix: "the same make, the same brass gone dull". Then add one observed sign of disuse from the night she cut it, for example the old shackle rusted into the hasp or the keyway packed with grit and spider silk. That makes "Nobody checks" a finding, not a hope.

5. **Sweeping a derelict floor leaves a visible sign, and the prose gives no reason for it.** "I swept the floor and bagged the sweepings." In an abandoned building, a clean floor tells investigators someone prepared the room. Iris would know that and would weigh it. Ch 8's "pump-station dust" on the vinyl shows dust remains in places, so the sweep is not a continuity error. It just has no reason given. Breaks: S3's own claim ("named … the things an investigator would look for … took each of them away"). Fix: one clause such as "because dust keeps a shoe print better than plaster, and mine were already in it from the night of the padlock".

6. **Clap force: the stinging palms and the test both need hard claps.** "where three claps' worth of small capillaries had been asked to take it". Three ordinary claps don't make palms sting. A clap is also a single short impulse, while a woman shouting is louder for longer, so a sceptic will doubt that the test covers a voice. The ledger fixes three claps (Fixed numbers: Soundproofing test). Fix without changing the count: say each clap was as hard as she could make it, "harder than anyone claps for anything". That explains the stinging palms, and the strongest sound her hands can make becomes a credible stand-in for a voice. Optionally add one dry line in which she knows a voice lasts longer than a clap and accepts that limit.
