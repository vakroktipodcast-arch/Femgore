# ch06 — Voice

- **Verdict:** REVISE. Light revision. The narrator is the anchor's narrator, but the draft spends lines Chapter 17 needs, leans on a few tics and twice explains itself.

## Findings

1. **"The dead don't bleed. I have known that for fifteen years"** (sc3, the pin). Chapter 17 uses this line as its revelation ("I need you to understand about that. The dead don't bleed. The dead seep."). If it appears here first, the anchor's beat turns into a repeat. Rule: anchors/README (17 is the lush pole; 6 must not pre-empt it), 02 pt 1. Fix: keep the pin simile and the "Nothing came" image and cut the aphorism. For example: "Nothing came. Nothing ever does, at my end of it. It was still the plainest thing in the room."

2. **"glottal, involuntary, the larynx closing on something"** (sc1, Diane's sound). This lifts the exact pair from 17 ("below decision: glottal, involuntary"), where it carries the scene. The drip "as if it were paid by the drop" (sc1, para 3) also pre-echoes 17's house-pianist joke ("paid by the note"). Rule: anchors (voice standard; do not spend the anchor's material). Fix: describe Diane's sound some other way that is still exact (e.g. "a small catch at the back of the throat, the larynx shutting on something it had not been told to swallow"). Give the drip a different joke, or no joke.

3. **"floor by floor"** shows up three times: the face "lit floor by floor" (sc1), the policy paragraph (sc3), and "understanding, slowly, floor by floor" (sc3). Paragraphs 93 and 99 also tell the same idea twice, that the matter number leads investigators up the building. That is explanation, and the repetition flattens the effect. Rule: 01 pt 1 / 02 (direct address implicates, never explains); 06 (exactness). Fix: keep "floor by floor" in only one place, probably sc1. Cut the second half of the policy paragraph ("Whoever found her would pick up a fragment…") and let para 99's wanting do that work. Para 99's "read them" also has no clear antecedent; change it to "read the fragments."

4. **"Sensory deprivation lowers the startle threshold… That was the design."** (sc2, the blindfold). This reads as a procedure manual, and "That was the design" could sit in any thriller. Rule: 02 pt 1 (dry and alive, not explaining); reviewer brief (any-novel sentence). Fix: cut both sentences, or swap them for one physical consequence she can see (her shoulders coming up at the sound of the tape roll).

5. **"I can think of an institution with the same reflex, and so can you."** (sc2, the CO2 paragraph). The implication is right, but the line explains the metaphor the paragraph has already built. Rule: 01 pt 1 (implicate, never explain). Fix: "So can you." on its own, or "You know the institution."

6. **"It was the worst thing she could have said."** (sc1, end). Generic, the kind of sentence any novel could contain. The paragraph's real point comes in the next sentence. Rule: reviewer brief (any-novel sentence); 02 pt 1. Fix: cut it and start the paragraph at "She still believed the file had been the protection…"

7. **The "noted/logged" tic.** Count: "noted that they were steady," "noted it, and did not solve it," "expected, logged," "I noted it," "noticed, as a finding," plus "I am reporting that. It goes in the same column." The last one lifts 17's "I am reporting that" and "same column" verbatim. At this density the reflex becomes a mannerism and blunts 17. Rule: 02 pt 3 (the grading voice leaks; it shouldn't saturate); anchors. Fix: cut two of the "noted" lines (keep "did not solve it. I had a lot on." because it's funny). Rephrase the "same column" sentence, e.g. "It goes on the sheet next to 10:47."

8. **"I held the cap in my teeth"** (sc3, the marker). Something goes in her mouth and no taste is given. Rule: 06 pt 4 (everything she puts in her mouth is described). Fix: one clause, e.g. "the plastic tasting of solvent and somebody else's office."

9. **Colour repetition**: slate four times (face "plum going to slate," shirt, paper "going to slate," plus the earlier dusky), plum twice. Rule: 06 pt 1 (named precisely, which means varied). Fix: change the shirt to another named colour (ash, charcoal), and the paper's wet edge to "going to dishwater" or "lichen."

## What works

- The gap between Iris out loud ("I know. I used them.") and on the page, and the way the grading reflex turns on her ("graded the speed as a kindness, and took a mark off for it"). Rule 07 pt 3 and 02 pt 3, both done right.
- The long sentence at "Then the quiet…", greedy, bodily, and placed after the clipped procedure. It shows the rhythm rule working.
- The withheld word in the scene-2 coda, and "because I had decided to be": unreliable about why, reliable about everything else.
