1. **Dictation order is wrong, and a pathologist would hear it at once.** "The body is that of an adult female ... consistent with the stated age." / "The word that comes next ... is *well-nourished*." In a real external-examination preamble, "well-nourished" goes *before* "adult female", not after "stated age". Her own amended sentence puts it in the right place, so the two versions contradict each other. Rule: outline/ch19.md S3 Turn ("check ... the way I'd check a body") and ledger Ch 25 (she says "The body is that of" aloud and stops). It becomes correct procedure and sets up Ch 25's broken-off sentence more cleanly.
