- **Verdict:** REVISE (light; three small fixes, no structural change).

- **Findings:**

1. **The fixer keeps a dead man's ID without a thought.** "the halves and the licence into the sleeve, the sleeve back onto its shelf." Garrity's licence is the one object in the house that ties Cam to a body on a warehouse floor. A twenty-year fixer would clock that before he did anything else. The sleeve is fixed (ledger, Objects: Garrity's licence; beat 5), so it stays. As written, though, he files it without a flicker, and that reads as the author needing it on the shelf rather than as his choice. It also wastes the best evidence that he has stopped managing himself, which the Theo beat states outright two paragraphs later. Fix: add one clause before the shelving that shows he knows what it is and keeps it anyway, e.g. "He knew what the licence was. He shelved it with the rest." This should cost no more than a dozen words. The 8x10 left on the counter through Monday can stand. Its "Cam knew where the print had come from" already covers it.

2. **The suite record runs four hours on a twenty-minute side.** "He put the record on and turned it almost to nothing". He arrives about 6:20 (beat 9; ledger, Timeline Tue 13 Oct). Side one is about 20 minutes (ledger, Fixed numbers), and Ch 16 has side one still playing when Iris arrives about 10:15, running out about 10:35 (beats/ch16.md, line 150). As written, the record ends about 6:40 and the room sits silent until she comes, so Ch 15's Snapper (Mingus under the door) has nothing to leak. Fix: one clause showing the repeat, e.g. "and when the arm went home he set it back to the start." That also makes the waiting audible.

3. **"Almost to nothing" against the leak.** Same sentence. This is an acoustically treated room with the door merely unsealed, and Ch 15/16 need the music heard in the corridor. "Almost to nothing" through an acoustic door reads as inaudible. Fix: "turned it down low", which keeps "so that he would hear a knock". Optional, related: "The glass was already leaving its ring" is fine only if the ring comes from spirit on the base. Neat rye at room temperature doesn't sweat. If the reviser wants it airtight, give it the pour ("rye down the side of the glass"). Otherwise leave it.
