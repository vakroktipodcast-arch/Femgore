- **Verdict:** REVISE. The fixes are light; the interlude is close to passing.

- **Findings:**

1. "The record was on his own pillow, where his head went." This is the most femgore image in the unit: she stood over his sleeping place. It is rendered only by sight, which breaks `style/04` (smell first, sight last) and leaves `style/05` (Smell: the morgue on her skin that no shower removes) unused. Iris is the woman who cannot wash the morgue off, and it is her first trace in his bed. **Fix:** before he sees the record, give Cam one faint, wrong smell on the pillow, clinical and cold, that he cannot name and does not like. One clause is enough. It puts her body in his bed without putting her in the scene.

2. "Then the farm went dark, a reporter's message promised…" / "He knew the hand. Vale's." In Act II the gifts to Brandt are flirtation (`strategy/10`, Act II), and Iris's precision is the eroticism (`style/04`, point 2). Here her work turns into a summary list, and the label "Vale's" tells the reader what they should recognise. She becomes easier to file as plot (`style/12`). **Fix:** cut the list down to one shut door that is unmistakably hers, with a detail as exact as the quarter-inch licence. Let Cam recognise it from the detail and cut "Vale's." The reader should feel courted through his recognition.

3. "She could have killed him in his sleep, and she had chosen…" This copies the outline Turn almost word for word, and the sentence before it has already landed the Turn through the image ("break a record so that it would fit back together"). Spelling her appetite out as motive makes her a case study (`style/12`) when she should stay a pressure. **Fix:** cut that sentence and "She wanted him to understand first, and". End on the pillow image plus "he found, to his surprise, that he wanted to." The Turn stays intact and it is no longer explained.

4. "He played side one of the Black Saint… the tone arm lifted" / "He put the record on". The outline's Mingus is there, but in the final room "the record" is ambiguous: is it the mended Dolphy he carried in the mailer, or something else? Vinyl is Cam's motif (`style/09`), and in this room it is the man's last appetite set against hers. Make it clear what is on the platter so his pleasure has a name. **Fix:** name the record in the suite. Keep it Mingus and turn the volume "almost to nothing".

- **What works:**
  - "Cut first, then snapped. Not temper. Care." Iris's appetite is seen from outside as craft. This is the right Act II register: no violence, all intent. Keep it word for word.
  - Cam's appetites fail on the page: the rye "tasted thinner in daylight", the "coffee he did not drink", the post-midnight rule he invents. His body carries the scene and mirrors her meals in reverse.
  - The dry humour: "Through the glass Holt did not look up, which took real skill"; "Fair mark." He grades himself with her method, laughing, and never moralises.
