Status: **proposed, not approved.** Nothing here is in `ledger/continuity.md` yet. Sources: all 32 files in `beats/` (every `## New facts` line and every `## Conflicts` note), checked against `ledger/continuity.md`, `anchors/ch17.md`, the outlines and the bible.

How to read it:
- Each fact is tagged with the unit(s) that proposed it.
- **→ C*n*** means the fact is disputed. Clash *n* below gives the recommended value. Where a clash recommendation changes a fact, the fact is listed in its recommended form, with the losing value in the clash.
- **(cal)** means the date is restated on the merged calendar in the Timeline check. Some units' own dates are superseded there.
- The year is never named. Every weekday below matches a year in which Tuesday 25 August and Tuesday 3 November fall ten weeks apart, Labor Day is 7 September and the clocks go back on 1 November.
