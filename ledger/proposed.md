### C2. Ch 27 relay wording: a reading check, not a fact clash
- Mara: "Eight of them done by the end of that week. Then on Friday one came in through a lawyer". Read literally, "end of that week" is the Friday, and "Then on Friday" makes the ninth fall on or after the eighth on the same day.
- Ledger (lines 272–273, 280): Tue interviews lost, the rest completed Wed–Fri, the ninth is the eleventh name, who came in on Fri 6 Nov. The arithmetic (8 + 1 = 9 of 11, two left) closes.
- **Recommend no ledger change; fold item 9 as drafted.** The count agrees with the ledger, and the overlap is Mara's loose speech. If the author wants it exact, the prose fix is "Eight of them done by the Friday."

### C3. Brandt found by consultancy staff vs. "staff never enter the suite"
- Line 260 and line 468 say consultancy staff never enter the suite (his private room, kept locked). Ch 19 now puts "found … by staff from his own consultancy" on the page. The ch19 log left out how they got in.
- **Recommend recording that how staff came into the locked suite is unstated (as drafted under Timeline, line 260), and changing line 468 to "consultancy staff do not normally enter it".** "Never" is absolute, and the page now has staff finding him there.
