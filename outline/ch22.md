# Chapter 22 — The Point of No Return

- **Act:** Act III — The Final Exam (Chapters 19–27)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Reyes, closing on paper; the knowledge from 18 in every room. Appetite: starved.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch22.md`

**Scene 1 — Trapped.** Through the sealed service door while the detail stands outside the main one. Both locked behind me. No egress now that doesn’t go through the detail or back the way I came — and that way closes in forty seconds.

> **Turn:** The sticker is torn. Someone will see it. My clock has become a shorter clock.

**Scene 2 — Recognition.** He looks up from the photograph. He sees me. He does not recognise me.

> **Turn:** He smiles. It is the smile from the debate, and it is for me, and it means nothing, and I have been hungry for this for two years and find there is nothing in my mouth.

**Scene 3 — The Pause.** A long moment. I stand in front of him and wait. He says, helpfully: ‘I think there may have been a mix-up. Are you with the network?’

> **Turn:** I have no line for this. The syllabus begins with his recognition. I tell you that, and I hear how small my voice has got.

> **Snapper:** The Marine between us on the table, facing him.
