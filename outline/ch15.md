# Chapter 15 — The Isolation

- **Act:** Act II — The Practicum (Chapters 10–18)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Brandt, until Chapter 17; then her own knowledge. Appetite: named, indulged, and finally seen.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch15.md`

**Scene 1 — Resources Cut.** A regulatory inquiry triggered against the shell company. I don’t need it to succeed; I need him dark for forty-eight hours.

> **Turn:** It works in nine. I have more time than I need, and I distrust it, and I use it to cook.

**Scene 2 — The Herding.** Small, precise pressures push him toward the one safe place left: the soundproofed editing suite of a media consultancy he owns silently.

> **Turn:** He goes willingly. He has read the syllabus. He knows what a soundproofed room is for, and he goes anyway, and I understand that this is his answer to the broken record.

**Scene 3 — Dressing.** I prepare. Tools laid out like a tasting menu. I shower, I dress, I eat lightly — ‘you don’t eat before’. The reader should recognise the ritual from a different kind of evening.

> **Turn:** In the mirror I look like someone going on a date. I say so. I don’t correct it.

> **Snapper:** The suite’s door at the end of the corridor, and under it, faint, Mingus — The Black Saint and the Sinner Lady — turned down so he’ll hear me knock.

> *Editorial note:* v3’s ‘Cam Alone’ POV is folded into the second interlude. In v4 we arrive with Iris and hear the record through the door.
