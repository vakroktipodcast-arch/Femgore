# Chapter 2 — The Breach

- **Act:** Act I — The Prerequisite (Chapters 1–9)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: the campaign’s certainty that nothing is coming; from Chapter 8, Brandt. Appetite: present and unnamed.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch02.md`

**Scene 1 — The Approach.** A fundraiser at a downtown hotel. Cloned press credential, borrowed name, staff corridors walked with the confidence of someone who belongs. The campaign’s arrogance from inside — the door everyone walks through and no one checks. Iris on the staff: who she’d open first and why.

> **Turn:** A staffer waves me past with Diane’s name in his mouth. She’s here tonight, unscheduled. The recon has become an opportunity.

**Scene 2 — The Observation.** Visual contact. Diane handles donors, deflects a journalist, ends a young staffer’s career in thirty seconds of hallway. Competent, decent in nearly every register. I grade her. She fails.

> **Turn:** Her eyes pass over me and register nothing. I am invisible to these people. It is my advantage and the original injury, and I feel both, and the second one tastes like metal.

**Scene 3 — The Janitor.** The empty suite where I’ll stage my tools. A janitor walks in. I improvise, competently, and my hand trembles once before it stills.

> **Turn:** I have noticed the tremor. I don’t know what to do with it, so I describe it to you instead.

> **Snapper:** In the lift down I hold my left hand with my right until the doors open, and I am smiling, and I don’t know when that started.
