# Chapter 5 — The Trap

- **Act:** Act I — The Prerequisite (Chapters 1–9)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: the campaign’s certainty that nothing is coming; from Chapter 8, Brandt. Appetite: present and unnamed.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch05.md`

**Scene 1 — Sunday Morning.** I wait in the pump station. Diane walks the trail. The planted phone rings on the path and pulls her off-route.

> **Turn:** She picks it up and answers: ‘Olivia?’ In the dark, I almost stop. I don’t. I note that I almost did.

**Scene 2 — The Capture.** Administrator to captive, brutally quick, entirely physical. Three paragraphs. No more. Then a fourth, because in first person she tells you what it felt like, and what it felt like was good.

> **Turn:** Diane’s own phone buzzes in her coat — a text from Olivia. I power it down and seal it in foil. The text is still on the screen when it goes dark.

**Scene 3 — The Setup.** Inside, bound, I read her the first page in a voice I recognise for the first time as mine. She negotiates in the wrong currency — information: Brandt, a list, who else signed.

> **Turn:** ‘I don’t want information. I want the grade.’ She understands she is not in a negotiation. I understand I have been waiting two years to say that sentence out loud.

> **Snapper:** The foil-wrapped phone on the floor between us, a small silver brick, and my pulse in my teeth.
