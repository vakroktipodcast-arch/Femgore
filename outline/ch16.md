# Chapter 16 — The Abattoir

- **Act:** Act II — The Practicum (Chapters 10–18)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Brandt, until Chapter 17; then her own knowledge. Appetite: named, indulged, and finally seen.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch16.md`

**Scene 1 — Arrival.** I don’t knock. He looks up. ‘I wondered if it would be you.’ Not afraid. Curious. On the table beside his drink: the photograph of the shredded NDA in Diane’s mouth. He kept it. He’s been looking at it for weeks.

> **Turn:** He has set out two glasses.

**Scene 2 — The Conversation.** Long, almost civil. He knows the syllabus is for him. He asks how I came to it. He doesn’t beg. ‘I would have done the same thing.’ I don’t know what to do with that and so I drink. He tells me the list has eleven names.

> **Turn:** I had believed myself alone. I have built two years on being the only one. He watches the information land and is kind about it, and that is unbearable.

**Scene 3 — The Preparation.** I bind him. He lets me. ‘Take your time. I would rather it was you than anyone else.’ I tell the reader, plainly, that no one has ever said anything like that to me, and that I am going to take him at his word.

> **Turn:** The reader understands that whatever happens next is going to be the best night of my life and that I will not survive it as the person I am.

> **Snapper:** The record ends. Neither of us moves to change it. I roll up my sleeves.
