# Chapter 17 — The Lesson (Execution Two)

- **Act:** Act II — The Practicum (Chapters 10–18)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Brandt, until Chapter 17; then her own knowledge. Appetite: named, indulged, and finally seen.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes (Chapter 17 may run long, up to ~6,000)
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch17.md`

**Scene 1 — The Method.** The set piece. The longest scene in the book — let it run past the chapter’s usual proportions. An autopsy in real time on a man who is awake. Explicit, sensory, anatomically exact: what skin does, what it smells like, the sounds, the heat of him, the exact weight of things. Iris is happy. The prose is lush, funny, tender, obscene. She uses the word beautiful. She means it. The reader must not be able to look away because she so clearly cannot.

> **Turn:** He begins to talk. I hadn’t expected him to be able to. I slow down so he can.

**Scene 2 — The Conversation, Continued.** He talks while I work — about the system, about Holt, about himself. Generous in his dying. He gives me Holt’s habit: one minute alone before every speech, with Mark’s photograph, detail outside by standing instruction. ‘That’s your door. It’s the only one.’ I thank him. I mean that too.

> **Turn:** I have my way into the room. It came from the man I am taking apart, and I am taking him apart more gently now, and I notice, and I don’t stop.

**Scene 3 — The Truth.** Near the end he tells me about a woman — name withheld here, recovered by the reader in Chapter 18 — who tried to reach me three years ago and left a voicemail I never returned. Who is dead. ‘I’m sorry. I thought you should know.’ I finish. I pin the page. MISTOOK CLARITY FOR CRUELTY. I leave the suite the way I came in.

> **Turn:** I am elated. I tell you I am elated. I am in the car before the elation reaches the part of me that heard what he said.

> **Snapper:** The Mingus sleeve on the table, the record still on the turntable, unplayed since side one. My hands on the wheel, red to the wrist, and I had not noticed.

> *Editorial note:* This is the scene the whole book is sold on. Every rule in Part Four, section 4 is written for it. Draft it first if you need to find the voice.
