# Chapter 13 — The Midpoint Shift

- **Act:** Act II — The Practicum (Chapters 10–18)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Brandt, until Chapter 17; then her own knowledge. Appetite: named, indulged, and finally seen.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch13.md`

**Scene 1 — The Visit.** I enter Brandt’s home. I don’t steal; I leave. Diane’s photograph on the kitchen counter. A record from his own collection on his pillow, broken cleanly in half. I handle his things like I’m choosing a gift.

> **Turn:** Beside the record, the dead contractor’s ID. I am telling him what it cost. I am, the reader should understand before I do, flirting.

**Scene 2 — His Kitchen.** I stay longer than I need to. I open his fridge. I eat something of his, standing up, in his kitchen, in the dark. I describe the taste.

> **Turn:** His piano in the next room. I touch one key. It is in tune. I leave before I can play more.

**Scene 3 — The Letter.** On the pillow, the second page. Lesson Two. The Architect. I don’t stay to watch him find it. I watch on the feed from my car.

> **Turn:** He laughs — a real, surprised laugh — pours a drink, puts on a record that isn’t the broken one, and plays it through. I sit in the car and listen to the whole side with him.

> **Snapper:** The needle lifting at the end of side one in two silent rooms three miles apart.
