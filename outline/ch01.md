# Chapter 1 — The Hook & the Syllabus

- **Act:** Act I — The Prerequisite (Chapters 1–9)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: the campaign’s certainty that nothing is coming; from Chapter 8, Brandt. Appetite: present and unnamed.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch01.md`

**Scene 1 — The Suite.** I open a Jane Doe and name each organ for Mara, who is watching the way people watch a chef. Establish the voice in the first paragraph: exact, funny, in love with the work. The smell, the weight of a liver in the hand, the small satisfaction of a clean cut — rendered as pleasure, not procedure, with Iris not quite calling it that.

> **Turn:** Mara says, quietly, that I work like I’m taking apart something I built. I let it pass. I note that I liked hearing it.

**Scene 2 — The Sister.** Lunch with Hannah and the nieces in a park. Hannah pushes gently on my life; I deflect with practised warmth and eat two sandwiches to her half. The ordinary world and its cost, and the first sign that Iris’s body wants more than it is given.

> **Turn:** Hannah asks if I’m seeing anyone. I lie, fluently and kindly, and tell the reader exactly how good the lie was.

**Scene 3 — The Glass and the Drawer.** Evening. The debate on my tablet. Holt adjusts his cuff in a small, specific way. Two seconds. Cold — real, physical, a body’s report. An hour without moving. Then the drawer: dossiers, photographs, THE BUTCHER’S SYLLABUS, two years old. I read Lesson One aloud to the empty flat and hear what my voice does to it.

> **Turn:** I lay out the red marker, the black tape, the zip-ties, and notice I am hungry.

> **Snapper:** I step out into the night. The tablet is still on. He is still talking to an empty room, and I have decided to stop being one.

> *Editorial note:* The grab is the autopsy, the trouble is the cuff, the hero takes hold at the drawer. Appetite is planted three times before she acts: Mara’s compliment, the second sandwich, the hunger at the drawer.
