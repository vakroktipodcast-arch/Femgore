# Chapter 19 — The Dark Night of the Soul

- **Act:** Act III — The Final Exam (Chapters 19–27)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Reyes, closing on paper; the knowledge from 18 in every room. Appetite: starved.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch19.md`

**Scene 1 — Isolation.** The syllabus has cost me everything outside it. Hannah has called four times this week. Mara has stopped texting. I’m alone with the work, and the work is the only thing I still want, and I want it less.

> **Turn:** Hannah leaves a voicemail. I find I cannot listen to voicemails any more. The phone carries one unheard message for the rest of the book.

**Scene 2 — The Apex.** Holt’s Election Eve schedule. Secret Service-level security, a ballroom, a managed press pool. I map the fortress for the third time and feel, for the first time in two years of planning, nothing — not dread, not hunger. Paperwork.

> **Turn:** On footage from two previous rallies I confirm Brandt’s gift: detail out, door shut, sixty seconds. He told me the truth while I was inside him. I say that sentence to the mirror to see if it sounds like a joke. It doesn’t.

**Scene 3 — The Mirror.** A long look. Whatever was there in Chapter 1 — Hannah’s sister, Mara’s mentor — is gone. The replacement is competent and very tired and has lost weight.

> **Turn:** I check the replacement for flaws the way I’d check a body. I find them. I don’t look away.

> **Snapper:** The unheard voicemail’s notification, which I turn face-down. The fridge, which I open and close.
