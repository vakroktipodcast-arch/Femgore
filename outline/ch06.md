# Chapter 6 — The Lesson (Execution One)

- **Act:** Act I — The Prerequisite (Chapters 1–9)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: the campaign’s certainty that nothing is coming; from Chapter 8, Brandt. Appetite: present and unnamed.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch06.md`

**Scene 1 — The Sentencing.** I explain the curriculum. Diane realises who I am — a small sound at the back of the throat. She apologises. Not for what she did; for not having protected me from it.

> **Turn:** It is the worst thing she could have said. I watch her not know why, and for one line I am not amused by anything.

**Scene 2 — The Method.** On the page. The shredded NDAs going soft in her mouth; the specific noise of industrial tape; the black over the eyes; what a body does in the dark when it cannot breathe, in the order it does it, described by a woman who has seen the inside of what she is now watching the outside of. Iris is calm, funny, exact, and then she stops being funny and does not notice that she has.

> **Turn:** Before the tape goes on, Diane’s stutter surfaces once in my hearing — a hesitation before a word I never hear. I will hear it for the rest of the book.

**Scene 3 — The Mark.** The page pinned. GATEKEEPER circled in red. Evaluation: Confused process for protection. The shredded NDAs left where they are.

> **Turn:** I want the first investigator to read them. I want, I notice, a lot of things right now, and none of them are to leave.

> **Snapper:** I leave the way I came in. I am ravenous.

> *Editorial note:* First kill: on the page, enjoyed, with one crack. The enjoyment is the horror; the crack is the seed for Chapter 18.
