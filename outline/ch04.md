# Chapter 4 — The Stalking

- **Act:** Act I — The Prerequisite (Chapters 1–9)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: the campaign’s certainty that nothing is coming; from Chapter 8, Brandt. Appetite: present and unnamed.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch04.md`

**Scene 1 — The Reservoir.** Two Sundays at distance. The bench, eleven minutes of reading, the turn for home. I describe Diane’s body the way I describe bodies: build, gait, where the fat sits, what the liver probably looks like. Affection and appraisal in the same paragraph.

> **Turn:** Second Sunday she isn’t alone — Olivia, home from college, walks beside her. I abort. The target has a daughter in frame now, not in a file.

**Scene 2 — The Campaign’s Arrogance.** HQ, seen through my planted access: Holt briefed on polls, laughing at a donor; Diane managing him. The system’s self-regard.

> **Turn:** Holt asks Diane, lightly, whether ‘the old files’ are closed. She says yes. I have just watched two people discuss me in five seconds, and I have to put the laptop down.

**Scene 3 — The Place.** The abandoned pump station near the trail. Preparations. The soundproofing test — one clap inside, one outside, one more inside.

> **Turn:** Third clap, inside, door shut: nothing reaches the trail. I stand in the dark until my eyes adjust and find I don’t mind it. I find I like it.

> **Snapper:** I eat dinner in the car outside the pump station, all of it, and lick the container.
