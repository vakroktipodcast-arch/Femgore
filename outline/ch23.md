# Chapter 23 — The Climax Begins

- **Act:** Act III — The Final Exam (Chapters 19–27)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Reyes, closing on paper; the knowledge from 18 in every room. Appetite: starved.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch23.md`

**Scene 1 — The Naming.** I tell him my name. He searches, visibly, for the file. Nothing. He apologises for his memory and offers his hand. That I have restructured my life around a man who cannot place me is unbearable. Write it without commentary — the one place in the book the voice goes flat.

> **Turn:** He uses my first name back to me, warmly, as if he has just learned it. He has.

**Scene 2 — The Reminder.** I tell him exactly what he did. He listens. He frowns. With what he believes is gentle correction: ‘I think you may be thinking of someone else.’ Whether he is lying the reader is never permitted to know. Stay in Iris.

> **Turn:** He glances at the door — not for rescue. For his minute. He is worried about the time.

**Scene 3 — The Refusal.** He declines to be the antagonist of the story I’ve spent two years writing. A man who has done many things and remembers almost none of them as wrong. The syllabus does not anticipate this.

> **Turn:** I don’t yet know what I’m going to do, and for the first time in the book you are ahead of me, and I know it.

> **Snapper:** Thirty seconds on a clock only I am counting. No appetite. I check for it the way you check a pocket.
