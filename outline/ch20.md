# Chapter 20 — The Desperate Plan

- **Act:** Act III — The Final Exam (Chapters 19–27)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Reyes, closing on paper; the knowledge from 18 in every room. Appetite: starved.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch20.md`

**Scene 1 — Reconnaissance.** Everything on the venue. The vulnerability is not the Secret Service; it is the bureaucracy. The campaign controls back-of-house press and volunteer credentials, printed on its own badge printer; the credential audit was Compliance’s job, and Compliance has been dead two months. Brandt’s access — mine now — holds the template. The venue map: a service stair beside the green-room corridor, a housekeeping closet one floor down, a loading dock with a vendor gate.

> **Turn:** The green room is a converted meeting suite with a service door the Service has sealed but the hotel floor plan still shows. The seal is a sticker.

**Scene 2 — The Letter.** I write to Hannah. I don’t send it. I leave it on the desk where it will be found if I don’t come home. I describe what I don’t say in it.

> **Turn:** I sign it with the name Hannah uses for me, which no one has used in two years, and have to put the pen down.

**Scene 3 — Staging.** I pre-stage the catering jacket in the housekeeping closet a day early, on a volunteer badge. I eat nothing before. ‘You don’t eat before.’ The ritual from Chapter 15, hollowed.

> **Turn:** In the mirror this time I don’t look like someone going on a date. I look like someone going to work. I preferred the date.

> **Snapper:** The letter squared to the desk edge, the drawer beneath it locked, the third page in my jacket where my hand keeps finding it.

> *Editorial note:* The escape in 26 is only as credible as this chapter. Every element it uses — stair, closet, dock, badge, sticker, the minute — is seen here first. Reyes’s subpoena beat is now her interlude.
