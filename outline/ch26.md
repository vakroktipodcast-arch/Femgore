# Chapter 26 — The Escape

- **Act:** Act III — The Final Exam (Chapters 19–27)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Reyes, closing on paper; the knowledge from 18 in every room. Appetite: starved.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch26.md`

**Scene 1 — Egress.** Service door, stair, the housekeeping closet one floor down, the catering jacket staged in Chapter 20. Two floors below before the knock becomes a breach. Competent, exhausted, muscle memory.

> **Turn:** The lockdown order over a hotel radio as I pass the loading-dock office. I am on the wrong side of it by eleven seconds. I keep walking.

**Scene 2 — The Corridor.** A woman going the other way in the service corridor — mid-forties, flat shoes, the walk of someone who has worn a sidearm for twenty years. Half a second. She looks at my hands. I look at hers.

> **Turn:** She doesn’t stop. I know exactly who she is. I tell you her name before her interlude does.

**Scene 3 — Out.** The vendor gate. The street. The city unchanged. I walk home. Two hours. No cab.

> **Turn:** Halfway home I realise I’ve been holding the folded page through the jacket the whole way, the way I held my left hand in the lift in Chapter 2. I am not hungry. I note that I would once have called that a finding.

> **Snapper:** The vendor gate closing behind me, unhurried, on a timer.
