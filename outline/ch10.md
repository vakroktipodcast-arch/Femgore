# Chapter 10 — Escalation of Trouble

- **Act:** Act II — The Practicum (Chapters 10–18)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Brandt, until Chapter 17; then her own knowledge. Appetite: named, indulged, and finally seen.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch10.md`

**Scene 1 — The Bait.** Brandt leaks a fabricated whistleblower profile to draw out anyone watching and seed a decoy Butcher. I watch it land and admire the craft.

> **Turn:** The profile has a real face. A woman who looks like me. Her name is Tessa Quinn.

**Scene 2 — Collateral.** Tessa is doxxed and harassed inside forty-eight hours. I watch. Brandt watches. Neither of us lifts a finger, for slightly different reasons, and I tell you mine honestly: she is not on the syllabus.

> **Turn:** A voicemail reaches the ME’s office — Tessa, asking for ‘the pathologist in the news’. I listen once. I delete it. I eat lunch.

**Scene 3 — Detachment.** Detachment is the syllabus’s first principle. I maintain it, mostly. The crack is small enough that I can refuse to look at it — and I tell the reader I am refusing, which is the voice doing what the plot can’t yet.

> **Turn:** I write Tessa’s name in the margin and draw a line through it. The line is the crack.

> **Snapper:** The deleted voicemail’s timestamp, which I find I have memorised.
