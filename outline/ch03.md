# Chapter 3 — The Re-engineering

- **Act:** Act I — The Prerequisite (Chapters 1–9)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: the campaign’s certainty that nothing is coming; from Chapter 8, Brandt. Appetite: present and unnamed.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch03.md`

**Scene 1 — The Margins.** Home. Red ink in the syllabus’s margins; the plan revises itself on the page and the reader watches me think. I cook while I think. I eat standing up.

> **Turn:** The dossier has a gap. Diane has no unsecured hour in it.

**Scene 2 — The Pattern.** The gap closes: a fitness app profile Diane never set private, three years of Sunday reservoir routes. The intersection point is set.

> **Turn:** I feel nothing I can name and write that down. Then I write down what I ate, in the same hand, and don’t ask why.

**Scene 3 — The Rehearsal.** I rehearse the capture on the kitchen floor with a rolled rug. The absurdity is on the page — Iris knows how she looks. So is the competence.

> **Turn:** I get the rug’s ‘airway’ closed in under four seconds on the third attempt and find I’m breathing hard and happy.

> **Snapper:** The red marker, capped, laid parallel to the page’s edge. The rug back where it lives.
