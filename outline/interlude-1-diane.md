# Interlude 1 — Diane

- **Placement:** after Chapter 3
- **POV:** Diane, close third, past tense
- **Target:** 600–900 words, one Turn
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/interlude-1-diane.md`

Diane at home. She calls Olivia at Wellesley; they talk about a roommate dispute. Diane is, in this moment, a tired mother. The reader is given exactly what Iris will end.

***Turn:*** *Diane mentions the Sunday walk she takes ‘to not be reachable for one hour of my life’. The reader has the gap before Iris does — and knows Iris has already found it.*
