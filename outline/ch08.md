# Chapter 8 — The War Room Reacts

- **Act:** Act I — The Prerequisite (Chapters 1–9)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: the campaign’s certainty that nothing is coming; from Chapter 8, Brandt. Appetite: present and unnamed.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch08.md`

**Scene 1 — The Autopsy.** Mara performs Diane Pell with me observing as senior reviewer. I watch my own work from the other side of the table and narrate it as a critic: what was elegant, what was rushed, where the tape left adhesive I should have anticipated. Mara notes, quietly, an unusual level of anatomical expertise. I agree, in the tone I’d use for weather.

> **Turn:** Mara hesitates over the word ‘practised’ and writes it. It is in the file now. I am proud of her, and I am in the file.

**Scene 2 — The Second Page.** Home. Lesson Two read for an hour. Brandt’s dossier open beside it: the vinyl, the Tuesday piano, the divorce he does not talk about. Iris is interested in him in a way she was not interested in Diane, and tells the reader so, and does not examine it.

> **Turn:** On his file, in my handwriting from two years ago, a note I don’t remember writing: ‘the only one who’ll understand it.’

**Scene 3 — The Feed.** My planted access to the War Room. Panic, containment, the syllabus page photographed before anyone knows what it is. Brandt summoned.

> **Turn:** I watch him look at the photograph for a long time and go very still. I don’t know why. The reader will, after the interlude.

> **Snapper:** I rewind the feed and watch him go still again. Then once more.
