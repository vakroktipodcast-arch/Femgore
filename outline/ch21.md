# Chapter 21 — The Infiltration

- **Act:** Act III — The Final Exam (Chapters 19–27)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Reyes, closing on paper; the knowledge from 18 in every room. Appetite: starved.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch21.md`

**Scene 1 — Entry.** In as a credentialed volunteer press assistant. Heist pacing: tight, procedural, one near-failure recovered through a pathologist’s calm. Iris narrates the crowd as bodies — pulses, sweat, who is on what medication — out of habit, and the habit is the only pleasure left.

> **Turn:** A credential check that didn’t exist at the fundraiser. I pass because the checker is a campaign volunteer, nineteen, with a laminated list. The hole is exactly where I said.

**Scene 2 — The Map.** Back corridors I’ve memorised. The Service is competent, not omniscient; I count their handoff rhythm.

> **Turn:** The rhythm is tighter than the footage. The minute alone has become forty seconds. My plan was built for sixty.

**Scene 3 — The Threshold.** The green-room corridor as Holt is walked between events. The detail stops at the door. He goes in alone. The door closes.

> **Turn:** In the gap before it shuts: a framed photograph of a Marine on the dressing table, already turned to face the chair.

> **Snapper:** I count to five and put my hand on the sticker. My hand is steady. I wish, briefly, that it weren’t.
