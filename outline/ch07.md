# Chapter 7 — The Cleanup

- **Act:** Act I — The Prerequisite (Chapters 1–9)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: the campaign’s certainty that nothing is coming; from Chapter 8, Brandt. Appetite: present and unnamed.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch07.md`

**Scene 1 — The Egress.** Procedural exit; what I remove, what I leave, why the NDA fragments stay — control numbers point at campaign counsel. I leave more than I realise: the numbers lead to a list, and I am on it.

> **Turn:** A cyclist’s bell on the trail as I reach the road. I was not alone out here as long as I believed.

**Scene 2 — The Meal.** Home. Forty minutes in the shower. No tears. Then I cook — properly, two courses — and eat all of it at the counter at 3 a.m., and tell the reader about adrenaline and glycogen and cortisol, and the reader does not believe me, and neither, quite, do I.

> **Turn:** My left hand, beside the plate, is perfectly still. I note it the way I’d note a finding.

**Scene 3 — The Morning.** Mara texts about an unexpected case. I know whose body it is. Second text: the Chief has given it to Mara, not me — ‘a face that’s never been near politics’. I will review.

> **Turn:** I wasn’t asked. I will be in the room anyway, standing where Mara stood last week.

> **Snapper:** I reply: On my way. I am already dressed. I have been dressed for an hour.

> *Editorial note:* The meal replaces v3’s tea as the aftermath ritual. Tea survives as the smaller daily gesture; the meal is the one that follows a kill.
