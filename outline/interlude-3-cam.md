# Interlude 3 — Cam

- **Placement:** after Chapter 13
- **POV:** Cam, close third, past tense
- **Target:** 600–900 words, one Turn
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/interlude-3-cam.md`

Brandt comes home, sees the broken record, sits on the bed holding it for a long time. A man who has arranged consequences for others for twenty years, arranging them for himself. He finds the page. Over the following days he becomes erratic, accuses a junior staffer of leaking, drinks before noon for the first time in nine years, and tells Holt there is ‘a problem from the old files’. Holt says, ‘I thought Diane closed those,’ and changes the subject, and quietly reassigns Brandt’s access. Brandt has watched Holt do this to other men for twenty years. He pours a drink and puts on Mingus and waits for a woman to come to a soundproofed room.

***Turn:*** *He understands she could have killed him in his sleep and chose to warn him instead. She wants him to understand first. He finds, to his surprise, that he wants to.*
