# Chapter 25 — The Final Lesson (Execution Three)

- **Act:** Act III — The Final Exam (Chapters 19–27)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Reyes, closing on paper; the knowledge from 18 in every room. Appetite: starved.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch25.md`

**Scene 1 — The Kill.** Brief. Brutal. Unceremonious. Three paragraphs. No procedure, no anatomy, no Latin, no pleasure. Rage without appetite — the first act of violence in the book that gives me nothing, and I notice the nothing while I’m doing it, and that is the horror of the scene. He was supposed to live.

> **Turn:** It is over before the minute is. I check for the taste. There isn’t one.

**Scene 2 — The Aftermath.** He is on the floor. The page is on the table. I pick it up and try to pin it. My hands won’t. I fold it once and put it in my inside pocket.

> **Turn:** The folded page is the first thing in two years I have not been able to make myself do, and I didn’t want to anyway.

**Scene 3 — The Door.** The corridor filling with voices. The minute becomes two. A knock. His name.

> **Turn:** The detail doesn’t open the door. Standing instruction: not to be disturbed. The system protects him for one more minute, and I use it.

> **Snapper:** The Marine’s photograph face-down on the carpet where it fell. I don’t turn it over. I think about it.

> *Editorial note:* Chapter 17 and Chapter 25 are the two poles of the book: ecstatic, lush, slow versus numb, plain, fast. Every sentence-level choice in 25 is the inverse of 17.
