# Chapter 12 — The Close Call

- **Act:** Act II — The Practicum (Chapters 10–18)
- **Act pressure:** Pressure: Brandt, until Chapter 17; then her own knowledge. Appetite: named, indulged, and finally seen.
- **POV:** Iris, first person, past tense
- **Target:** ~3,150 words, three scenes
- **Manuscript file:** `manuscript/ch12.md`

**Scene 1 — The Ambush.** Egress. Two contractors, not police, no constraints — but a brief: take her, don’t kill her. Brandt needs to know what I have.

> **Turn:** One of them says my name. Not Dr. Vale. Iris.

**Scene 2 — The Violence.** Fast, ugly, on the page, and — this is the chapter’s job — exhilarating. I win and take a knife to the left flank, and I narrate which muscles, and I narrate that it felt, the whole time, like the morgue with the lights on. The reader should be frightened by how much fun she is having.

> **Turn:** One contractor is dead on the warehouse floor. Not planned. No page. He is the first thing in the book that isn’t on the syllabus — and the first thing I did for no reason but that I could.

**Scene 3 — Self-Repair.** Home. I stitch the wound myself, anatomically exact. Mara’s voice in my head from a lecture we gave together on wound geometry. Afterwards I go out, find a stranger, and come home at four. Not narrated at length. The point is the morning: I go to work.

> **Turn:** I realise I haven’t graded the dead man and don’t know how to, and that I slept better than I have in years.

> **Snapper:** The needle on the sink edge, parallel to the tap, and a second toothbrush I throw away.

> *Editorial note:* The appetite is now sexual as well as alimentary. Keep it brief, unsentimental, and never the point of the scene.
