# The Test

When in doubt about a sentence, a scene, or a structural choice, run it against one question:

*Does this make the reader more or less able to look away from Iris?*

If it makes her easier to dismiss — as monster, as fantasy, as femme fatale, as a case study — cut it. If it makes her harder to dismiss, even when the reader wants to, even when the reader is enjoying it and wishes they weren’t, keep it. The novel is the second of those two experiences, sustained for eighty-five thousand words.
