# Point of View & Tense

- First person, past tense, Iris throughout. She addresses the reader directly — ‘you’ — sparingly, and always to implicate: to make the reader a student, a witness, or an accessory. Never to explain.
- Five close-third interludes, headed with the character’s name, set between chapters: Diane (after 3), Cam (after 8, after 13), Mara (none — Mara is seen only through Iris), Reyes (after 20, after 26). Never Holt. Each interlude 600–900 words, past tense, one Turn.
- Never head-hop. Interludes never share a scene break with Iris. Iris never knows what an interlude contains unless she finds it out on the page.
- The syllabus pages at chapter heads exist outside tense. They are documents. In v4 they also sound like her, which is the point.
