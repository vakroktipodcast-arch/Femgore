# Chapter Architecture — The Dent Checklist

Before a chapter is drafted, it passes all eight:

- Three scenes, ~3,150 words. Chapter 17 may run long; nothing else may.
- Scene 1 puts Iris into the chapter’s trouble or task inside its first page.
- Every scene ends on its Turn. The Turn lines in Part Three are the minimum.
- The chapter’s named pressure is felt in every scene.
- Iris gets out by her own competence. No luck, no rescue, no unlocked doors she didn’t unlock.
- The appetite is present — fed, denied, or noticed — at least once. If a chapter has no body in it, it is not in this book.
- At least one joke per page in Acts I–II; the ratio falls in Act III on purpose.
- The chapter closes on its Snapper — an image or an admission, never a cliffhanger. First and last sentences are load-bearing.
