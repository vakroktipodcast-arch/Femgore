# Sentence-Level Texture

- Colour: named precisely. Slate, oxblood, ash, sodium, bone, charcoal, lichen, liver. Never red, grey, brown unmodified.
- Light: by source. Sodium, fluorescent, halogen, dawn, the blue of a phone at 2 a.m., the one lamp in Brandt’s suite.
- Bodies: anatomically correct in Iris’s narration, always. Layman’s vocabulary in the interludes. A lung is a lung to Iris; the lung to Mara; something Diane is no longer using to Cam.
- Taste: a new sense for v4. Everything she puts in her mouth is described. So is the metal taste of the original injury when it surfaces.
