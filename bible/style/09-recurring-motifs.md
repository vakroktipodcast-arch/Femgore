# Recurring Motifs

- The meal after. Chapters 7, 12, 17 (planned, not taken), 27.
- Hands. See section 5.
- Tape. Black vinyl; surgical tape in the morgue; the tape Hannah uses on a child’s birthday present; the sticker on the service door.
- Voicemail. Three unreturned: the woman three years ago, Tessa Quinn, Hannah in 19. Iris stops being able to listen.
- Vinyl. Cam’s. Iris has never owned a record (true up to the night of 27; the Ch 17 anchor line stands). In 27 there is one in the flat, unplayed, against the writing desk. Do not explain it.
- Mirrors. 15 (the date), 19 (the body), 20 (work). The reader supplies 27.
- The Marine’s photograph. Face-up 21–24; face-down 25.
- The grade. Iris grades everyone. The last grade in the book is Mara’s, in 27: full marks, for being afraid of her.
