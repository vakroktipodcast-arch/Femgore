# The Banned Phrase List

Unconscious defaults that will sneak in under daily word-count pressure. Catch them in revision.

- ice in her veins
- trained killer / professional killer
- calculating eyes
- femme fatale (anywhere, including your own notes)
- dance, danced, dancing as a verb for combat
- perfect as a violence descriptor
- breathed as a dialogue tag
- demon, monster, beast — for Iris, the targets, or the violence. The genre’s whole argument is that she is a woman.
- beautiful — anywhere except Chapter 17, where it is required once.
- lucky, luckily, fortunately — Dent’s hero rule has been broken nearby
- I shouldn’t have enjoyed it / I’m not proud of — Iris never apologises for appetite. She reports it. The reader does the judging, or doesn’t.
- empty / hollow / numb as a description of her state. Show the absence of appetite through the body (the fridge, the plate, the pocket she checks); never name it.
