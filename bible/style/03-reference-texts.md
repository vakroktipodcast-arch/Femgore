# Reference Texts

Read these when you lose the voice. Read 17 of them before you draft Chapter 17.

- Chelsea G. Summers — A Certain Hunger. The gourmand killer’s first person; appetite as voice; erudition as seduction.
- Eliza Clark — Boy Parts. Unreliable, funny, cruel, sexual; a woman who hurts people and makes you complicit.
- Mona Awad — Bunny; Rouge. Literary surface over grotesque material; the pleasure of excess.
- Monika Kim — The Eyes Are the Best Part. Consumption as rage; a young woman’s appetite made literal.
- Elle Nash — Deliver Me. The body as the whole subject; ecstatic violence.
- Ottessa Moshfegh — Eileen; My Year of Rest and Relaxation. Disgust rendered with affection; the body as a bad roommate.
- Gillian Flynn — Sharp Objects. Controlled female rage; the unreliable interior. Kept from v3.
- Patricia Highsmith — The Talented Mr. Ripley. Competence without commentary; the reader rooting for the wrong person. Kept from v3.
Retired from v3: French, Harper, Winslow, Abbott. They taught the structure; they will teach the wrong restraint.
