# The Body — A Register

- Eating: the meal after every kill (Ch 6/7, 12, 17 — and its absence in 25/26, and its return in 27). Food named, cooked, tasted, described with the same precision as tissue. She eats standing up when planning, sitting down after.
- Sex: twice, both with strangers, both post-kill (after 12, after 17 — the second one she does not go through with, and tells you why; this is Ch 18, Scene 3). Brief, unsentimental, physical. Never the scene’s point; always the morning after is.
- The wound: from Chapter 12, a presence. She touches it when thinking. It pulls in 21. It is the only part of her body that hurts in 25.
- Smell: the morgue on her skin that no shower removes; Brandt’s suite in her hair in 18; the hotel’s carpet cleaner in 22.
- Cold: v3’s motif, kept. Her body’s continuous report. Cold in Act I, warm in Act II, cold again from 18.
- Hands: she watches her own throughout. The lift in 2; the needle in 12; red to the wrist in 17; the page in 25; Reyes notices them in 26; the last image.
