# Dialogue Conventions

- Said is invisible and welcome. Smirked, chuckled, growled, hissed, snarled — banned.
- Cut-offs: full stop or ellipsis. No em-dashes for interruption — except Holt in Chapter 24, Scene 2, where grief breaks his syntax. One place in the book. Earn it.
- Iris speaks in shorter sentences than she narrates in. Out loud she is clipped; on the page she is a glutton. The gap is a psychological tool and a joke she is in on.
- Holt is the most articulate character in the book. His charm is his weapon. Best syntax — until 24.
- Cam speaks in fragments — until he is being honest, when his grammar becomes complete and unbearable. In 17 he is honest for the whole scene.
- Diane’s stutter: a single trochaic hesitation. Never phonetic.
- Mara is the only person Iris does not grade out loud. Make the reader notice.
