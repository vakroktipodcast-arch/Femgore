# The Genre Contract — What a Femgore Reader Is Buying

Femgore is women who stalk, slash, dismember and devour, written at the seam between literary fiction and horror, with the violence on the page and the woman enjoying it. The reader is promised:

- A voice. First person, intimate, transgressive, funny. The narrator is the attraction; the plot is what she is doing while you listen to her.
- Appetite. Pleasure in the body — her own and other people’s — and in the act. Disgust and relish in the same sentence.
- Gore that is rendered, not implied. Specific, sensory, competent. The reader wants to see the thing done and to see her see it.
- The ‘good for her’ arc, complicated. Revenge against a system or a man, delivered — and then the book asks what it cost, or what it was really about, without taking the revenge back.
- Humour. Dry, dark, frequently about bodies. The genre is funnier than its covers suggest.
- Literary surface. Sentences that could sit in a literary novel, deployed on material that could not.
Every one of these is a debt this book now pays. The Dent structure underneath is what keeps the velocity while the voice does the work.
