# The Appetite Arc — New in v4

This is the spine the genre reader is actually following, running under the three-act structure. Track it in every chapter:

- Act I: Appetite as competence. She enjoys the morgue and does not call it enjoyment. The first kill surprises her with how good it feels; she files the feeling under ‘the grade’. She eats enormously afterwards and tells the reader it is the adrenaline.
- Act II: Appetite as identity. She stops pretending. The planning is foreplay, the fight in Chapter 12 is a pleasure she describes as such, the midpoint gifts to Brandt are flirtation, and Chapter 17 is consummation. The reader is seduced along with her.
- Chapter 18: Appetite seen. She hears the voicemail and understands what she was feeling while Brandt told her about it. She does not decide the appetite is wrong. She decides to keep it and continue, knowing. That is the wound.
- Act III: Appetite starved. She plans Holt without pleasure. She enters the suite hungry for the one meal the whole curriculum was for, and he does not recognise her, and when she kills him there is nothing — no taste, no heat. Chapter 27: she sits in front of food and cannot eat it. Then she can. Then she looks at her hands.
