# The Three Lessons

#### Lesson One — The Gatekeeper (The True Believer)

**Target:** Diane Pell, Campaign Chief of Staff and Compliance Director, 52. Weaponised an NDA against Iris for what she still privately calls the greater good. Single mother, daughter at Wellesley, Sunday calls to her own mother, a Sunday walk to be unreachable. Competent, decent in every register that does not touch the campaign. Dangerous because she is not a monster.

**Execution:** Asphyxiation under sensory deprivation. Zip-ties, industrial tape, the mouth packed with shredded NDAs. On the page: the texture of the paper going soft, the sounds Diane makes and then does not, Iris’s running commentary — clinical, amused, and then, for one line she does not expect, not amused. The horror is in how much Iris enjoys the quiet.

**What she leaves:** the shredded NDAs. Fragments carry the campaign’s legal control numbers. Iris leaves them to point investigators at the campaign. She does not think through where the numbers lead.

#### Lesson Two — The Architect (The Self-Aware Corruption)

**Target:** Carmichael ‘Cam’ Brandt, lead fixer, 48. Planted blind items that ended her credibility when the NDA was not enough. Divorced, jazz vinyl, Tuesday piano at a private club. The most aware of his own corruption; stopped lying to himself long ago. The kill she will never recover from — and, in v4, the one she enjoys most.

**Execution:** Deconstruction in a soundproofed editing suite. The book’s set piece: the longest scene, the most explicit, the most sensory, the funniest, the most tender. An autopsy performed on a man who is awake and talking and interesting, by a woman who is having the best night of her life. The reader should want to look away and be unable to, because she is so clearly happy.

**What he gives her:** the voicemail she never returned, and Holt’s habit — one minute alone before every speech, with his brother’s photograph, detail outside by standing instruction. He gives her both while she is elbow-deep in him. She leaves the suite elated. The elation lasts as far as her car.

#### Lesson Three — The Apex (The Untouchable Idol)

**Target:** Senator Holt Marchetti, the Candidate, 54. The original abuser. Magnetic, kind in small rooms, a dead Marine brother driving a sincere veterans’ policy, memorises staffers’ children’s names. Not a sociopath. Worse: loves humanity in the abstract and uses individuals as means without registering it.

**Prescribed execution:** Exposure, not death. Immobilised, conscious, pinned, left alive to be found with eleven names already in an FBI agent’s hands. The syllabus’s final lesson is that he will live and be seen.

**What actually happens:** he does not recognise her. He grieves Diane in front of her. She kills him outside the curriculum — briefly, without ceremony, and without pleasure. It is the only act of violence in the book she takes nothing from. The page stays in her pocket.

> *Editorial note:* Lesson Three as exposure (new in v3) stays. In the femgore frame it does more: the one kill she does not enjoy is the one the syllabus never asked for. Appetite and curriculum come apart in the same scene.
