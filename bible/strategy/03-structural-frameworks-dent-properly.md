# Structural Frameworks — Dent, Properly

Unchanged from v3 in principle, retuned for the shorter chapter. Dent’s master plot divides a story into quarters that each end on a turn, with a snapper at the close. Applied here:

- Three scenes of roughly 1,000–1,100 words. Every scene ends on a Turn — reversal, menace, or information that changes the situation.
- Scene 1 grabs. Scene 2 shovels grief. Scene 3 reverses, and closes on the Snapper.
- The Snapper is an image or a line Iris would notice — never a cliffhanger. In first person the snapper is often the last thing she admits.
- Menace in every quarter: the campaign, Brandt, Reyes, and from Chapter 18 her own knowledge. In v4 add a fifth pressure that runs the whole book — her appetite, which the reader should feel as a danger to her before she does.
- Hero rule: Iris gets out of everything by her own competence. No luck, no rescue.
- Interludes do not count toward the chapter’s three scenes and carry their own single Turn.
