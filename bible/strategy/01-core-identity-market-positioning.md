# Core Identity & Market Positioning

**Title:** The Butcher’s Syllabus. (Reserve: A Curriculum for Carnage.)

**Subtitle / shelf line:** A Novel. (Femgore is a BookTok and review-page label, not an Amazon category. It goes in the blurb, the keywords and the cover language, never in a subtitle.)

**KDP categories:** Horror > Psychological; Thrillers & Suspense > Psychological; Literary Fiction > Women’s. Keywords lead with the genre label and its comps.

**Length:** 85,000 words. 27 chapters, ~3,150 each, plus five short interludes.

**Premise:** A forensic pathologist who has spent 1,400 autopsies learning what the human body is made of decides to teach three people what it is for. She has a curriculum. She has a red marker. She has, it turns out, an appetite. She does not call it murder. She calls it grading — and she is very good at it, and she likes it, and the book is about the exact moment she finds out what the liking was covering.
