# Beat sheet: interlude-2-cam

- **Unit:** Interlude 2, Cam (after Chapter 8, before Chapter 9)
- **POV:** Carmichael "Cam" Brandt, close third, past tense. Heading in manuscript: `# Interlude — Cam`.
- **Target words:** 600–900 (aim ~800). One scene, one Turn.
- **Act pressure:** Act I, The Prerequisite. The campaign is certain nothing is coming. This interlude is the moment that certainty breaks inside one man and nobody else. From here on Brandt is the pressure (ch08 outline: "from Chapter 8, Brandt").
- **Iris's appetite at entry:** Act I, appetite as competence, present and unnamed. She has killed once and eaten enormously (ch06–07). In ch08 she is interested in Brandt the way she was not interested in Diane, and she has watched him go still without knowing why.
- **Iris's appetite at exit:** Unchanged. She is off the page and the interlude cannot touch her appetite. What changes is the reader, who now knows why Brandt went still and knows that the "only one who'll understand it" (ch08 S2 Turn) has already started to. Dramatic irony goes in; no appetite from Iris leaks into Cam's head.
- **Register reminder (interlude-drafter rules):** plainer and cooler than Iris. Layman's words for bodies ("something Diane was no longer using"). No jokes in Iris's style, no direct address, no appetite. Cam thinks in fragments and speaks in fragments. His grammar only becomes complete when he is honest with himself, which happens once, at the Turn.

## Conflicts

None. The outline agrees with the ledger: shredded NDAs in the mouth, fragments carrying campaign legal control numbers, Brandt wears charcoal. Constraints the beats keep to:
- Brandt must **not** know Iris's identity here. The ledger fixes that for Ch 9. He recognises the *paper*, not the person.
- Brandt must **not** pull the signatory list here. The paperwork thread (strategy/09) puts that in Ch 9. He can know the list exists; he does not open it.
- Brandt must **not** detect Iris's planted feed. Ch 9 S2 depends on her still watching him. Nothing in his caution can involve cameras or audio in the room. His caution is aimed at people and phones.
- His stillness must line up with ch08 S3 (Iris watching on the feed) so the reader can lay the two scenes over each other.

## Scene 1: The War Room, Sunday night

1. Brandt comes into the War Room last, summoned, at 11:52 p.m. He is in the charcoal suit with no tie, which is the only sign he was called from home. The room is already loud: a dozen staff standing, nobody sitting, every face lit blue from below by a phone.
2. He takes a coffee from the urn by the door without asking how long it has been there. It is tepid. He drinks it anyway because the cup gives his hand something to do while everyone tells him things at once. Comms wants a statement by 6 a.m. Counsel wants nobody to say anything. Someone says *activist*. Someone says *lunatic*.
3. The photograph is up on the wall screen and in the staff channel: Diane on the concrete floor of the pump station, black tape across her eyes, a printed page pinned to her chest, one word circled in red. Everyone in the room is reading the page. Brandt says, "Phone. Not the wall." He opens it on his own screen.
4. He reads the page once. Gatekeeper. *Confused process for protection.* In fragments he thinks it is a fair mark, then puts that thought face-down. Then he looks at what nobody else is looking at, which is her mouth: packed full, the paper gone grey and soft where it was wet.
5. He enlarges it with two fingers. A serif face. A thin footer rule. Half a control number. He knows the typeface because he chose it, four years ago, for the template he had Diane use: Century Schoolbook, "because it looks like a judge wrote it." His thumb stops on the glass and stays there. **This is the stillness Iris watches in ch08 S3: he does not move for a long time, and the coffee goes on cooling in his other hand.**
6. "Cam?" The deputy comms director (unnamed) wants to know if it's political. Brandt says, "Crank. Probably." Then: "Who's in the channel." Fourteen people. He asks who posted it. Theo Akers, the junior digital staffer, says a contact at the county sheriff's office sent it to campaign security, who sent it to him, who thought everyone should see it.
7. Brandt's thumb saves the image to his own phone before it deletes it from the channel. The order is not quite a decision. Then he deletes it as channel admin and tells the room, "Off your phones. All of it. Nobody has seen this." Theo starts to say something about the record. Brandt looks at him until he stops.
8. Campaign counsel, standing at the glass with the city behind him, asks whether the paper in her mouth is "anything." Brandt watches him ask without looking at it. "Paper," Brandt says. He thinks about how many things Diane's name is on, some of them his, and does not say that either.
9. **[TURN]** He does not tell anyone what he has recognised. Not counsel, who drafted the agreements and does not know what they look like torn up. Not comms. Not the Senator, who will be told in the morning that it is a tragedy, with a statement attached. His grammar comes together for the first time in the scene: he understands that he is already working alone, and that he started the moment his thumb stopped. It feels less like a decision than a habit. Twenty years of arranging what other people know, now turned on the room he is standing in.
10. He finishes the coffee, cold now, and drops the cup in the bin by the urn. Then the Snapper.

- **Sensory lead:** Taste first: tepid urn coffee and the waxed lining of the paper cup. Then sound: a dozen people talking over the HVAC hum, the small separate *tock* of a phone set down on the conference table. Texture: the phone glass under his thumb, warm from his hand. Temperature: the window glass cold where counsel leans on it, the coffee falling to room temperature over the scene. The smell is the hotel's: carpet, recycled air, someone's room-service fries left on a tray. Plain and unremarked. Cam does not smell things the way Iris does.
- **Dry observations (Cam's register, not Iris's; no jokes in her style):**
  - Comms wants a statement by six. Counsel wants silence by six. Cam thinks, in fragments, that they will both get what they want, in that order.
  - "Activist," someone said, about a woman who had circled one word in red and spelled it correctly.
- **The body:** His hands carry the scene. One holds a cup going cold. The other holds a thumb that stops on the glass and then acts before he does (save, then delete). He does not sit. He does not loosen the collar he isn't wearing a tie with. Diane's body is seen only in the photograph and only in layman's terms: her mouth "full," the tape "across her eyes," the mouth "something she was no longer using."
- **Motifs:**
  - **Tape:** the black vinyl across Diane's eyes, which Cam reads as record-sleeve black. That is a single note, his own vocabulary, and it seeds the vinyl motif without explaining it.
  - **The grade:** he privately judges the Evaluation a fair mark and turns the thought face-down. This is the first flicker of the kinship that ch09 S2 names from Iris's side.
  - **Vinyl:** only through the tape colour. No records in the room.
  - **Paperwork thread:** the typeface and half a control number, the first place the system's own filing points somewhere.

## Snapper

He puts the phone with the one remaining copy into his inside breast pocket, left side, where the page was pinned on her, and buttons the charcoal jacket over it.
